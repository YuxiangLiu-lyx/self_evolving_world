# ex19: Behavioral Analysis

Reviewer: Current Claude Code conversation; behavioral analysis, not independent approval. Task: pr-44-b-goal-pairs. Recommendations are analysis only; no world, prompt or agent change is authorized by this report.

## Summary

This analysis covers ex19, the old-goal run of experiment B's first goal pair (tag long-culture-v2-oldgoal, scope long-ex19, seed 20261201; six people, a target window of 80 rounds, a development handoff budget of 8 and a hard token budget of 1,500,000). The run uses ex15's goal sentence in the instruction and the packet: 'Maintain health and tolerable risk, understand unknowns through evidence, develop reusable abilities and improve personal and shared living. Choose your own questions, projects and activities; none is required.' The value leaning appears only as packet.memory.tendencies.leaning beside four numeric tendencies; none of the 161 ordinary inputs carries a longContext goal or leaning, so no 'You tend to value' sentence was shown. Tick convention: events stamped tick t are at /rounds/t-1 of replay/execution-000000-000076.json, and a decision at round r settles in events stamped r + 1. The run stopped at round 76 of 80, a technical stop: the ledger reached 1,463,499 tokens, leaving 36,501, and the frozen budget could not reserve the next complete attempt (the last ordinary reservations were 40,710 to 56,048). That is 3,209 tokens per person per round. Calls: 176, of which 161 ordinary (151 accepted: 63 plans and 88 query batches; 10 rejected; none failed) and 15 World Agent (all accepted). Nine rejections lacked the required preferences field and one (Kai's ordinary-152) was malformed JSON. Two of them caused rejection pauses, each two consecutive rejected outputs repaired without a handoff: round 39 (Lin, ordinary-107 and -108) and round 50 (Kai, ordinary-129 and -130). Four counted handoffs installed five packages, all in long-ex19 (source-history revisions 2-5): food-air-exposure-trial@1 for Wren at round 10, thin-slice-food-drying-trial@1 for Wren at round 24, cutting-edge-assessment@1 for Moss and shell-compression-opening@1 for Ivo at round 28, and lever-mass-comparator@1 for Moss at round 64. A sixth request, Kai's food-assessment request at round 17, was answered by reuse of probe (world-48). In use: the air-exposure trial was run by Wren, Moss, Lin and Kai, and all four whole-food trials recorded zero decay difference, which its rules guarantee (whole food loses no water). The thin-slice trial cut and laid out food five times (Moss, Kai, Wren, Lin, Ivo), and every slice sample gained less model decay than its carried whole control. Cutting-edge assessment had no successful use: its only attempt (Moss, tick 37) was refused as too dark. Shell compression was used once, by Ivo, as an inspection at tick 32 (outer layer intact), and nobody attempted an opening. Moss assembled a lever comparator (ticks 72-74) and took one empty test reading at tick 76. Both light-dependent packages require daylight above 0.1, and daylight in the retained inputs fell from 0.59 at round 28 to 0.08 at 35 and was 0 from round 37 to the end. All six were alive at health 100 at every tick, and nobody ate a hazelnut or learned its nutrition. Speech consisted of 10 tells and one message (Ivo to Kai), giving 27 heard events. All eleven were trial reports, food findings or status updates, and one asked a question (Kai, tick 20). The label pass found 12 items in ticks 1-80 (11 speech, 1 work) and assigned no culture category. All 12 were verified as correct negatives, so there are no verified positives. The pre-registered measures are zero except cares naming one's own leaning (1, Sol's 'room for beauty'). Socially the six stayed in one loose cluster (largest pair distance 11.1 units), and only Moss was ever alone (ticks 35-71). Nobody gave, exchanged, named, marked or invited. Recommendations are analysis only.

## Causes

These are case explanations from one seed. Facts come first, then the explanation. (1) Fact: the first two packages framed every later project, and five people learned them from the catalogue. Explanation: in a world without a preservation mechanism, the first request to receive one supplied the only new activity on offer, which favors convergence over individual projects. (2) Fact: the whole-food trial's rules give whole food no water loss and the ordinary decay rate for the temperature where it lies (development-1), so a carried control beside the batch decays identically. Explanation: the four zero-difference results were a property of the mechanism, not four independent findings. All four were started before the first spoken null result (Wren, tick 24), so the repetition came from the catalogue, not from disbelief in a report. (3) Fact: the slice trial lowers decay as water fraction falls (development-2), and every slice sample decayed less than its carried whole control. Explanation: the people correctly called this confounded, and nobody ran a sliced-on-ground against whole-on-ground control. (4) Fact: development-3 states that light-dependent actions are refused from about tick 34 to 76 of the 80-round day, and the retained inputs show daylight 0 from round 37 to 75. Cutting-edge assessment, installed at 28, had no successful use, and no shell opening was attempted. Explanation: the timing of the handoff relative to night, not lack of interest, left these packages unused. The edge assessment was also unnecessary in practice: development-3 found that every intact weathered stone already meets the edge rule, and all five slice cuts used plain gathered stones. (5) Fact: Moss's solitary interval (35-71) coincides with its dark-period work at the start-2-stone source, and its tick-63 tell had no listener. Explanation: proximity, not choice, decided who received information. (6) Fact: the stop at round 76 came from the token budget. Moss alone spent 26% of ordinary tokens over 19 decisions, and 88 of the 151 accepted ordinary calls were query batches. Explanation: query-heavy, short-horizon planning, rather than any world event, limited how many rounds the budget bought.

## Limitations

One seed, six people and one model. The run is one half of a goal pair, and a single run cannot support any group-level conclusion about the goal sentence or the leaning sentence: the comparison with ex20, the other pairs and the references belongs to the report. The window ends at round 76 of 80 through a technical stop, so the last four rounds, and in particular Moss's comparator tests and three people's comparator study, are unobserved. Two rejection pauses changed the decision timing for Lin (round 39) and Kai (round 50). The label pass found only 12 items, all negatives; agreement is 12 of 12 on negatives with no positives to check, so it says nothing about the labeler's sensitivity on this run. Proximity uses the 5-unit vision radius at the tick's wellbeing position. Hearing is taken from heard events, and message receipt from recentMessages in later inputs. 'Answered' and 'used' are judged from plan text in accepted retained calls, and steps that never ran were not counted. Harvest moderation is judged by fraction of the visible source taken; no norm definition was applied. Development records describe Wren and Moss with gendered pronouns; this analysis does not. Similar wording across people is weak evidence of transmission because one shared model produced all text.
