## Summary

This analysis covers ex21, the second old-goal run of experiment B (tag long-culture-v2-oldgoal, scope long-ex21, seed 20261202; six people on the culture v2 world). Choose your own questions, projects and activities; none is required.') and shows each person's leaning only as the packet field memory.tendencies.leaning, with no leaning sentence ('You tend to value ...') and no goal or leaning marker in longContext. The run reached round 44 of its 80-round window and is paused. It stopped for review after Moss's ordinary-137, -138 and -139, three consecutive rejected outputs (configuration rejectionRecovery: uncounted, stopAfter 3); repair refuses at that point by design and the operator did not bypass the stop. The archived phase reason is the generic pause text ('A long-development decision failed; ...'). Tick convention: events stamped tick t are /rounds/(t-1) of replay/execution-000000-000044.json, and a decision (a call) at round r settles in events stamped tick r + 1. The ledger charged 1,021,470 of the 1,500,000-token budget: 986,353 recorded tokens (ordinary 855,091 input and 52,835 output; World Agent 67,149 and 11,278) plus 35,117 reserved for World Agent call world-89 (tick 22), which failed with 'Response stream ended without response.completed' and records no usage; that is 3,869 tokens per person per round. There were 123 ordinary calls (114 accepted, of which 46 were plans and 68 query batches; 9 rejected; none failed) and 16 World Agent calls (15 accepted; world-89 failed and world-90 completed that review). All nine rejections are 'Expected the registered task plan shape', and in each the top-level preferences field is missing and nothing else: Lin ordinary-39 (round 12), Wren ordinary-69 (16), Lin ordinary-79 and -80 (20), Kai ordinary-129 (42), Ivo ordinary-133 (43) and Moss ordinary-137 to -139 (44). The technical pauses round-20 (Lin's two consecutive rejections) and round-44 (Moss's first two) were repaired without a development handoff. The round-20 pause record keeps captureStatus 'running' with browser.json, final.png and overview.png missing, because repair started before the develop process had finished its capture; the simulation state was preserved and the final capture ran normally. Four counted handoffs of the budget of 8 installed five packages, each implemented by the current conversation as a simplified but real mechanism (source-history revisions 2-5 at rounds 10, 12, 15 and 22): air-dry-food-trial@1 for Sol's berry-spoilage request (round 10, assigned to Sol); small-combustion-source@1 for Wren's heat-source request (round 12, ability operate-small-combustion-source assigned to Wren); compare-material-samples@1 for Moss's request to measure the moisture loss of a prepared root sample (round 15, assigned to Moss) together with its measuring dependency beam-mass-comparator@1, the ex19 lever comparator rebuilt by a factory under a new identity, which the declaration assigns to nobody; and targeted-food-constituent-test@1 for Lin's hazelnut request (round 22, assigned to Lin). Communication consisted of six direct messages, all trial reports or evidence questions to a person within 5 units; there were no tells, names, marks, invitations, gifts or exchanges. The seven labeled items (the six messages and Sol's comparator progress) were all verified negatives with no model positive, and all seven pre-registered measures are 0 for ticks 1-44, the whole run. The clearest pattern is convergent instrument work: after Moss's handoff, five people studied or planned the beam comparator, three mastered it, and only Sol built one. ## Causes

These are case explanations from one seed. (1) The run's short length is a technical failure, not a behavioral one. Facts: all nine rejected outputs omit only the preferences field; five of them fell in rounds 42-44; the third consecutive one stopped the run. The rejected plans were otherwise coherent and close to their accepted retries (Lin's ordinary-79 and -80 against -81; Ivo's -133 against -134). Why the model omitted the field is not established by the archive. (2) The handoffs produced mastery rather than outcomes because the world lacked what the mechanisms needed. Facts: the declarations record that whole food does not dry under the air-dry rules; that ignition is impossible with the world's wet branches and grass; that the constituent test's run mode needs reagents, kits and readers the scope does not represent; and that sample comparison needs a cutting tool, a support and a tested comparator. Explanation: people read these contracts and, consistent with their cautious notes ('does not establish', 'not a demonstrated'), declined to start them. (3) Convergent instrument work followed the dependency structure of Moss's handoff. Fact: beam-mass-comparator came with Moss's request, and five people studied or planned it. Explanation: it was the only new operation whose first steps (assess, then assemble from a branch and a stone that anyone could gather) were executable. The work was not pooled: Sol built one comparator, Ivo prepared another, Moss assessed its own materials, and the two proposals to compare first (Moss's and Ivo's) were lost in rejected outputs. (4) Communication stayed instrumental. Fact: each message reported the sender's own trial limits or asked for others' tested findings, and each person sent at most two. Explanation: projects were individual and food was abundant, so nothing required coordination; this run alone cannot say how far the instructions shaped this. (5) Whole-patch taking had no consequence because food was not scarce: commons patches of 20 lay nearby, and satiety never fell below 58.9. (6) Refusals shaped timing. Fact: 6 goal-overlap refusals arose where people converged on the same stone and hazelnut sources, and 14 of 46 plans were repairs after a refusal or an invalid reference. (7) Wren's isolation was spatial. Fact: it worked the western roots and branches alone until tick 26 and joined others only when it went for stone.

## Limitations

One seed, six people and one model (gpt-6.1-sol). Only 44 of the 80 target rounds were observed, so the window is 55% of the planned one and is cut by a technical stop. Later behavior, such as any test of Sol's comparator or any reply from Kai, is unobserved. A single run cannot support any group-level conclusion about the goal sentence or the leaning sentence. The supplementary common window of ticks 1-44 and every comparison between arms belong to the experiment B report. The seven labels were all model negatives, verified by Claude agents rather than by a human (agreement 1.0). With no positives, the verification cannot test the labeler's sensitivity in this run. Proximity and absence are judged by the 5-unit vision radius from wellbeing-event positions. Breaking the moderation norm is defined here as emptying or taking over half of a source while someone within 5 units has satiety below 50, a criterion chosen for this report; a per-capita definition would flag Ivo's and Lin's emptying gathers. Text is quoted from accepted and rejected retained calls; plan steps that never ran (Ivo's tick-18 message, Moss's replies, Kai's comparator gathering) are not counted as actions. The feasibility statements about ignition come from the developer's throwaway check recorded in the declaration, not from replayed events. Rejected outputs changed decision timing for Lin, Wren, Kai, Ivo and Moss. The archived phase reason is generic text. All behavior was produced by one shared model, so similar wording across people is weak evidence of transmission or of individuality.
