# ex16: Behavioral Analysis

Reviewer: Current Claude Code conversation; behavioral analysis, not independent approval. Task: pr-39-culture-v2-run. Recommendations are analysis only; no world, prompt or agent change is authorized by this report.

## Summary

E3's first attempt (ex16) ran the culture v2 scenario (tag long-culture-v2, scope long-ex16) on the ex13 rules, seed and world with six people (Lin, Moss, Sol, Kai, Wren, Ivo). It kept the ex15 culture conditions and added the open goal ('Keep yourself healthy and safe; that is required. Beyond that, how you live, what you do and what you care about is up to you. You tend to value [leaning].'), lossless context compaction with a 25-round recent-use window, a 24,000-token context guard, 6,000-code-unit exact reads and uncounted recovery of rejection and transport pauses. It completed the 80-round window with all six alive at health 100 in every round (satiety never below 60). 149 calls (140 ordinary, 9 World Agent) charged 1,161,283 ledger tokens: 1,097,515 of known usage (1,029,287 in, 68,228 out; 2,286 per person per round) and 63,768 reserved for two transport failures with unknown usage (Moss, tick 2). Four outputs were rejected: three by Kai, each containing a 241-character tell (two recorded as tell-length errors, one as a plan-shape error), and one plan-shape error by Moss. Both plan-shape errors omitted the preferences field. The two transport failures and Kai's double rejection at tick 24 were recovered without a handoff. The largest input estimate was 10,983 tokens (Moss, tick 49), far below the guard. Three requests became three counted handoffs, each an installed, simplified but real mechanism: cultivate-plants@1 for Wren (development-1), branch-screen@1 for Sol (development-2) and hand-weave-fiber-mat@1 for Moss (development-3). Gate G2 failed on both conditions. Survival behavior took 50.6% of actor-rounds (243 of 480), 186 of them rest; without rest the share is 11.9%. Rest was a default idle behavior by both analyzer criteria: 115 of 186 rests were idle and 145 of 186 began at 75% of the energy cap or more. Wren made no symbolic action. The others told 3 times (Lin), 9 times plus one invitation (Moss), 6 (Sol), 7 (Kai) and 3 (Ivo); nobody named, marked or messaged. Speech centred on a shared camp. Kai's opening wish for 'a fair camp' and Moss's question 'What would make a shared camp welcoming to you?' drew answers from Lin, Kai and Sol. Sol's windbreak request and Moss's sitting-mat request both came out of that exchange. Moss wove and trialled a grass mat and invited listeners to it for round 60; Ivo came, and Moss and Sol were present. Sol built a branch screen that gave no shelter in the prevailing wind, and said it liked the pattern anyway. Five people mastered cultivation, but only Wren planted. The planting rule (ordinary soil, clear of people and objects, fewer than 3 plants of the same food or prepared plots within 5 m) and its clearance check refused Lin, Kai (twice) and Ivo near the camp, and those three set cultivation aside for rest or waiting. Wren walked about 12 units away to an uncrowded site, planted one root that had not sprouted by tick 62, began a reed mat, and heard six utterances without ever speaking. Lin's hazelnut observation and trial produced the run's only satisfaction event (tick 74) and its only new food knowledge (one hazelnut: 18 satiety, toxicity 0). Five of the six cares name the person's leaning; Wren's paraphrases it and Wren's preferences name 'practical mastery'. Five people wrote 11 private views, and the analyzer found no norm statement. Ticks below are replay event ticks; a call recorded at tick T decides the actions that start at tick T+1.

## Isolation

## Behavior

Survival behavior took 243 of 480 actor-rounds (50.6%): rest 186, eat 29 attempts and gather 28. Without rest the share is 11.9%. Rest was a default idle behavior. By the step criterion, 115 of 186 rests were idle; 96 came from plan steps with no energy condition, typically a rest repeated until satiety fell, used as a timer to the next meal. By start energy, 145 of 186 began at 75% of the cap or more. Lin (44), Kai (39) and Moss (36) rested most. Ivo idled with wait instead (38 waits outside study, 11 rests). Symbolic action was speech plus one invitation: 28 tells and 1 invite, with no names, marks or messages. Eight tells open with a listener's name. Three requests (root cultivation, a branch windbreak, a grass mat) were all developed. Five people mastered cultivate-plants; it produced one plot (Wren's) against four refused plantings. Only Sol mastered and used branch-screen, which ended as a complete screen with no current lee effect. hand-weave-fiber-mat produced Moss's mat; Kai learned it without using it, and Wren was using it at tick 80. Plans were long: 59 accepted plans, a median gap of 6 rounds, 10% single-execution, and none used interrupts. The analyzer found no norm statement, one causal note and one non-transactional utterance in 29. All six kept health 100, and Lin's hazelnut was the only food added to anyone's knowledge.

## Causes

Three mechanisms explain most of the window. They are case explanations from one seed. (1) Idle rest. Health and safety were the only requirement and food was plentiful, so plans filled the time between meals with rest repeated until satiety fell, not until energy recovered. Rest at the cap restores nothing. The ex16 prompt did not say so, and only Moss noted it (tick 71). The gate counts all of this as survival, including the quiet company near Moss's mat. (2) The planting site rule. cultivate-plants reuses the dispersal law's spacing: a plot needs ordinary soil, 0.6 m of clearance and fewer than 3 plants of that food or prepared plots within 5 m. Berry and root commons ring the start area. In an offline count over initial.json, none of the 30 meadow cells within 5 units of the people's starting centroid (14.0, 15.0) has fewer than 3 berry plants or fewer than 3 root plants within 5 m, and the nearest eligible cells are about 7.4-7.5 units away. Near the camp the density rule refused Lin (6 berry plants) and Kai (4 berry plants), and the clearance check refused Ivo and Kai. Lin, Kai and Ivo then set cultivation aside, which is where much of their idle time began. Wren had read the rule before moving (its tick-12 notes) and walked to (21, 8.65) to plant, about 12 units from where the others stayed. (3) Wren's silence. None of the six utterances it heard named it, it never requested the tell contract, and every one of its plans consisted of practical work. Its distance from the clearing after tick 21 also reduced what it could hear. Kai's three rejections share one cause: the tell contract says 'Say up to 240 characters' while its schema shows maxLength 1000, and each rejected output contained a 241-character tell. Capabilities with long timescales could not pay off within 80 rounds. Wren's root showed no sprout after 24 rounds at light 0, and the screen's shelter rule does not act above wind 0.8.

## Limitations

One seed and six people give a case, not an estimate. ex15 to ex16 changed the context (compaction, recent-use window, guard, exact-read width) and the prompt (open goal and leaning) together. G2's survival half counts rest whether it recovers energy or serves as company, and its symbolic half fails on a single silent person; neither half measures culture by itself. Two transport failures have unknown token usage (63,768 reserved tokens), and developer and review model usage is not metered. The analyzer treats tells as undirected, so named turns and the camp exchange do not appear in its relationship graphs, and its norm and idea patterns are rule-based English matches. The eligibility count near the camp is this reviewer's offline approximation from initial.json at cell centres, ignoring moisture, people and later plots; it is not an archived measurement. Planting and weaving outcomes (establishment, mat wear) lie beyond the window. The reasons for silence or idleness cannot be observed: the prompt asks for no inner monologue, and outputs record only plans, notes and memory.

## Proposed improvements

1. Report an idle-invariant activity measure next to G2's survival share. Count as idle every rest that begins within one nominal rest of the cap or comes from a plan step with no energy condition, every wait outside study, and every navigation round that does not move; then compute the survival share over the remaining actor-rounds. In ex16 survival was 50.6% with rest and 11.9% without. Ivo idled about as long as Lin, but with wait, which the gate does not count. The raw share therefore depends on which idle interface a plan names rather than on how much survival work people do. Verification: Compute the measure from retained ex15 and ex16 files without a model call. It should change little when only the idle interface changes, which the ex16-to-ex17 rerun can test, and every pinned ex7-ex16 culture metric should stay unchanged.
2. Add an exposure line to pnpm metrics:culture for each person without a symbolic action: utterances heard, utterances that name them, query reads of the name, mark, tell and invite contracts, and rounds within the hearing radius of another living person. G2's symbolic half failed on Wren alone. The archive shows that Wren heard 6 utterances, none naming it, read no symbolic contract, and was within 5 units of someone in 45 of 80 rounds. These counts separate 'not reached' from 'reached but silent' without reading private memory. Verification: On ex16 the line reports Wren with 6 heard, 0 naming it, 0 reads and 45 rounds, and reports no other person, matching the counts in this analysis.
3. In a new scenario tag, align the text schemas of tell, name, mark and invite with their enforced limits (240, 60, 120 and 120 characters), so the schema in the packet states the same bound as the function text. packages/world-core/src/expression.ts builds these schemas with text(), whose default maxLength is 1000. Kai's packet in ordinary-54 shows 'Say up to 240 characters' next to maxLength 1000. All three of Kai's rejected outputs (ordinary-54, -70 and -71) contained a 241-character tell, and they caused the run's only rejection pause. ex15 also had a tell-length rejection (Sol, ordinary-52). Verification: Prompt-compatibility digests change only for the new tag (ex7-ex16 unchanged), and a later run under the tag records no tell-length rejection.
4. Before interpreting dispersal or co-location in a culture run, publish an offline eligibility map of the cultivation site rule over the start layout, and report each planting attempt's distance from the group and its refusal reason. Under the dispersal-law spacing, no meadow cell within 5 units of the start centroid admits a berry or root planting, so anyone who wants to plant must leave the group. In ex16 the rule and its clearance check refused four attempts near the camp, three people gave up, and the only planter moved about 12 units away. Whether planted plots should follow a different spacing is a world-rule decision for the user; this analysis does not propose that change. Verification: The map, computed from initial.json and the replayed plots, reproduces the four ex16 refusals and Wren's accepted site, and the reported distances match the replay.
