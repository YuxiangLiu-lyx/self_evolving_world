# ex16: Behavioral Analysis

Reviewer: Current Claude Code conversation; behavioral analysis, not independent approval. Task: pr-42-culture-v2-extension. Recommendations are analysis only; no world, prompt or agent change is authorized by this report.

## Summary

This analysis covers the whole ex16 archive (E3's culture v2 main run, tag long-culture-v2, scope long-ex16; six people on the ex13 rules, seed 20261123 and world) through round 250, and focuses on what changed after the first window, which the earlier analysis pr-39-culture-v2-run covers and which stays as it is. Round convention: ticks are replay event ticks, and a call recorded at tick T (a decision at round T) settles in events stamped tick T+1. The first window is ticks 1-80 (decisions 0-79) and the extension is ticks 81-250 (decisions 80-249); the analyzer's round numbers for texts are event ticks too. After round 80 the run continued on its own source branch from the round-80 checkpoint commit 2409b37 (plan pr-42-culture-v2-extension, R1a), with checkpoints at 150 and 250 (window-history.json preserves the through-80 and through-150 windows). All six stayed alive at health 100 in every round; minimum satiety ranged from 45 (Moss) to 64.9 (Lin). 340 calls (322 ordinary: 314 accepted, 5 rejected, 3 failed; 18 World Agent) charged 2,903,618 ledger tokens: 2,794,058 of known usage (2,645,721 in, 148,337 out) and 109,560 reserved for three calls with unknown usage (ordinary-19 and -21, Moss, tick 2; ordinary-168, Lin, tick 99, 45,792), or 1,935.7 per person per round. The extension's calls (ticks 80-249: 182 ordinary, 9 World Agent) charged 1,742,335 tokens, 1,708 per person per round, below the first window's 2,419, because plans grew longer. Two calls failed or were rejected after round 80: ordinary-168 ('fetch failed', no retained evidence) opened technical-pauses/round-99, closed by the empty, uncounted development-4; ordinary-334 (Kai, tick 241) omitted the preferences field and was rejected, and Kai's next call (ordinary-335) was accepted without a pause. The largest input estimate was 13,326 tokens (Wren, ordinary-338, tick 243). Of 7 handoffs, 6 were counted, and the three after round 80 were all Wren's: hand-break-food@1 (round 204), hand-twist-fiber-cord@1 (221) and hand-tie-cord-knot@1 (234). The revised gate G2 passes at rounds 120, 150 and 250. The original gate fails at all three: on both halves at 120 and 150 (survival share 51.5% and 52.9%; Wren silent), and at 250 only on the survival share (55.8%), because Wren spoke at ticks 204, 233 and 249. Idle time (rest and wait) rose from 50.6% of actor-rounds in ticks 1-80 to 63.9% in ticks 81-250, and reached 59.7% of the whole run (895 of 1,500: rest 692, wait 203). Most of it was rest or wait at or near the energy cap, repeated until satiety fell to a meal threshold, spent close to companions. Speech thinned from 29 symbolic actions in the first 80 rounds to 16 in the next 170 (8 in ticks 81-150, 8 in 151-250), and plans had no interrupts. The camp vocabulary of ticks 18-25 ('camp', 'fair', 'room to come and go', 'food accessible') never returned to speech after tick 37. 'Quiet company' (or 'quiet pause') became the recurring phrase (9 tells after round 80). Two new items spread: Lin's hazelnut report ('18 satiety with no observed toxicity'), which Sol, Moss and Kai each verified by eating one (ticks 102, 128, 137), and Sol and Kai repeated it aloud; and an appreciation of the woven objects ('crossing branches'). Two stable pairs formed: Moss and Ivo were within 5 units in all 170 extension rounds, Sol and Kai in 139. Lin left on a solitary western and southern loop (ticks 100-209) and was within 5 units of anyone in only 4 of rounds 151-250. Wren mastered six packages and ran a continuous personal chain: harvest from its root plot (tick 183), one hand-break trial (a root snapped into two pieces, tick 207), a 0.30 m two-ply reed cord that held 10 N for 60 s (tick 232), and an overhand knot that held 5 N for 30 s and was untied (ticks 240-246). Nobody else learned the cord or knot packages. Sol and Kai learned hand-break-food from the interface catalogue, not from Wren, and each used it once (a hazelnut resisted, a berry deformed). No name, mark, message, give, exchange or new invitation occurred in the whole run.

## Per-agent findings

### lin

Lin (leaning freedom) spent the first window near the camp after its single berry planting was refused. After round 80 its pattern reversed. At tick 95 (ordinary-163) it chose to 'study mat weaving as a practical new option for making things independently' and widened its cares to 'freedom to wander and make things' (tick 96, its only cares change after tick 1). It mastered hand-weave-fiber-mat at tick 99 and passed a dry-grass plaiting test at tick 103 (event-103-lin-1211). The source was then empty and Lin held only 2.9 of the 4 units a mat needs (ordinary-180), so it paused the mat. From tick 100 to 209 it made a solitary loop of about 50 units: west to the hazelnuts at (9.8, 15.3), the wild apples at (6.8, 18.3), the forest edge at (1, 22), south to a cave at (6, 31), then east and north to the berry patch at (15.1, 21.1). It probed and then ate an apple at tick 130 (10 satiety, toxicity 0, event-130-world-1510), the run's only new food after the hazelnut. It probed the cave at tick 177 and planned 'a quiet pause inside the cave' (ordinary-250). Its preferences were explicit: 'Prefer discovering new places and materials over repeating meals solely for their temporary pleasure' (ordinary-220) and 'Explore without committing to construction or a permanent home' (ordinary-250). Lin made no symbolic action after tick 78 and never reported the apples. After round 80 it heard only two utterances (Moss, ticks 98 and 100), and it was within 5 units of anyone in 37 of rounds 81-150 and 4 of rounds 151-250, the least of anyone (first window: 80 of 80). Back at the berry patch it rested for most of ticks 211-250 ('rest repeat 24 until satiety <= 65', ordinary-271). At tick 240 it planned to 'study cord-making and read its exact working contract' (ordinary-332), which it found in the interface catalogue, not by hearing Wren. That step was not reached by round 250. Lin rested 83 times and waited twice in ticks 81-250, and had the largest movement distance of anyone (59.5). It ended at health 100, satiety 71.

Lin's leaning shaped the extension more than any other: a solitary exploration loop, a cares text that now includes 'freedom to wander and make things', and preferences against a permanent home. The cost was social. Lin left hearing range, stopped speaking, and its apple knowledge never reached anyone. Its mat project stalled on material, not skill: the dry-grass source near the camp was exhausted.

## Causes

These are case explanations from one seed. (1) Idle co-presence. Food was abundant and health and safety were the only requirement. Plans therefore filled the time between meals with rest or wait repeated until satiety fell, and people placed those pauses beside companions or objects they liked. Several of them knew that rest at the cap restored nothing. (2) Fading speech. A heard utterance is not a decision trigger: packages/world-core/src/continuous-tasks.ts re-decides on mastery, season change, spoilage, animal alerts, injury and similar events, and no plan used interrupts. A listener therefore answered only when its own plan ended, and plans grew long (Moss 61 rounds from tick 127, Ivo 70 from tick 124). This explains the 47-round silence (ticks 148-194), Moss's silence after tick 204 and the absence of replies to Wren. The season change at tick 200 re-decided all six at once, and 5 of the 16 extension utterances fell in ticks 201-220. (3) Wren's chain. Each request was built from Wren's own last result (a harvested root, the reed it held from mat work, the finished cord). The World Agent chose develop each time (world-287, -312, -325), and the packages are real but deliberately reward-free: no satisfaction, no qualified practice flag, and hand-break pieces eaten like a whole root (14 satiety). Wren's root-preparation request asked for 'a different meal', and the result changed only the form, which Wren noted: 'this did not improve its nutrition, safety, taste or variety' (ordinary-294). Its only satisfaction came from a probe press on a berry (tick 219), because the confirmed-understanding source is bound to probe. Wren never ate the berry: it treated Kai's report as testimony and not as its own record. (4) Unheard first tell. Wren decided to speak while Moss was 3.5 units away, then rested three rounds first. Moss's own plan, also from tick 200, carried it to 5.16 units. (5) Material scarcity. The dry-grass source near the camp was exhausted (Lin's 2.9 of 4 units at tick 103, stock 0 at Sol's probe at tick 215). This stalled mat-making for Lin and Sol after both had mastered it. (6) Hazelnut spread. The report travelled because listeners had their own reason to try it (variety, Kai's open question). Each verified it personally before repeating it, so the propagation coincides with real food knowledge. Ivo heard the report but never planned a trial, and Wren never heard it; neither ate a hazelnut. (7) Lin's exploration took it out of earshot. The listener set for most extension speech was Moss, Sol, Kai and Ivo.

## Limitations

One seed and six people give a case, not an estimate. The extension ran on a separate source branch. This analysis did not run the R1b check that replays the archive on main's code with byte-identical requests; it relies on the archive's own replay and integrity records. The term, sharing, rule, norm and phrase detectors are rule-based English matches. Three of the six rule adoptions G2 counts at round 250 are post-80 private texts matched to other people's private notes, with exposure only to a tick-1 tell. The woven-object appreciation and the hazelnut chain are this reviewer's reading of the tells and events. Positions are carried forward from each person's last positioned event, and distances use the 5-unit vision radius as hearing range. Idle percentages from the analyzer include study waits (895 actor-rounds); excluding them gives 857. The window ends with work in progress: Wren's second cord (round 1 of 4) and its planned request to join cords, Lin's planned cord study, and Kai's planned message to Sol. Three calls have unknown usage (109,560 reserved tokens). Developer and review model usage for the four extension handoffs is not metered, and development-6 took about 16.8 hours of wall time, which does not affect the world. The reasons for silence, unanswered questions and idleness cannot be observed: outputs record plans, notes and memory, not inner reasoning.
