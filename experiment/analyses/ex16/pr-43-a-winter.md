## Summary

This analysis covers experiment A of ex16 (E3's culture v2 main run, tag long-culture-v2, scope long-ex16; six people on the ex13 world, seed 20261123), which extended the archive from round 250 to round 400 as the configured survival pressure began. It focuses on ticks 251-400 and on the change at tick 300; the earlier analyses pr-39-culture-v2-run (rounds 0-80) and pr-42-culture-v2-extension (0-250) stay as they are. Tick convention: ticks are replay event ticks, and a decision (a call) at round r settles in events stamped tick r + 1, so ticks 251-400 hold decisions at rounds 250-399. Comparisons use the halves 201-300 and 301-400 and 25-tick blocks, read by exact replay (reconstructContinuousSegment over replay/execution-000200-000300.json and execution-000300-000400.json; every round matched its recorded hashes, events and cursors). A ran in three windows (250-300, 300-350, 350-400; window-history.json preserves through-250, through-300 and through-350) and source-history.json records revisions 9-16. The pressure ramps linearly from tick 300 to full at 440: at tick 400 its intensity is 0.714, the cold 1.07 degrees, the rain floor 0.57 and the plant growth factor 0.714. The world's season label was summer through tick 399 and autumn at 400; winter begins at tick 600. A charged 2,044,361 ledger tokens (4,947,979 at round 400 against 2,903,618 at 250) in 188 of the run's 528 calls: 170 ordinary (166 accepted, 2 failed with 'fetch failed', 2 rejected) and 18 World Agent, 2,271 per person per round. Handoffs 8-15 installed six packages, all requested by and assigned to Wren: hand-join-fiber-cords@1 (round 256), hand-bind-crossing@1 (278), hand-twist-sectioned-cord@1 (298), hand-lash-crossing@1 (329), hand-frap-crossing@1 (363) and hand-twist-long-sectioned-cord@1 (368). The two empty, uncounted handoffs closed transport pauses: development-10 for Wren's ordinary-375 (282) and development-14 for Lin's ordinary-484 (365). All six were alive at health 100 at every tick; no injury occurred. The cold had not bitten by tick 400. Mean body temperature fell from 28.6 degrees (201-300) to 25.4 (301-400), minimum 20.1, mostly through the seasonal cycle (its sine term fell 7.8 degrees between ticks 300 and 400, against 1.07 of configured cold). Because bodies had been above the 27-degree comfort limit in 590 of 600 actor-ticks before tick 300, the cooling raised the thermal comfort term from 0.865 to 0.992, and mean wellbeing rose slightly (9.1 to 9.2). Nobody spoke or wrote about cold, temperature, season or the pressure, although every packet showed it. Nobody made fire, built or repaired shelter, or used a mat or screen. Both screens became damaged (Sol's at 321, Wren's at 394) and stayed unrepaired. Kai's cave visit (ticks 378-384) was an inspection that ended with 'no additional shelter effect has been established'. Food stayed abundant (world edible stock 9,840 to 9,494 nutrition). Gathering rose (422 to 518 nutrition) and eating fell (558 to 476). The largest store was Lin's 180 nutrition, 2.5 forest days of drain. No one gave, exchanged or transferred anything. The culture metrics find 13 sharing expressions in A, 2 of them spoken, and no gather emptied a source or took more than half of it while a nearby person was short. The clearest change after tick 300 was social. Tells fell from 11 and a message in 201-300 to 3 tells and a name in 301-400 (heard events 24 to 3), and solitary actor-ticks rose from 103 to 233. The Sol-Kai pair dissolved, and only Moss and Ivo stayed together. Idle time fell (69.0% to 57.7%) and walking rose (67 to 115 navigate rounds) as Kai, Sol and Lin explored apples, fiber, a cave and a birch. The run's first message (Kai to Sol, tick 270, answered at 284) and first name (Sol's 'The Little Apple Pause', 373) fall in A. Wren ran the longest chain: cords of 0.54, 0.64 and 1.14 m overall, a single-wrap binding, a two-turn lashing that slipped at 4.3 N, and a frapped two-turn lashing that held 5 N sliding and 10 N separation at ticks 399-400. It spoke once in A (277), and nobody else used its work. Recommendations are analysis only.

## Causes

These are case explanations from one seed. (1) The pressure was too weak by tick 400 to matter physiologically. Its cold is 1.07 degrees against a 7.8-degree seasonal fall, bodies started above the comfort band, and damage begins below 7 degrees while the lowest body temperature was 20.1. Cooling first removed heat discomfort, so wellbeing gave no signal to act on. (2) Food was not scarce. Abundant commons patches (most at 20 units) and a growth factor still at 0.71 left stocks almost unchanged. Moderation therefore cost nothing, which explains why it was easy to keep and why no one needed to give. (3) Nobody interpreted the reported pressure. Packets carried 'pressure accumulating' and 'gradually cooling', but plans attended to inventory, energy and stock counts and repeatedly refused to assume effects ('not a demonstrated source of warmth', 'no additional shelter effect has been established'). That caution, a strength in craft work, also kept people from anticipating the season. (4) Dispersal came from curiosity and individual projects, not from the cold: Kai's apple trial and reed search, Sol following Kai's report, Lin's fiber search, Wren's workspace. (5) Long plans and the end of speech cut the remaining social channel. Ivo's one plan to tick 470 had no interrupts. Wren and Lin did not speak, and listeners after tick 300 were only Sol and Ivo. (6) Wren's frapping result is not a controlled improvement. The frapped lashing used a thicker cord (6.2 mm against 5.9 mm) and a different cord length than the lashing that slipped, as Wren itself noted.

## Limitations

One seed, six people and one model; no counterfactual run without the pressure, so its effect is not separated from the seasonal cycle, the drought hazard or the passage of time. The window ends at tick 400 at intensity 0.714; the full difficulty (from 440) and winter (from 600) are unobserved, so nothing here predicts behavior under real cold. Thermal comfort is reconstructed from the wellbeing formula and body temperature, not read from a separate record. Hoarding is judged against a three-forest-day drain (216 nutrition) chosen for this report. Breaking a norm is defined here as emptying or taking over half of a source while someone within 5 units has satiety below 50; other definitions (for example share per capita) would flag Moss's 12-of-20 and Lin's 2-of-2 gathers. The sharing expressions are rule-based English patterns and miss paraphrases such as Ivo's 'Leave the nearly depleted berries alone for now'. The response window of 10 rounds misses Sol's answer to Kai. Absence is judged by the 5-unit vision radius at decision time. Text mentions were read from accepted and rejected retained calls; plan steps that never ran were not counted as actions. Transport failures (ordinary-375, -484) and two rejections (ordinary-380, -501) changed the decision timing for Wren, Lin and Sol. Development records describe Wren with a gendered pronoun; this analysis does not. All behavior was produced by one shared model, so similar wording across people is weak evidence of transmission.
