# ex15: Behavioral Analysis

Reviewer: Current Claude Code conversation; behavioral analysis, not independent approval. Task: pr-37-culture-pilot. Recommendations are analysis only; no world, prompt or agent change is authorized by this report.

## Summary

E2 ran the culture scenario (scope long-ex15) on the ex13 rules, seed and world with six people (Lin, Moss, Sol, Kai, Wren, Ivo), voice and private memory, differentiated dispositions, the name, mark, tell and invite interfaces, survival slack and the food water representation, under frozen budgets of 1,800 ordinary and 300 World Agent calls, 40 development handoffs, a 3,200,000-token ceiling before round 80 and a 10,000,000-token ledger. It paused at round 70 of the 80-round window: the pre-inference guard estimated Ivo's next decision at 14,396 tokens against the frozen 14,000 limit, and the lossless compaction that would fit (12,674) changes a source file the verifier protects mid-run, so the run cannot continue under its protocol and E3 and E4 were not run. All six people were alive at health 100 (satiety 49-95). 159 calls (147 ordinary, 12 World Agent) used 1,320,077 tokens with no unknown usage, 3,143 per agent per round (ex13 rounds 0-69: 6,511; ex14: 3,641). Gate G2 is met on rounds 0-69 (survival behavior 39.5% of actor-rounds, every person told at least once) and cannot fail through round 79 (at most 47.1%). Symbolic use was speech only: 28 tells and no names, marks or invitations. Only 3 of 9 direct messages were delivered; 6 failed because the recipient was not visible, 4 of them addressed to Lin. Nine tells that open with the listener's name formed a Moss-Sol exchange about bark and vines. Five requests produced five developed packages, none a first release: cultivation-trial was learned and planted by five people, food-preparation learned by three and used by two, shared-shelter learned by two, simple-shelter and bark-separation only by their requesters; no shelter was started and no planting established within the window. Of eight counted handoffs, four installed packages, two recovered pauses after two rejected model outputs, one was a failed submission (a declared visual the viewer lacked) and one closed the context pause. The E0 analyzer found 14 candidate terms and no shared term, no norm statement, one causal note, three reciprocated gives and no non-transactional speech. Private memory stayed nearly static: each person wrote cares once at round 0 (mean pairwise similarity 0.248, unchanged) and the only view was Sol's of Lin, so private view convergence is null.

## Per-agent findings

### lin

Lin (curiosity 0.8, leaning freedom) asked aloud at round 0 whether anyone had tested building materials and requested a weather shelter (ordinary-3); after one plan-shape rejection at round 2 (ordinary-19, corrected in ordinary-20) the request executed at tick 9. The World Agent chose develop (world-32); development-3 failed validation and development-4 installed simple-shelter@1, which Lin mastered at tick 12. At round 12 Lin told everyone nearby the recipe (13 branches, 4 vines, 6 bark, 12 work rounds) and 'Sol, let's coordinate materials' (ordinary-46, event-13-lin-146). Its own vine search at round 26 returned no record, but it had heard Wren report vines at (8.2,13.3) at tick 22; its message to Sol cites that report (ordinary-78) and it gathered 4 vines there at tick 50. By tick 67 it held exactly 13 branches and 4 vines, the whole single-bay recipe except bark. From ticks 31 to 37 it stayed within about 5 units of Moss and heard Moss say it held Sol's five bark, but it did not re-decide until round 45, when its long plan had taken it west, about 14 units away. Its one delivered message (to Sol, tick 40) and Sol's reply (tick 41) pointed it to Moss; its message to Moss failed at tick 68 because Moss was not visible (ordinary-136). Lin ate twice (ticks 4 and 35) and ended with the lowest satiety, 49, at health 100.

Lin is the clearest case of speech changing an action: it acted on a heard vine location when its own records were empty. Its shelter stayed unbuilt one transfer short, with the six bark it needed in Moss's inventory about 15 units away; the binding constraint was contact range and plan length, not materials or rules. Its final preference ('avoid repeated messages to absent recipients') shows it learned the message limit but tried neither walking to Moss nor an invitation.

## Isolation

Scope long-ex15 inherits no generated ability; every developed package is scoped to long-ex15 and declares its reuse of earlier infrastructure. Each person learned interfaces individually, through learn or the requester's assignment. Information crossed between people through 28 spoken tells (heard by every living person within 5 units), 3 delivered messages, 3 completed gives and the public interface registry. Private cares and views appear only in the owner's packet: Lin's round-26 packet (ordinary-78) holds its own cares and an empty view list, while Sol's view of Lin appears only in Sol's packets (for example ordinary-143). The analyzer reads cares and views only in its owner-only section.

## Individuality

Dispositions spread from 0.2 to 0.8 and each person had a different leaning, but no one used its leaning word (mastery and belonging occur only as 'mastered ability' and 'a trial belonging to Ivo'), and the six cares written at round 0 are near paraphrases of one another (staying alive, learning through evidence, making everyday life better for oneself and nearby people; mean pairwise content-word similarity 0.248, unchanged for 70 rounds). Behavior diverged more than stated values: Lin gathered shelter materials alone in the west; Moss and Sol coordinated bark and vines in the south; Kai verified reported foods; Wren scouted planting sites; Ivo made two requests and mastered four interfaces. Moss and Kai made no request. Only Sol recorded a view of anyone.

## Behavior

Rest remained the most common action (115 of 420 actor-rounds) and survival behavior took 39.5%. Plans were long: 65 accepted plans, a median gap of 5 rounds and 16.9% single-execution plans; no plan used interrupt events, so heard speech never triggered a re-decision. Speech was the only symbolic channel used (28 tells; no names, marks or invitations); 14 tells named another person and 9 named turns formed a Moss-Sol exchange. Direct messages mostly failed (6 of 9, all for an invisible recipient). Requests concerned shelter (3), food preparation (1) and cultivation (1). Cultivation spread to five people; food-preparation was used twice, bark-separation once, and neither shelter was started.

## Causes

Messages need a visible recipient and plans were never interruptible by hearing, so the only person who could build the one-bay shelter (Lin) and the person holding its bark (Moss) lost contact after tick 37 while Lin executed a long plan that took it west; the shelter remained one transfer short. Survival slack and mild weather removed urgency: nobody's health fell below 100, so nothing forced the coordination. Developed capabilities with long timescales could not pay off within 70 rounds: in the developer's test a planted root established after 82 rounds under this seed, and light was 0 at every trial observation from tick 39 to 65. The context guard was reached because packets grew with work records, memory and the ability catalogue (first-call estimates rose from about 6,100 tokens in rounds 0-9 to about 9,300 in rounds 40-69 and 12,174 for Ivo at round 70), and the only adequate lossless compaction lives in a protected file. This is one seed with six people; these are case explanations, not causal estimates.

## Limitations

The run covered 70 of 80 planned rounds; gate G2 is judged on rounds 0-69, although it cannot fail through round 79. Comparisons use ex13 rounds 0-69 and all 64 ex14 rounds, both with three people, and the culture conditions (six people, voice, symbols, slack, differentiation, food water) were switched on together, so no condition has a separate effect. Four of eight counted handoffs produced no package (two rejection pauses, one failed submission, one closing recovery). Developer and review model usage is not metered. The E0 analyzer treats tells as public and undirected, so the named Moss-Sol exchange is absent from its relationship graphs, and its rule-based text detection has the limitations listed in the baseline. The bark-separation package fills the missing composition with ex12's 10% bark share although the World Agent specified not to invent a fixed branch-to-bark conversion.

## Proposed improvements

1. Decide, as a user decision, how a culture run may continue past the context guard: either a new run version that includes the culture-gated lossless compaction of the ordinary packet in apps/simulator/src/long-context.ts, or a verifier policy that admits lossless, disclosed, scenario-gated packet changes mid-run when compatibility digests are unchanged. The guard, not tokens, time or handoffs, stopped ex15 at round 70 (Ivo 14,396 against 14,000); the measured compaction gives 12,674 with ex7-ex14 compatibility identical, but long-context.ts is protected mid-run and unprotected modules save about 125 tokens. Verification: Under the chosen option, Ivo's round-70 input estimate is below 14,000, ex7-ex14 compatibility digests stay identical, and a culture run reaches the round-80 and round-150 checkpoints without a context pause.
2. Make whole exact reads cover developed interface records, either by letting exact reads of ability records use a larger width in a new scenario or by checking at handoff that a developed interface record fits the 4,000-code-unit width. Four of the five developed ability records were served in 7-9 pages of 600 code units; ex15 had 13 continuation-part queries (12 on developed abilities) and 9 of 25 decisions without a query batch were made on a partly read record, where ex14 had 0 and 0 of 14. Verification: Continuation-part queries and decisions with an unfinished record, against ex15 (13 and 9/25) over the same rounds.
3. Stop spending counted handoffs on work that installs nothing: treat a pause after two rejected model outputs like the uncounted transport recovery, and offer a non-counting pre-submission check that runs the cheap structural checks (visual components, excluded vocabulary) before typecheck, test and build. Four of eight ex15 handoffs installed no package: two rejection pauses (Kai at round 0, Sol at round 13), the failed development-3 submission (a declared visual the viewer lacked, found only after the full validation) and the closing context recovery. Verification: Counted handoffs per installed package and the share of handoffs without a package, against ex15 (8 handoffs, 5 packages, 4 without a package).
4. Report tells that open with a listener's name as a separate, clearly labeled directed-speech relation in pnpm metrics:culture, alongside the existing message graphs. Spoken words are public by design and the analyzer treats them as undirected, so 9 named Moss-Sol turns and the response chains around them are invisible in the relationship graphs (1 message response in the whole run). Verification: On ex15 the new relation lists the 9 Moss-Sol turns and the Lin, Wren and Ivo addressed tells counted by hand, and every existing metric and the ex7-ex14 pinned values are unchanged.
