# ex17: Behavioral Analysis

Reviewer: Current Claude Code conversation; behavioral analysis, not independent approval. Task: pr-39-culture-v3-rerun. Recommendations are analysis only; no world, prompt or agent change is authorized by this report.

## Summary

The G2-failure rerun (ex17, tag long-culture-v3, scope long-ex17) repeated ex16's world, seed, six people, budgets and context settings with one adjustment to the wording of part A. The open goal now also leaves open 'with whom', and the leaning is followed by 'Rest restores missing energy up to the cap; at full energy it restores nothing.' The run completed the 80-round window with all six alive at health 100 in every round (satiety never below 47). 110 calls (107 ordinary, 3 World Agent) used 793,053 tokens (741,747 in, 51,306 out; 1,652 per person per round), with no rejected output, no transport failure and a largest input estimate of 9,964 tokens. One request (Wren's root cultivation) became the one counted handoff, plant-cultivation@1. It rebuilds ex16's cultivation code through a factory and adds inspection of a unit and a site, 1-energy planting rounds and a progress check. Gate G2 failed again. Survival behavior fell to 27.3% (131 of 480 actor-rounds; rest 85; 9.6% without rest), which passes, but Lin and Wren made no symbolic action; Moss told 7 times, Sol told 4 times and gave 1 name, Kai told 7 times and Ivo 4. The rest fact took hold. All six restated it, in 23 notes or preferences. Every rest step was conditioned on energy, so only 26 of 85 rests were idle by the step criterion (31%, not a default), although 56 of 85 still began at 75% of the cap or more. Waiting replaced rest as the idle filler: 159 waits, 147 outside study (ex16: 57). Rest plus wait came to 244 actor-rounds, against 243 in ex16, and Lin spent 15 more rounds navigating to the place where it already stood. Socially the run formed a Moss-Sol pair. Moss opened with a private message to Sol instead of an open greeting. Sol named the root patch 'Earth's Quiet Gold', the only name in ex15-ex17, and Moss adopted the name after seeing its sign. Kai's proposal of 'a small shared food plot' drew both of them. The planting rule made a shared plot impossible near the clearing: site inspections refused Moss (5 root plants within 5 m) and Kai (7 berry plants), and everyone dispersed to plant. Ivo, Kai, Wren and Lin each planted one unit 8-9 units from the start. Moss and Sol found only wet ground to the east and came back together. No plot sprouted by tick 80. Wren again worked alone in the north, within earshot of anyone in only 25 rounds, and did not answer Ivo's 'Hello, Wren'. Lin's silence was different. It lived next to Ivo and heard 12 utterances, including 'Hello, Lin' twice. After the first greeting it requested the tell contract in three query calls, and it never spoke. No norm statement or belief was detected, and three people wrote 8 views. Ticks below are replay event ticks; a call recorded at tick T decides the actions that start at tick T+1.

Its declaration records that ex16's cultivate-plants@1 code was refactored into a shared factory, with the ex16 serialized package byte-identical, so the reuse is source code, not knowledge given to any person. Information crossed between people through 22 tells (60 heard events), two delivered messages from Moss to Sol, one name sign that Moss saw in its observation (ordinary-47 and ordinary-48 list sign-14-sol-158), and the public registry. Nothing was given or exchanged. Private cares and views stayed owner-only: Lin's, Wren's and Ivo's packets carry empty view lists, and the analyzer reads cares and views only in its owner-only section. The ex16 archive is not an input to any ex17 person.

## Individuality

Four of the six cares name the person's leaning: Lin 'freedom to explore', Sol 'beauty', Kai 'treating neighbors fairly', Ivo 'living calmly'. Moss's cares ('finding companionship', 'a place where people can feel welcome') and Wren's ('genuinely skilled') paraphrase theirs, and Wren's first intention names 'mastery'. Views fell to 8 writes by three people (Moss, Sol and Kai), each about the other two; Lin, Wren and Ivo held none (ex16: 11 views by five people). Behavior again followed the leanings in kind. Moss sought company through messages, Sol named for colour, Kai proposed a shared plot and reported negative results, Wren pursued cultivation alone, Ivo kept a calm routine and greeted others, and Lin pursued 'food independence' and kept its own commitments. The two runs differ in who formed ties. ex16 had a five-person conversation about a camp; ex17 had a pair plus Kai, a talkative Ivo with a silent neighbor, and Wren apart.

## Behavior

Survival behavior took 131 of 480 actor-rounds (27.3%): rest 85, eat 27 and gather 19; without rest the share is 9.6%. Rest was not a default by the step criterion (26 of 85 idle; no rest step lacked an energy condition), but it was by start energy (56 of 85 began at 75% of the cap or more). Waiting became the idle filler: 159 waits, 147 of them outside study. By person: Ivo 36, Moss 29, Sol 29, Kai 23, Wren 22 and Lin 20. The waits were usually bounded by a tick or by satiety, for example 'wait repeat 14 until tick >= 77'. Rest plus wait totals 244 actor-rounds (ex16: 243). Symbolic action was 22 tells and one name; Moss's two messages to Sol fall outside G2's set. Twelve of the 22 tells open with a listener's name (ex16: 8 of 28), and seven of those passed between Moss and Sol. The one request was developed. All six mastered plant-cultivation, and Moss also learned probe. The new inspection mode checked 10 sites before any cost and refused 7: 2 by the density rule, 2 on wet bank and 3 for occupancy or overlap. Four people planted one unit each (Ivo at tick 26, Kai at 40, Wren at 43, Lin at 57), at 8.0-9.1 units from the people's starting centroid. No plot sprouted by tick 80; the checks came after 21-23 rounds, at light 0. Plans: 55 accepted, a median gap of 6 rounds, 29% single-execution and no interrupts. No norm statement, belief or non-transactional utterance was detected. All six kept health 100.

## Causes

These are case explanations from one sample per condition. (1) The rest fact was read and applied. It was restated 23 times and every rest step was conditioned on energy, so idle rest fell from 115 to 26 and rest from 186 to 85. The freed time went to waiting (non-study waits rose from 38 to 147) and, for Lin, to stationary navigation. People did not do more survival work or other work: survival without rest fell from 11.9% to 9.6%, and rest plus wait stayed level (243, then 244). The survival half of G2 passed because idle time was relabeled. (2) No output mentions choosing with whom to live. The run had more pairwise contact than ex16 (two messages where ex16 had none, more tells opening with a name) but fewer views and fewer speakers. One sample per condition cannot attribute either change to the new clause. Every person's first plan already differed from ex16 in wording, and two differed in action: Moss messaged Sol instead of greeting everyone, and Ivo spoke at tick 1. By tick 10 Lin had heard 3 utterances, none an open invitation, where in ex16 it had heard 5, including 'Does anyone want company'. (3) The planting rule again pulled people out of the clearing. In an offline count over initial.json, no meadow cell within 5 units of the starting centroid (14.0, 15.0) admits a berry or root planting, so Kai's shared plot could not be near the camp. Moss and Kai were refused there by density, and all planting happened 8-9 units out. Moss, Sol and Kai came back together afterwards; Lin, Ivo and Wren did not. (4) The developed package differed. The World Agent's ex17 specification asked for unit and site inspection, which let people check sites without spending a unit. As a result 4 of 6 planted and no planting was refused, against 1 of 5 people with four refusals in ex16. This follows from a different specification, not from the wording. (5) Wren repeated its ex16 route under the same rule. Lin's case shows that proximity, being greeted by name and reading the tell contract were not sufficient for speech, and the outputs give no reason.

## Limitations

One seed and one sample per condition make ex16 and ex17 a case comparison. The adjustment has two clauses (with whom, and the rest fact), and they cannot be separated. The developed packages differ between the runs, and model sampling made every first plan different, so differences after tick 0 have several sources. G2's symbolic half counts name, mark, tell and invite but not message, so Moss's two messages do not count. Its survival half counts rest but not wait or stationary navigation. The vocabulary analyzer reports no transmitted term, although Moss saw Sol's name sign before using the name. The eligibility count near the camp is this reviewer's offline approximation from initial.json at cell centres, not an archived measurement. The plots needed more than 80 rounds to show any outcome. Developer usage is not metered, and silence and idleness have no recorded reasons.

## Proposed improvements

1. Report an idle-invariant activity measure next to G2's survival share. Count as idle every rest that begins within one nominal rest of the cap or comes from a plan step with no energy condition, every wait outside study, and every navigation round that does not move; then compute the survival share over the remaining actor-rounds. Between ex16 and ex17 the raw survival share fell from 50.6% to 27.3% and the gate's survival half flipped from fail to pass. Over the same runs rest plus wait stayed level (243 and 244 actor-rounds), survival without rest moved from 11.9% to 9.6%, and Lin added 15 stationary navigation rounds. The wording changed which idle interface plans named, not how people spent their time. Verification: Compute the measure for ex15, ex16 and ex17 from retained files without a model call. The ex16-ex17 difference should be small, compared with the 23-point change in the raw share, and every pinned ex7-ex16 culture metric should stay unchanged.
2. Add an exposure line to pnpm metrics:culture for each person without a symbolic action: utterances heard, utterances that name them, query reads of the name, mark, tell and invite contracts, and rounds within the hearing radius of another living person. G2's symbolic half failed on Lin and Wren, but for different reasons. Lin heard 12 utterances, 2 of them naming it, requested the tell contract 4 times and was within 5 units of someone in all 80 rounds. Wren heard 7, 1 naming it, made no reads and was within earshot in 25 rounds. A wording change aimed at exposure cannot help a person who is already exposed. Verification: On ex17 the line reports Lin 12 / 2 / 4 / 80 and Wren 7 / 1 / 0 / 25; on ex16 it reports Wren 6 / 0 / 0 / 45. Both match the counts in the companion analyses.
3. Let the vocabulary analyzer count a name or mark sign in a person's observation as exposure to its text, as the sharing analysis already does for signs within the vision radius. Sol named the root patch at tick 14, Moss's observation listed sign-14-sol-158 at tick 17 (ordinary-47 and ordinary-48), and Moss used 'Earth's Quiet Gold' from tick 18. The metrics nevertheless report transmittedTerms 0 and no exposed later user, so the run's one transmitted term looks independent. Verification: On ex17 'Earth's Quiet Gold' lists Moss as an exposed later user and transmittedTerms becomes 1, and the pinned ex7-ex16 vocabulary values stay unchanged.
4. Publish an offline eligibility map of the cultivation site rule for the culture genesis, and report each planting site's distance from the group's centroid and each refusal reason in the culture metrics. No meadow cell within 5 units of the starting centroid admits a berry or root planting, so every cultivation attempt in ex16 and ex17 that succeeded was 8-9.4 units from the start. In ex17 that turned Kai's shared plot into four separate plots and two failed searches. Interpretations of dispersal and isolation should not credit the prompt with what the rule does. Changing the spacing of planted plots would be a world-rule decision for the user, not part of this analysis. Verification: The map, computed from initial.json, reproduces the refusals recorded in both runs (four in ex16; two density refusals among seven site refusals in ex17), and the reported distances match the replayed plot positions.
5. Before attributing a G2 change to a wording adjustment, run at least two samples per condition from the same genesis, or state in the plan that the rerun is a single case and compare tick-0 outputs explicitly. Every first plan in ex17 already differed from ex16, two of them in action (Moss's private message, Ivo's tick-1 introduction), and the World Agent's specification produced a different cultivation package. One sample cannot separate the wording from sampling and downstream divergence. ex16 and ex17 together used 1,954,336 ledger tokens, about 12% of the program's 16,000,000 limit. Verification: Across replicates of one condition, report the spread of the survival share, the idle-invariant share and the symbolic counts per person, and compare it with the ex16-ex17 difference before calling that difference an effect.
