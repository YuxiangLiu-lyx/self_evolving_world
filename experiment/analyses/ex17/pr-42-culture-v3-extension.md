# ex17: Behavioral Analysis

Reviewer: Current Claude Code conversation; behavioral analysis, not independent approval. Task: pr-42-culture-v3-extension. Recommendations are analysis only; no world, prompt or agent change is authorized by this report.

## Summary

This analysis covers the whole ex17 archive (the culture v3 rerun, tag long-culture-v3, scope long-ex17; six people on the ex13 rules, seed 20261123 and world, with the open-2 goal wording) through round 250, and focuses on what changed after round 80. The earlier analysis pr-39-culture-v3-rerun covers rounds 0-79 and stays as it is. Round convention, as in that analysis: ticks are replay event ticks, and a decision at round r (a call recorded at tick r) settles in events stamped tick r + 1. The first window is ticks 1-80 (decisions 0-79) and the extension is ticks 81-250 (decisions 80-249). Handoff rounds are action rounds, as in the development records, so Wren's round-156 and Sol's round-161 requests are the events stamped ticks 157 and 162. After round 80 the run continued on its own source branch from the round-80 checkpoint commit b643e48 (plan pr-42-culture-v2-extension, R1a), with checkpoints at 150 and 250. All six stayed alive at health 100 in every round; minimum satiety ranged from 39.9 (Kai) to 63.7 (Ivo). All 285 calls (276 ordinary, 9 World Agent) were accepted, with no transport failure, rejection or unknown usage. They charged 2,304,010 ledger tokens (2,182,394 in, 121,616 out), 1,536 per person per round. The extension's 175 calls (ticks 80-249: 169 ordinary, 6 World Agent) charged 1,510,957 tokens, 1,481 per person per round, below the first window's 1,652. The largest input estimate was 12,435 tokens (Wren, ordinary-215, tick 198). Three handoffs were counted, two of them after round 80: Wren's hearth-food-preparation@1 (round 156) and Sol's mechanical-food-handling@1 (round 161). The revised gate G2 passes at rounds 120, 150 and 250. The original gate fails at 120 and 150 on its every-person condition and passes at 250 (survival share 24.0%), because Lin spoke at ticks 128, 180 and 240 and Wren at tick 183. Behaviorally the extension was mostly waiting together. Idle time (rest and wait) rose from 50.8% of actor-rounds in ticks 1-80 to 81.2% in ticks 81-150, then fell to 71.5% in 151-250 (67.6% of the run: rest 199, wait 815). Every wait step written after round 80 waits for satiety to fall to a meal threshold. 668 of the 770 idle extension rounds were spent within 5 units of another person, and the other 102 were all Wren's. Lin, Moss, Sol, Kai and Ivo formed one cluster, each within 5 units of someone in all 170 extension rounds. Kai sat at one spot for 145 rounds and Moss for 133. Wren stayed at its northern root plot and was within earshot of anyone only in ticks 157-189. Speech dipped, then returned. Ticks 81-150 had 8 tells, and 5 of them were one plan step of Ivo's repeating 'Hello, Lin. My small root planting still has no visible sprout' every 15 rounds, even after the plot had sprouted at tick 99. Ticks 151-250 had 13, and 8 of those fell in ticks 201-226, after the season change re-decided all six. In that stretch Kai asked 'Has anyone found an interesting question to explore together?' (tick 203). Sol answered 'perhaps our shared question can be what catches each of our eyes here' (209), and Kai and Moss answered back (211). Lin joined at tick 240 with 'The berry red and the roots' quiet gold both catch my eye'. The name 'Earth's Quiet Gold' was not spoken after tick 65, but its colour phrase ('muted gold', 'quiet gold') was spoken six times after round 80, by Sol, Moss and Lin. The 'we needn't make every moment into work' saying was never spoken again; it survived only in the private plan texts of the five clustered people. No name, mark, message, invitation, gift, exchange or work was made after round 80. The two late packages were used unevenly. Nobody prepared a hearth, tried friction ignition or roasted food. Wren mastered the hearth at tick 159, in rain of 0.849 against an ignition limit of 0.15 and without a hardwood spindle. The rain fell below the limit only around tick 232, and Lin, who mastered the hearth at tick 243, studied it 'without committing to a fire'. Five people mastered mechanical-food-handling and used it in 18 rounds: 13 examinations, 4 openings and 1 refused reference. The only shell cracked was Wren's hazelnut, which split open under one stone strike on a rock (tick 181). Lin, Kai and Moss opened a berry, a berry and a root by hand. Nobody ate a hazelnut; Kai and Moss ate their opened units at the usual nutrition. The cultivation plots of the first window resolved. Kai's and Lin's berry plots rotted (ticks 96 and 99), and Ivo's and Wren's root plots sprouted (99, 102), matured (176, 178) and each gave one root at tick 197. After round 80 only Lin reported a plot outcome, while Ivo's repeated tell kept describing its plot as unsprouted.

## Limitations

One seed and one sample per condition make every ex16-ex17 difference a case. The extension ran on a separate source branch from b643e48. This analysis did not run the R1b replay on main's code and relies on the archive's own replay and integrity records. The term, phrase, sharing, rule and saying detectors are rule-based English matches. Two of the ten adopted sayings at round 250 restate the handling interface's own output, and all five post-80 rule adoptions match private texts to private texts. The revised G2's adoption condition after round 80 therefore rests mainly on the root-colour and 'what catches your eye' sayings, and at every cut it would still pass on the first window's name. The analyzer counts mechanical-food-handling as survival, and it counts Ivo's six repeated tells as six symbolic actions. Lin's lower-case 'quiet gold' is not a vocabulary term use. The 'work' saying's private spread is this reviewer's reading, and Lin's version is not evidence of transmission. Positions are carried forward from each person's last positioned event, and hearing is approximated by the 5-unit vision radius. The rain values come from call packets and are sampled only at decision ticks. The hearth was never used, so its physical model is untested in this world. The window ends mid-routine: Kai's and Moss's last plans were written to run until ticks 400 and 350, Lin holds an unused hearth skill, and Lin, Sol and Wren each carry an uneaten hazelnut. Developer and review model usage for the two extension handoffs is not metered. Outputs record plans, notes and memory, not reasons, so silence, idleness and the unreported harvests have no recorded cause.
