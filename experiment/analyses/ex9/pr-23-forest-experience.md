## Summary

One real gpt-6.1-sol run completed 1200/1200 rounds with three living actors, fixed seed 20261121 and the original 650/60/20 budgets. It spans 1.5 seasonal cycles. Five functionally new capabilities were implemented after autonomous requests: portable insulation, local fire, willow processing, rain canopy and recuperation; three were actually used, with all three actors reusing insulation, processing and recuperation. Fire and canopy remained unused. A separate actual operation-continuity repair is not new capability discovery. There were 404  ordinary attempts (4 failed),7 world attempts (1 failed), and 13 counted current-conversation handoffs (3 failed). The original policy was not tuned after inference began; all failures, four context pauses and source boundaries are retained. No model weights were trained.

## Causes

The call text ties portable insulation to observed exercise cost and cooling, willow processing to missing materials, rain interception to measured wet-mantle protection, and recuperation to retained health deficits. Those temporal chains support agent-reported motivations and actual use, not isolated causal effects of hunger, mood, seasonality or prompting. The shelter was used while warm, explaining zero measured cold benefit; approved fire and canopy were learned but never assembled, so availability did not ensure adoption. Generic short plans and repeated immediate maintenance plausibly competed with complex projects, but this interpretation was not experimentally isolated. Existing terrain, dropping load, maintaining one item and gradual recovery were effective simpler adaptations. Ex8 stopped at 897 rounds with 400  ordinary attempts (0.1486 per actor-round); its different seed, rules, baseline catalogue, budget and window prevent a causal comparison with ex9.

## Limitations

The strongest current benefits are measured insulation components, actual precursor consumption in persistent equipment and retained health. Component exposure totals are not total causal temperature or labor savings, and no matched counterfactual establishes a reduction in recurring subsistence work. Four context pauses at 451,478,689,974 required reversible transport changes under the unchanged cap; exact expansion preserves facts but can change model interpretation, and further growth can still pause. The actual495 continuation defect, failed handoffs and inference failures remain part of this observation. Recorded ordinary tokens are6928131 input/161305 output across known-usage attempts;4  ordinary attempts have unknown usage. Recorded world tokens are81707/11498. Internal developer-model calls/tokens, preparation/review and monetary charges are unmetered, not zero. This is one exploratory fixed-seed run, not seed selection, a model-training claim or a mechanism-level causal estimate.
