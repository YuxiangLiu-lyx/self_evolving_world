## Summary

This analysis covers experiment C, archive ex25: the import-with-newcomers arm of the culture v2 program (scenario culture-v2-import, scope long-ex25 inheriting the installed packages of long-ex18, incentives off, model gpt-6.1-sol, seed 20261123). ex18's world was imported at round 120; the source world hash, its exact replay, the recorded round hash and the stage checkpoint all equal d21459c66264e4645651e4f6f0287070e18c125261eb522dcf77bdada6f42e2d (manifest importedFrom), and ex18 is unchanged. The six residents (Lin, Moss, Sol, Kai, Wren, Ivo) kept their ex18 memory: cares, views, six heard entries and six remembered entries each in their round-120 input. Two newcomers were added without memory and with the median temperament 0.525/0.5/0.475/0.5: Tova (leaning curiosity) at (9.23, 19.77) with nobody in sight, and Bram (leaning calm) at (18.6, 10.4) with Wren and Ivo in sight. The run reached round 220 of 220 (phase window-complete; 'Observation window reached 220; the world is resumable, not terminal'). It made 233 calls, 210 ordinary and 23 World Agent, with 3 rejected ordinary outputs (Moss ordinary-230, Bram ordinary-194 and -225), no transport failure and no technical pause. The ledger charged 2,137,045 of 2,500,000 tokens (ordinary 1,897,595 input and 82,945 output; World Agent 143,309 and 13,196). Five of 12 development handoffs were counted, each a simplified but real package scoped long-ex25: vocal-music@1 for Kai (round 120), position-loose-material@1 for Sol (128), friction-heating-trial@1 for Wren (174), mechanical-food-separation@1 for Lin (194) and heat-exposure-trial@1 for Moss (197). Three further requests were reviewed as reuse (Moss's music request as vocal-music, world-32; Kai's shade request as probe, world-136; Bram's food-safety request as unheated-food-preparation, world-229). All eight people were alive at round 220 with health 100 and no deaths; final satiety ranged from 62 (Bram) to 82 (Moss, Sol). The season changed from spring to summer at round 200. Conventions: a call at tick T acts in replay rounds T+1 and later; replay pointers index rounds of replay/execution-000120-000220.json from 0, so rounds/N is round N+121; 'within 5' uses end-of-round positions from wellbeing events. The most notable behavior concerns the newcomers and the ex18 culture. Nobody spoke the name 'Berry Lantern' in rounds 120-220; the newcomers saw it only on Sol's ex18 sign (Bram from tick 133, Tova from tick 156) and neither used it. The lantern tale continued among the residents, first from carried memory (Moss round 121, Lin round 124), then as a new traveler, cup and keeper strand opened by Kai at round 177. Bram, a newcomer, continued that strand at round 190 ('beside the lantern, the traveler found a bench'), and Moss answered it aloud and wrote the only resident view of a newcomer. Tova heard four resident tale episodes, but none reached its input: it made no call between ticks 170 and 194, and the input keeps only the 6 most recent heard entries; the first lantern text in its input was Bram's sequel. No newcomer adopted a resident norm verbatim. All music was humming; nobody sang words. Verified facts were taken from validation-results/handoffs/c-facts.md and c-deviations.md and from the label verification files docs/presentation/labels/ex18-1-120 and ex25-121-220 (blind GPT labels checked by a Claude agent, not a human; ex25 agreement 93.9%, 31 of 33 with 2 partials), and rechecked against the calls and replay cited here. A premise correction: the norm phrase 'quiet company, no need to talk' named in the plan does not occur in ex18; it comes from ex16. ex18 has 'without obligation', 'without pressure', 'no obligation to join' and 'There's room for your silence too'. Recommendations are analysis only.

Lin's freedom leaning appeared as a chain of small food questions answered by its own trials or by asking others. Lin was the only resident to ask a newcomer for knowledge, and it treated Bram's answer as testimony, not as its own finding. It never addressed Tova. Its two map sequels kept the tale going within Bram's hearing, and the round-186 one became the direct source of Bram's continuation. Lin's own repetition of its 181 text suggests it was varying its own story more than answering anyone in particular.

## Causes

These are case explanations from one run, with facts kept apart from interpretation. Facts: (a) Tova made no call between ticks 170 and 194; each packet keeps 6 heard entries, and every resident episode Tova heard (rounds 177-189) had rolled out by 194. (b) Tova started out of sight of everyone and spent rounds 126-148 at the western apple tree, 7.6 from the sign; its first resident contact within 5 came at 154. (c) Bram started with two residents in sight, settled 3.39 from the sign, and was within 5 of Ivo, Moss and Lin for 94-100 rounds. (d) At tick 188 Bram's input held three sequels and Moss's invitation to share a story. (e) The name was never spoken in ex25; it existed only on the sign, attributed to Sol. (f) No resident told a tale episode to a newcomer by name; the only tale-related line naming a newcomer is Moss's acknowledgement of Bram's story at 196. (g) Trees provide no modeled shade, and the only enclosures (cave-1, cave-2) were 15-22 units away. (h) Rain in Wren's inputs was 0.96 at tick 190 and 0.62 at tick 213, above the 0.15 ember limit throughout. Explanations: (1) Bram's continuation is best explained by exposure. It composed a sequel in the frame of the episodes in its input; 'traveler' and 'keeper' appear in Lin's round-186 sequel. (2) Tova's lack of stories follows at least as well from (a) and (b) as from its leaning; the run cannot separate them. (3) In this run the name did not pass to the newcomers through the sign alone. One possible reason is that the sign is one record among many observations, while the tale was alive in speech; the archive cannot test this. (4) The residents did not make room for the newcomers as newcomers. Their stories were exchanges between existing partners (mostly Kai and Moss), and contact with newcomers was practical (Lin's food questions, Moss's note about Tova's plot) or reactive (Moss's answer to Bram). (5) The heat investigations followed the warming weather and the summer change at 200. Their limited results follow from (g). Wren's failure to make an ember follows from (h), not from its method. (6) Bram's 'For now, said the keeper' is coincidence or convergence within one model, not transmission.

## Limitations

One run, one seed (20261123), one model (gpt-6.1-sol) and 100 rounds. There is no control run without newcomers, with swapped start positions or leanings, or with the name spoken, so no causal estimate is possible. Tova and Bram differ in leaning and start position at once. 'Within 5' and first contacts use end-of-round positions. Heard events are not the same as input presence, and this analysis counts only what reached an ordinary input as received. The labels and their verification are model work (GPT labels, Claude-agent verification), not human annotation; ex25 agreement is 93.9% (31 of 33, 2 partial). Sung words are not label items, and none existed. Text searches for norm phrases and the name are exact and case-insensitive; paraphrases are judged by reading, not by a measure. Rejected outputs (ordinary-194, -225, -230) never ran, and planned steps that did not run by round 220 (Kai's moth story, Sol's root trial, Lin's shell opening) are not counted as actions. The four emptying food gathers were not tested against anyone's hunger. The plan's norm premise was wrong (the phrase is from ex16), so 'norm adoption' here is judged against the ex18 statements that do exist. All people share one model, so similarity of wording is weak evidence of transmission. Some quoted archive and handoff text uses gendered pronouns; this analysis refers to every person as 'it'.

Tova (curiosity) started out of sight and far from the storytelling group, while Bram (calm) started inside it.
