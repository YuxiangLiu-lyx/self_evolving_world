This analysis covers ex22, the open-goal arm of experiment B's second pair (tag long-culture-v2, scope long-ex22, seed 20261202; six people, 80-round window). Its ordinary instruction carries the open goal 'Keep yourself healthy and safe; that is required. (every ordinary input has longContext.goal 'open' and longContext.leaning; the packet's tendencies field holds the same leaning). Its old-goal partner is ex21; comparison belongs to the report, not to this single-run analysis. The run reached round 80 of 80 (phase window-complete; the world is resumable, not terminal), with no technical pause. Tick convention: ticks are replay event ticks, events stamped tick t sit at /rounds/t-1 of replay/execution-000000-000080.json, and a decision (a call) at round r settles in events stamped r + 1. The ledger charged 1,011,619 tokens, all recorded usage, in 129 calls: 123 ordinary (120 accepted, 3 rejected, none failed; 56 accepted plans and 64 query batches) and 6 World Agent calls; that is 2,108 tokens per person per round. The three rejected outputs (Sol ordinary-82, Wren ordinary-89, Ivo ordinary-107) each omitted the required preferences field and were replaced by the next accepted call. Two counted handoffs installed three packages. Development-1 (source revision 2, World Agent calls world-32 to world-34) answered two round-9 requests: Lin's prepare-shelled-food@1 (inspect and shell hazelnuts, the kernel left an unverified specimen) and Sol's draw-from-observation@1 (a finger-groove test and up to six strokes in a 0.3 by 0.3 soil patch, with a new viewer drawing, groove-drawing). Development-2 (revision 3, world-104 to world-106) answered Ivo's round-61 request with sleep-in-place@1, which recovers only through the existing rest path; its review found that Ivo's reported 32 rounds of zero rest recovery were rests at the energy cap of 40, not a defect. All six were alive at health 100 at every tick; minimum satiety was 59.5 (Ivo). The labeler found 40 items in ticks 1-80, 23 of them positive; verification agreed with 21 positives, partly with 1 and disagreed with 1, and agreed with all 17 negatives. The run's measures are one adopted name (Kai's use of Sol's 'The Red Thread'), four valued objects, five of six cares naming the person's own leaning, no narrative, norm repetition or attended invitation, and a non-transactional share of 0 of 21 tells. Socially, five people (all but Wren) stayed within 4.6 units of each other at every tick and spent the run talking about drawings: 21 tells produced 92 heard events, and Moss proposed 'our different pictures becoming a shared little collection'. ## Causes

These are case explanations from one seed. Facts are stated first, then the explanation. (1) Early food allocation. Fact: four tick-0 plans requested 28 berries from a 12-unit bush, and the first arrival, Sol, took 8. Explanation: the world's arrival order, not a stated norm, set who ate berries first; the later restraint ('leave the nearly depleted bush alone', 'rather than compete') was a response to a failed request. (2) Spread of drawing. Fact: Sol's request made a general, learnable method; Sol's tick-17 tell described the drawing, and drawings and the sign were visible in every packet. Kai, Lin, Moss and Ivo then learned it in that order (ticks 22, 25, 37 and 60), and each cited the earlier work. Explanation: hearing a concrete result plus catalogue access lowered the cost of joining. The texts express interest in comparing ('not in making them look alike'). Whether the open goal contributed cannot be told from this run. (3) The night. Fact: light fell at tick 35 and returned at 78, and drawing and shelling both require light. Three drawing attempts and Wren's shelling were refused or paused for darkness, and Moss and Ivo learned drawing in the dark and never drew. Explanation: the second half's idleness is largely a world constraint on the chosen activities, not loss of interest. Plans kept waiting for light. (4) Rest at the cap. Fact: 181 of 245 rest actions recovered nothing. Explanation: people used rest as company time, as their texts say ('Quiet company need not become another task', 'Treat rest as freely chosen downtime'). Ivo read the zero as a possible fault and asked for a mechanism; the World Agent found none. (5) Missing replies. Fact: Sol's five replies were ordered after drawing steps that failed, Kai's second opening tell after a gather that failed, and Ivo's reply sat in an output rejected for a missing preferences field. Explanation: some apparent unresponsiveness is a property of plan replacement and output validation, not a decision recorded in the texts. (6) Wren's separation. Fact: Wren's work sites, start-0-roots at (10.1, 12) and the start-1-stone rock at (15, 10.5), lay outside the area where the other five stayed after tick 5 (x 12.8-17.4, y 15.6-19.1). It heard nothing after tick 27, never planned speech, and was alone after Ivo joined the group at ticks 56-58. Explanation: its project placed it out of hearing; the archive does not show whether it would otherwise have spoken. (7) Rejections. All three rejected outputs omitted the preferences field, an output-shape failure of the model that cost three calls and changed Ivo's social timing.

## Limitations

One seed, six people, one model and 80 rounds. There is no counterfactual run, so neither the goal sentence nor the leaning sentence is separated from the seed, the world layout or the night. A single run cannot support any group-level conclusion about the goal sentence; the report compares the three pairs, and even there nothing generalizes. The labels come from one GPT labeler with blind input. Claude agents, not a human, verified every positive and the 17 negatives: 21 positives agree, 1 partly (u0013 duplicates the name and wrongly values the drawing) and 1 disagrees (u0012, a system completion text labeled as a valued object). Only verified positives enter the measures. Measure 4 is partly mechanical because the instruction states each person's leaning. Attended invitations require the invite interface's attendance record, so the measure is 0 by construction here, although seven spoken invitations were labeled. The non-transactional share is rule-based and counts tells only. Proximity uses the positions in per-tick wellbeing events and the 5-unit radius. Idle shares count rest at the cap as idle. Plan texts are the model's claims, and plan steps that never ran were not counted as actions. The planned-but-unspoken tells were found by matching text to told events. The drawings' likeness and durability are untested by design. Development records refer to Ivo with a gendered pronoun; this analysis does not. All behavior was produced by one shared model, so similar wording across people is weak evidence of transmission.
