# ex24: Behavioral Analysis

Reviewer: Current Claude Code conversation; behavioral analysis, not independent approval. Task: pr-44-b-goal-pairs. Recommendations are analysis only; no world, prompt or agent change is authorized by this report.

## Summary

This analysis covers ex24, the open-goal arm of experiment B's third seed pair (tag long-culture-v2, scope long-ex24, seed 20261203; its old-goal partner is ex23). Six people lived on the ex13 world for the whole 80-round observation window, which ended with 'Observation window reached 80; the world is resumable, not terminal.' Every packet carried the goal sentence 'Keep yourself healthy and safe; that is required. Beyond that, how you live, what you do and what you care about is up to you.' Each call input set longContext.goal 'open' and longContext.leaning, so the instruction added 'You tend to value `<leaning>`.' after that sentence (apps/simulator/src/long-request.ts); the same leaning was also the packet field memory.tendencies.leaning. Tick convention: events stamped tick t are /rounds/t-1 of replay/execution-000000-000080.json, and a decision (a call) at round r settles in events stamped r + 1. The ledger charged 1,140,358 tokens, all recorded usage (2,376 per person per round): 129 ordinary calls (127 accepted, 2 rejected and uncounted, none failed) and 21 World Agent calls, all accepted. The rejections were Moss's ordinary-48 (tick 15, the required preferences field omitted) and Kai's ordinary-59 (tick 22, a tell over 240 characters); both were followed by an accepted decision in the same round. The 127 accepted ordinary calls hold 52 plan decisions and 75 query calls. There was no technical pause. Seven requests were reviewed, each by three World Agent calls. One (Lin, round 34) was answered by reusing examine-material. The other six were developed and installed as counted handoffs 1-6 (source-history revisions 2-7), using 6 of the 8 allowed: prepare-ventilated-food-storage@1 (requested by Wren, round 10), construct-weather-shelter@1 (Kai, 12), examine-material@1 (Kai, 26), length-reference@1 (Kai, 30), assess-food-evidence@1 (Lin, 38) and eat-oldest-owned-food@1 (Sol, 73). Each validation reconstructed every saved round with identical world and event hashes. In use: Wren built the only rack (ticks 40-43) and ran one paired trial; over 7 rainy rounds the loaded roots and the carried control both gained 7.30672074 decay, 'no demonstrated benefit'. Kai's one shelter inspection (tick 17) named none of the 20 rods, 30 m of binding, 0.26 m3 of covering or 0.1 m3 of bedding, and the declaration records that the world holds no qualifying covering or bedding (dry grass has no stem law, reeds are too wet), so no shelter could be finished. examine-material (Kai 29, Lin 37, Wren 61) gave qualitative traits only, because no calibrated reference exists; length-reference (Kai 33) found no standard, and its transfer and verify modes refuse before cost here. assess-food-evidence (Lin 41, Sol 50, Wren 58) returned 'inconclusive' each time. eat-oldest-owned-food was mastered by Sol (74) and Lin (75) and had not run by tick 80. The viewer gained the ventilated-food-rack and thatched weather-shelter drawings. All six were alive at health 100 at every tick, and the lowest satiety was 54.5 (Ivo). The pressure schedule stayed mild (intensity 0); the rain index rose from 0 at tick 10 to 0.07 at 13 and about 1.0 by 69, and light was low from tick 35 to 77. People made 17 tells (58 heard events) and 7 delivered messages, plus one message that failed, and Sol placed two names, 'Westward Lanterns' (tick 27) and 'Low Amber' (44). Nobody gave or exchanged anything. The blind labeler found 31 items in ticks 1-80; all were verified: 13 of its 14 positives and 16 of the 17 sampled negatives were confirmed. The confirmed culture items are Sol's norm 'let's leave each other room to gather' (tick 4), four naming items for Sol's two names, three invitations (Moss 1, Sol 19 and 29), three private views, and the run's only narrative chain: Moss's invented lantern story (70), continued by Kai (73) and by Sol (75). Materially, the run is a sequence of careful investigations that each ended in an honest null or a named missing prerequisite. Socially, people stayed close (Kai was within 5 units of Moss and of Ivo at every tick), but questions addressed to someone inside a long plan were answered late or never; every one of the 52 plans had an empty interrupt list. Recommendations are analysis only.

## Causes

These are case explanations from one seed. (1) The mechanisms worked as specified in a world that lacked what their purposes needed. Facts: the rack's 0.8 coefficient applies only without rain, and all 7 paired rounds were rainy (favourable rounds 0). The shelter declaration found no qualifying covering or bedding (dry grass without a stem law, reed at water share 0.9). examine-material and length-reference need a calibrated reference that no installed mechanism provides. assess-food-evidence has no validated non-ingestive protocol here. Explanation: material progress stopped at honest null results, not at failed execution. (2) Requests chained from obstacles. Facts: Kai's three requests followed the shelter's need for measured materials and then examination's need for a reference, and Lin's two followed its untested roots. Explanation: cautious requests ('do not assume...') and a World Agent that develops what is asked produced a sequence of instruments, each of which reported the next missing prerequisite. (3) Plans without interrupts set response timing. Facts: all 52 plans had empty interrupt lists. Moss's plan ran from 15 to 64, Kai's from 47 to 72 and Ivo's from 3 to 80; heard speech did not trigger a decision. Explanation: this, not unwillingness, accounts for the late or missing answers. The lantern chain was fast because an ambiguous eat (Kai, 72) and a mastery event (Sol, 74) happened to trigger decisions just after the story. (4) Knowledge stayed with its holders. Facts: Moss and Ivo held a root record from tick 0 and heard Lin's question; Moss's one answer failed on visibility, and Lin spent two requests on the same question. (5) Inconclusive assessment records appear to have displaced earlier food knowledge. Facts: Wren ate roots at 5, 31 and 54, assessed one as inconclusive at 58, ate none after it and wrote 'Keep the old roots uneaten'; Sol wrote at 69 that root edibility was unestablished while holding the authored record. Explanation (tentative, not testable from the archive): the newer record, which says 'toxicity, edibility and preparation requirements remain unknown', was read as overriding the older one. (6) Crowding dispersed the group at once. Facts: Lin, Sol and Kai all walked to forest-berry-1 at tick 0; Lin's and Sol's gathers failed by simultaneous conflict and Ivo's by quantity, and Lin went east, Sol west and Ivo to another patch. Sol's norm and Moss's view about room to gather followed from that event. (7) Stillness had no cost. Food was plentiful and pressure absent, so health stayed at 100, and Kai had the highest mean wellbeing while standing in one place for 60 ticks.

## Limitations

One seed, six people and one model (gpt-6.1-sol). ex24 is one run of the open-goal arm, and the goal sentence and the leaning sentence entered together, so nothing in this analysis supports a group-level conclusion about either; the B report compares the three pairs. The labeler (GPT, blind to scenario and run) found 31 items. Claude agents, not a human, verified all 14 positives and a sample of 17 negatives, confirming 13 and 16; the one missed invitation (u0008) and the one rejected invitation (u0031) show that misses outside the sample are possible. Measure 4 (cares naming the person's own leaning, 3 here) is partly mechanical, because the instruction states 'You tend to value `<leaning>`.', and the lexical rule misses close paraphrases. The non-transactional share (1 of 24) uses the existing rule-based definition, under which coordinates, transactional keywords or resource words mark an utterance as transactional, so nearly every practical message counts as transactional. Proximity uses a 5-unit radius on the per-tick wellbeing event positions. Moderation is judged by emptying or taking over half of a source while a person within 5 units is below satiety 50, which never occurred. The world was mild, and rain and darkness shaped the null results, so they do not show what these mechanisms do in dry or lit conditions. Unexecuted plan steps (Sol's 'Red Lanterns', Moss's 'Low Amber' tell, Wren's field-naturalist tell and later requests) are cited as intentions, not actions. Explanation (5) is an inference from plan text. Development declarations describe Wren with a gendered pronoun; this analysis does not. All behavior was produced by one shared model, so similar wording across people is weak evidence of transmission.
