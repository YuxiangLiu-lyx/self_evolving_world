## Individuality

The six Ash members executed identical subsistence prefixes despite different assigned goal text. Other groups selected short surveys, observations, preparation and weather-protection requests. These are choices of A shared controllers, not six private model deliberations per group. A uses neutral 0.5 tendencies and shared observed group information. There is no reported trust change, disagreement, accepted public proposal or true teaching event in the seven completed rounds.

## Behavior

Of 126 settled member actions, 28 were waits, 28 navigation, 28 rest, 19 gathering, 14 eating, five observations/trials, three settled requests and one prepare-food operation. Seven additional requests at the pending round-7 barrier are not included as settled actions. World-8 approved two distinct functions; world-11 reused shelter for another member. Cedar 2 completed preparation with a persistent physical output; its planned consumption and report did not execute. Zero report/board/teaching events and zero completed shelters are observed. Five grounded project records exist, but their proposed outcomes are not benefits. Two ambiguous references caused replanning before action settlement, so the action-failure column alone undercounts execution friction.

## Causes

Three initial provider responses exceeded the inherited reader limit; one stopped in-flight invocation has unknown outcome and was never resent. The bounded collective reader was repaired under two counted development attempts, one of which failed architecture validation. A later packet exceeded the unchanged context guard; the existing lossless encoder was activated for collective shapes under the final A development slot. At round 7, two world-review calls timed out and the next necessary handoff could not fit the frozen allocation. No agent was rescued and no resource, incentive or limit was retuned. These engineering costs truncate observation; they are not evidence that shared coordination prevents development.

## Limitations

The target was 200 rounds, but only seven completed. No season transition, durable role specialization, meaningful lifetime survival, long-term savings, facility use, knowledge diffusion or intergroup interaction can be inferred. Eighteen survivors at full health primarily reflect the initial exploration margin and short duration. B begins after generic technical repairs, which confounds any numerical comparison with A. Known API tokens exclude three unknown invocations and all unmetered preparation/current-conversation development/review costs. The archival request validator was corrected to use retained source revisions; no historical request or reservation was rewritten. The observatory screenshot is a global human view, never proof that a model saw the global map.
