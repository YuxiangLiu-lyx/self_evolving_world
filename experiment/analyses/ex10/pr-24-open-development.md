Fifteen implemented interface entries include diagnostics and archival support and are not fifteen independent inventions. Navigation/rest/gather/eat/wait/pickup accounted for 76.91% of rounds; some travel served projects. Eleven messages but no item handoffs or accepted structured exchanges occurred. No formal entertainment, art, joint construction or animal management was observed. Authored ecology produced seed dispersal and plant establishment; these are not agent cultivation.

## Causes

The execution traces link the water prerequisite to completion of Lin's unchanged material specimen and link actual input material to Sol's cup and water. They do not isolate why actors favored measurements or failed to obtain broader benefits. Restrictive physical unknowns, tool dimensions, travel, detailed contexts and skill implementation cost are plausible contributing constraints. Mood effects on rest were small (recorded factors 1–1.004134). Ex9 used inherited skills and different conditions; comparison is exploratory, not causal.

## Limitations

Five accepted lossless technical repairs delayed but did not bound catalogue/memory growth; the sixth context pause could not be repaired under the original 20-attempt development cap. Ordinary attempts were 209/650 (one failed), world 15/60 (one failed), development 20/20 (one failed). Known reported tokens total 4,061,286, excluding unknown failed-fetch usage and unmetered conversation development cost. Summed ordinary/world latency is 58.52 minutes, not wall time. The prelaunch exact-baseline 1,600-round fixture passed, while a separate unchanged 10,000-round diagnostic lost a life at 3,967. Fixture success is not formal emergence; no long-horizon survival or civilization claim follows. Original archives, failed calls, paused state and source boundaries remain retained.
