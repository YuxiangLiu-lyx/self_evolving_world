Actual executed events produced 74 bounded satisfaction awards across three sources. The highest total wellbeing was 13.04616718 for Sol at round 425: physical 9.04616718 plus mental 4. ## Behavior

Real physical and experiential effects are separable. Nut consumption delivered actual higher nutrition per unit than ordinary berries: hazelnuts provide 18 and walnuts 22 versus berries 8, while all travel, gathering, study and assessment costs remained charged. Sampling is costly evidence gathering, not automatically a more efficient meal. The shelter generated 262 interior exposure measurements, 257 with positive avoided one-step cooling; it added no heat or healing. Warming performed 217 paid rounds, spending 651 energy and adding 0.35 temperature units each after ordinary physiology. All observed completed warming rounds had positive net temperature change, but energy exhaustion and cancellation occurred. These are measured local code effects, not estimates of lifetime benefit.

There were 25 nature-appreciation awards, 14 cautious-food-relief awards and 35 shared thermal-refuge awards, of which nine came from shelter conditions and 26 from warming. Active sources expire, refresh under cooldowns and do not add without bound. Across the 2,691 actor-rounds, 326 had total wellbeing above 10. Application, learning, construction completion and route diagnosis themselves awarded nothing. The run contains 811 rest events, only four with zero recovery; long rest/warming sequences usually changed real energy or temperature. It also contains 69 failed intents: 45 navigation, eleven food assessment, eight eating and five wildlife attention. Failures forced a fresh normal decision, and no invalid event-name subscription was accepted. Thus the earlier zero-feedback issue is resolved in actual execution, while repeated reference and navigation mistakes remain visible.

## Causes

Three distinct mechanisms explain several apparent stalls. First, the round-542 doorway was truly occupied: the body-radius exclusion interval covered its full usable cross-section. Sol asked Lin to move at 544, Lin moved at 545 and replied at 546, and Sol exited at 551 without a software change. Second, the exact round-619 geometry admitted a continuous corner connection missed by the half-unit navigation grid; isolated swept-collision reproduction justified development-6. The same formal run resumed with normal costs, learned diagnostics and later exit movement. Third, later failures sometimes had genuinely overlapping destinations or bounded searches: round 639 returned unknown on search exhaustion, round 693's generic cave approach point intersected the cave geometry, and round 840's selected approach overlapped a canopy. A blocked destination does not establish that every entrance or approach is unreachable.

Food-interface errors were also concrete rather than proof that food vanished. Eight eat failures supplied bare resource names instead of owned IDs or resource-prefixed aliases. Several assessments supplied resource aliases although assess-food requires an actual owned stack ID. At 762 Lin repeated an already completed assessment after the remaining portion had been consumed; at 770 Moss's repeated sampling stopped when satiety exceeded 90. The public task protocol defines repetition as completed executions, but the model sometimes used repetition as if it counted operation rounds. The current important-message rule also interrupts a sender's own plan, causing extra replanning after outgoing messages. These observations motivate interface and event refinements; they do not justify replacing the model with scripted actions or silently changing guidance mid-run.

## Limitations

No formal deaths occurred, so there is no empirical answer about post-respawn adaptation. Death causes, three-life exhaustion and cross-life learning are supported only by isolated regressions. The single exploratory run was stopped by budget at 897, before Lin's corrected tuber assessment executed; late survival and future maintenance are unknown. The final world, partial plans, accepted decisions and paused checkpoint are retained, but the original exhausted budget does not authorize further inference. There is no multi-seed comparison, causal treatment/control, proof of optimal strategy or long-term sustainability. Only one shelter was built; no maintenance, giving, animal husbandry or other unrequested mechanism emerged. The map contains unvisited regions and undiscovered properties.

Measured costs: 400 ordinary attempts (398 accepted, two failed), 4,755,601 known input tokens and 126,513 known output tokens; six world reviews, 42,775 input and 5,663 output tokens. Total known usage is 4,798,376 input and 132,176 output tokens. Ordinary-164 timed out after a partial stream and ordinary-250 failed transport; both remain budgeted, with unknown usage. Each produced the existing single fallback wait followed by a new normal decision, not a retried identical request. The existing plan-funded transport supplies no reliable per-call dollar ledger. Recommendations below are analysis only; no new policy, prompt, run or experiment is implied.

## Proposed improvements

1. Make target reference kinds explicit and consistently validated across public interfaces. Bare names, resource aliases and owned stack IDs currently differ across eating and assessment, producing real avoidable failures despite actual stock. Verification: Use isolated stale/consumed/owned/ground target cases and a separately authorized real run; preserve failed historical calls and compare attempted actions without injecting a strategy.
2. Expose operation duration separately from repetition and review which messages require fresh decisions. Completed-operation repetitions sometimes caused unintended repeated sampling or exercise; outgoing messages also discard the sender's later planned steps. Verification: Test received versus sent messages and multi-round execution boundaries, then measure decision counts and resource costs in a new retained observation.
3. Investigate feasible object approach points and bounded routing near mixed walls, trees and actor bodies. The corner defect was repaired, but chosen destinations can still overlap geometry and bounded search can remain unknown. Verification: Retain exact 542, 619, 639, 693 and 840 layouts as different cases; verify swept collision, no hidden knowledge, normal costs and explicit search uncertainty.
4. Evaluate longer survival and cross-life adaptation only with a separately authorized budget and retained checkpoints or new registered runs. The current cutoff leaves Lin hungry and provides no formal death or respawn observation. Verification: Record the exact continuation protocol and cost authority, preserve all existing artifacts, and report every death and next normal decision without supplying a standard solution.
5. Use preregistered controls or multiple retained seeds before attributing survival or happiness differences to individual mechanisms. Local temperature and nutrition effects are measured, but the single evolving run combines seasonal changes, learning, social interaction and software repairs. Verification: Compare complete time/energy/material costs, failures and censored endings; never discard unfavorable seeds or count fixtures as autonomous behavior.
