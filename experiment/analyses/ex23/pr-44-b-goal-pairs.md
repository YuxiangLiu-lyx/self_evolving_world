## Causes

These are case explanations from one seed. (1) No need for sharing arose. Food stayed plentiful (commons patches of 15-20 units, nobody below 59.8 satiety), so moderation cost nothing. The two emptying gathers at tick 4 executed plans written at tick 0, before Sol's harvest changed the stock, and Kai's plan explicitly intended to leave some. The archive shows the plan text and the stock, not the intent at execution, so this reading is an explanation. (2) The packages bounded what could follow. The casing package marks every product 'edibility, nutrition, toxins, safety, preparation are not established', and every opener wrote that it would not eat the kernel. No package or authored knowledge offered a test of kernel safety, so the casing chain ended at three opened samples. (3) Watering went unused for measured reasons. Lin's plant was already at 0.65 against 0.6. The declaration states that every water-limited plant stood at least 12.7 m from water, while hand water loses 40% per round plus 20% per metre walked. The narrowed scope, which allowed no vessel making, left no carrier that could change this. (4) Propagation stalled on the world's light, not on effort. All three patches were sown in the dark interval, and the remembered and final values (0 at 68, 0.142 at 78, 0.475 at 80) are consistent with establishment accruing only once daylight returned at tick 78. The repeated overlap refusals are consistent with the inspector standing on the inspected point, as Sol hypothesized, and the offset sowings that followed succeeded. The archive does not name the overlapping object. (5) The catalogue, not speech, carried the methods. Nine of the 13 masteries of new packages were by non-requesters, while speech carried results only among people already within 5 units. Moss's verified tool (tick 43) was never told, and Ivo and Wren learned the same method without hearing of it. (6) The single message went unanswered because of rejection recovery. Sol's reply was in ordinary-61, which was rejected for a missing preferences field, and the accepted ordinary-62 was a new plan without it. Whether Sol would have replied in a later decision cannot be known; the message stayed visible to it through tick 74.

## Limitations

This is one seed, six people and one model (gpt-6.1-sol) over 80 spring rounds, with no counterfactual run. None of these behaviors is therefore attributed to the goal sentence, the absent leaning sentence, the packages or the seed. A single run cannot support any group-level conclusion about the goal sentence; the experiment report makes the comparison across the three pairs. The labeling found no positive item, and its 9-of-9 agreement covers negatives only. A labeler that missed rare culture items would also produce this result here, although the verifiers checked every item. The culture measures are rule-based: the cares match counts the word 'beautiful' and is partly mechanical. Proximity uses a 5-unit radius on wellbeing-event positions at each tick. The counts of idle rounds are rest or wait actions per round, not the culture metrics' idle share. Establishment is read from the round-80 checkpoint and remembered object records. No patch could root within the window, so propagation outcomes are unobserved. The permission pause and the time limit of handoff 1 come from process notes kept outside the archive, not from archive files. The five rejections changed the decision timing for Sol, Ivo (twice), Moss and Wren; the lost reply in ordinary-61 is the only content effect found. Development records describe Lin with a gendered pronoun; this analysis does not. One model produced all the text, so similar wording across people is weak evidence of transmission.
