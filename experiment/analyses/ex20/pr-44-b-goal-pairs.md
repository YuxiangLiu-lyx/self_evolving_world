# ex20: Behavioral Analysis

Reviewer: Current Claude Code conversation; behavioral analysis, not independent approval. Task: pr-44-b-goal-pairs. Recommendations are analysis only; no world, prompt or agent change is authorized by this report.

## Summary

This analysis covers ex20, the open-goal arm of experiment B's first goal pair (tag long-culture-v2, scope long-ex20, seed 20261201; ex19 is the old-goal run with the same seed). Six people lived through the whole 80-round window, and the world stopped at round 80 with 'Observation window reached 80; the world is resumable, not terminal.' Every packet carried the goal 'Keep yourself healthy and safe; that is required. Beyond that, how you live, what you do and what you care about is up to you.' Each call input's longContext carries goal 'open' and the person's leaning, from which the instructions add 'You tend to value `<leaning>`.' after the goal sentence (apps/simulator/src/long-request.ts). The leaning is also in packet.memory.tendencies. Tick convention: ticks are replay event ticks; events at tick t are /rounds/t-1 of replay/execution-000000-000080.json, and a call at tick r settles in events at tick r + 1. The ledger charged 1,017,600 tokens of the 1,500,000 budget, 2,120 per person per round: 974,876 recorded usage (ordinary 891,461 input and 55,581 output, World Agent 24,235 and 3,599) plus 42,724 tokens reserved for Sol's ordinary-55, which failed at tick 23 with HTTP 503 and has no usage. There were 129 calls: 123 ordinary (119 accepted, of which 59 made plan decisions and 60 were query rounds; 3 rejected; 1 failed) and 6 World Agent. Both technical pauses are at round 23: round-23 for the transport failure (repaired after 60 s, recovery 1 of 6) and round-23-2 for Sol's ordinary-58, rejected because its tell had 242 characters. Kai's ordinary-71 (tick 28) and ordinary-79 (33) were rejected because they omitted the preferences field and were replaced in the same round. Two handoffs were counted, of a budget of 8. Handoff 1 (round 8, world-26 to -28, source revision 2) installed apply-marking-material@1 for Sol's request to test whether berry juice leaves a visible branching drawing: a probe-press test, then up to three strokes of at most 0.3 total length within 0.5 hand reach, with the debit conserved as deposit, residue and waste, durability always reported as untested and no reward. It also added a viewer drawing, applied-mark, that shows only real deposits. Sol, Moss, Kai and Lin each made one test and one three-stroke branching mark with it (64 successful events), and eight attempts were refused before any charge. Handoff 2 (round 12, world-41 to -43, revision 3) installed open-and-separate-sample@1 for Lin's request to open a hazelnut. It offers hand or supported-tool compression with a light requirement, and it implemented a kernel 0.4 / shell 0.6 composition because the specification required missing composition to be implemented. A bare hand applies about 0.29 against a hazelnut strength of 0.4, and a fractional kernel cannot be eaten with eat, which takes whole units. In use, Lin (tick 14) and Wren (26) each made one bare-hand squeeze that left the nut intact, and both cancelled. The supported-tool mode never ran: Lin's tool was a 0.9-unit tested stone sample (refused at tick 39), and Wren's two attempts fell in the dark (ticks 40 and 63; local conditions were low light from tick 35 until daylight at 78). No hazelnut was opened, so the composition and separation were never exercised. All six were alive at health 100 at every tick, and the lowest satiety was 57.1 (Lin). Nobody gave, exchanged, dropped or picked up anything. The run's labels hold 37 items: 17 model positives (13 agreed, 1 partial, 3 disagreed, all three Sol's statements labeled valued-object) and 20 negatives (all agreed). The verified items give one name (Sol's 'The Redberry Pause', tick 3, used by nobody else), Kai's norm R1 (tick 18) repeated three times, one attended invitation (Moss's sign-12-moss-136, with Lin, Moss and Sol present), two valued objects (Sol's berry mark mark-11-sol-117 and Moss's root mark mark-26-moss-335), five of six cares naming their own leaning, a non-transactional share of 0 among 21 messages, and no narratives. The most notable behavior was a small making circle. Moss invited everyone to 'a small neighborly pause' at its own position and stayed there from tick 2 to 80. Sol, Moss, Kai and later Lin each drew a three-armed berry or root mark, and Sol and Moss repeated Kai's voluntary-participation norm ('with everyone free to contribute or simply watch'). Lin and Wren worked on hazelnuts at the stone outcrop to the north, and Ivo never spoke. Recommendations are analysis only.

The night ended the chain before the supported trial the package made possible. Its late turn to marking, framed as 'something of my own', and its cares revision toward 'creativity and freely chosen company' are the clearest within-run change of any person. Its northern company was Ivo, who never spoke, and Wren, who reported a matching squeeze at tick 28. Moss's message to Lin at tick 32 was refused because Lin was out of sight.

## Causes

These are case explanations from one seed. (1) A cheap, visible outcome spread by speech and the catalogue. Fact: with the marking ability a person could make a visible mark from one owned berry in four rounds and about 1.1 energy. Sol described its mark at ticks 16, 23 and 24 to the people around Moss's meeting point, and Moss (mastered 23), Kai (32) and Lin (65) then learned it from the catalogue. Explanation: hearing about Sol's mark plausibly prompted their learning, but one shared model could produce the same idea independently. (2) The common form is partly set by the interface. The ability allows at most three strokes and reports branching only where three arms meet, and Sol had asked for 'a simple branching pattern'. Four three-armed branches are therefore weak evidence of imitation, although Moss did say 'Sol, I liked your branching idea' (tick 31). (3) The hazelnut chain was stopped by constraints the mechanism stated. A bare hand cannot open a hazelnut (about 0.29 against 0.4), a tested stone sample becomes fractional, and opening needs light, which was low from tick 35 to 77 in this world's day. Lin and Wren obeyed every refusal and waited rather than retry. (4) Food was easy. Nobody fell below 57.1 satiety and the commons patches held up to 20 units, so moderation cost nothing and nobody needed a gift. (5) Hosting at a fixed place made a meeting place. Moss invited listeners to its own coordinates and never moved, and Sol and Kai settled within 5 units of it and stayed. (6) Long interrupt-free plans limited responses. Ivo heard the invitation inside a plan that ran until tick 44, and Moss made no decision after tick 32. Most speech came by tick 48, after which plans were long rests through the dark. (7) The open goal and the leaning sentence were present throughout this run, so everything above happened with them. Whether any of it depends on them is a question for the pairwise comparison with ex19, not for this run.

## Limitations

One seed, six people and one model (gpt-6.1-sol) in an 80-round window, with no counterfactual inside the run. A GPT labeler made the labels from blind input, and Claude agents, not a human, verified them. Positive agreement is 13 of 17 (0.765; lenient 0.824), and all three disagreements concern Sol's valued-object labels, so the valued-object category is the least reliable here. Measure 4 (cares naming one's own leaning) is partly mechanical, because the instructions state each person's leaning. Measure 3 is a rule-based definition and gives 0 of 21 messages. The attendance event decides presence by its own rule: Kai, who said it would join, was 4.1 units from the meeting point at round 18 and was not counted. Proximity uses the 5-unit vision radius on wellbeing-event positions. Plan decisions are separated from query rounds by the presence of an intention. The transport failure and the three rejections changed Sol's and Kai's decision timing at ticks 23, 28 and 33. Plan steps that never ran are not counted as speech, for example Kai's ordinary-77 tell and Sol's rejected ordinary-58 tell. The hazelnut composition was never exercised, so this run gives no evidence about it. All behavior comes from one shared model, so similar wording across people is weak evidence of transmission. Above all, a single run cannot support any conclusion about the goal sentence or the stated leaning. The experiment B report makes the pairwise comparison with ex19, and nothing here generalizes.
