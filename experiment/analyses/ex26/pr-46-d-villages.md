## Summary

This analysis covers the whole ex26 archive, experiment D (scenario culture-v2-villages, tag long-culture-v2-villages, scope long-ex26; culture v2 prompt and rules, incentives off, goal open, model gpt-6.1-sol, seed 20261123, a 144 x 36 map, development budget 16, hard ledger 3,000,000 tokens, rejection recovery 'uncounted' with stopAfter 3). The companion analysis pr-46-d-villages-100 covers the preserved round-100 window and stays as it is. The run completed its first window at round 100 (window-complete), was extended by 'extend ex26 200' (window-history.json), and stopped at round 162 of the 200-round target, in phase paused. The stop: at round 162 Kai's ordinary-285, -286 and -287 were rejected in a row with 'Expected a bounded executable personal plan.'. Each plan began with eat guarded by self.inventory.berry >= 1 while Kai held no food item. In packages/world-core/src/continuous-tasks.ts, v7TaskAction ends a plan whose current step's guard is false ('The actor-defined guard no longer holds.') instead of skipping the step, so the plan yields no first action, and the run layer (apps/simulator/src/continuous-run.ts) rejects such a plan. The recovery record technical-pauses/round-162/recovery.json shows the resume after two consecutive rejected outputs (consecutiveRejectedOutputs 2, stopAfter 3). The third ended the run; by design, repair refuses to resume or hand off such a stop (scripts/continuous-repair.ts), and it was not bypassed. The phase reason recorded in execution.json and results.json is the generic long-call pause sentence, the same as at the round-13 pause. Cost: 287 calls, 263 ordinary (250 accepted: 111 decisions and 139 read-only query batches; 12 rejected; 1 failed) and 24 World Agent (8 reviews, each two query batches and a decision). The ledger charged 2,105,707 tokens (70.2% of 3,000,000): 2,071,663 of known usage (ordinary 1,845,108 in and 103,448 out; World Agent 108,065 and 15,042) and 34,044 reserved for the failed ordinary-47 (Ivo, tick 13, 'fetch failed' without evidence; technical handoff development-3, empty and uncounted). That is 2,166 per person-round; the 12 rejected outputs cost 105,777. The largest ordinary input estimate was 10,766 (Lin, ordinary-272, tick 161). The rejected outputs were 8 'Expected the registered task plan shape.' that omitted the required preferences field (ordinary-33, Moss, tick 5; -58, Sol, 16; -94, Sol, 36; -163, Moss, 93; -190, Sol, 112; -241, Kai, 150; -268, Sol, 160; -275, Kai, 161), 1 tell over 240 characters (ordinary-88, Moss, 33) and the 3 at round 162. ## Causes

Case explanations from one run, with facts kept apart from interpretation. Facts: (a) initial knowledge was one food for four of the six people, and a knowledge record changed only through use (Wren's berry at round 120). Moss's round-1 message about roots did not change Lin's diet, and Kai's round-131 root plan found its root plant empty. (b) bounded-resource-search@1 visits only sources seen or remembered with ripe stock inside a radius of at most 8, and it leaves sources seen empty unvisited. The recheck and low-health variants revisit empty sources, but only inside their own radius around a chosen origin. At tick 161, Lin's input still recorded commons-berry-0-0 as empty since round 12. (c) The four uncollected berries ripened at rounds 104-106; nobody who knew berries came within 5 of them afterwards (Lin's closest 12.8-18.4; Kai 6.41 and Wren 7.4 to commons-berry-4-0). Moss, who knew only roots, came within 0.81 and 2.71 of two of them. (d) Over 162 rounds each person walked 60.2-79.8 units in total (results.json movementDistance), mostly in search loops. Net x displacement toward the middle was Sol 33.3, Ivo 32.3, Lin 7.7 (after 19.3 at round 120), Wren 1.1, Kai 0 and Moss -1.8. (e) Lin's plans from tick 113 and Kai's from tick 122 ('revisit the nearest remembered berry plant', ordinary-202) turned back toward remembered home sources. (f) The stop followed from v7TaskAction's guard rule together with the rejection policy, as described in the summary; Kai's ordinary-276 plan at tick 161 had the same eat-if-berry step, accepted only because its first step was a message. Explanations: (1) The groups did not meet because only Sol and Ivo travelled steadily toward the middle, and they started late; the people under most pressure searched near home for the one food they knew. (2) Lin's and Kai's starvation is best explained by the combination of single-food knowledge, stale memory of empty sources and searches that do not revisit remembered-empty sources outside their radius. The ripe berries in both villages show that local food existed. (3) The silence after round 100, with no speech in the West and no proximity in either group, removed the one channel that could have carried Moss's sightings of ripe berries to Lin; in the East, Kai's request for food reached Wren only at round 162. (4) The stop was a plan-form failure under starvation: Kai wrote 'eat if I have a berry' as the first step three times, received only the generic validation error twice, and the run's rule stopped it at the third. The archive cannot show whether a clearer error would have changed the fourth attempt.

## Limitations

One run, one seed (20261123), one model (gpt-6.1-sol), and 162 of 200 target rounds; the stop prevented the second window from completing. There is no control run, and the counterfactuals in the causes (other knowledge, other search tools, a clearer validation error) were not run. Changing tools in a replay would change the world, so they are untested here. Because the groups never met, R4 items 2-5 are reported as not observed. Within 5, sightings and closest approaches use end-of-round positions. 'Came within 5' is a distance, not proof of a sighting, since obstacles can block view. Heard events are not input presence. Text searches are exact word-boundary searches. The labels and their verification are model work (GPT labels, Claude-agent verification), not human annotation. The label files are outside the archive and are not hash-bound by this record. Statements about code behavior (v7TaskAction, the run layer and the repair stop) describe the source on this branch, which is not an archive artifact. The description of development-8 follows its retained decision record. The sources beside a person's start are described by layout only: nothing marks ownership. This analysis refers to every person as 'it'.
