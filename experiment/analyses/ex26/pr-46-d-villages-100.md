## Causes

Case explanations from one run, with facts kept apart from interpretation. Facts: (a) the closest starts were 111.6 apart and movement was at most 0.8 per round; (b) path lengths over rounds 1-100 were 30.4-46.2 (Kai 46.2, Lin 45.7, Ivo 40.1, Wren 36.7, Sol 35.5, Moss 30.4), mostly search loops near home, and the net x displacement toward the middle by round 100 was Lin 15.1, Sol 14.4, Ivo 9.7, Moss 8.7, Wren 8.6 and Kai 0.6; (c) bounded-resource-search, the dominant action, takes candidates only from sources the person sees or remembers inside a radius of at most 8 around a chosen origin, leaves sources seen empty unvisited, and may walk only to observed dry ground inside that radius (its catalogue text in every input); (d) no input named the middle area or contained a middle-area source id; (e) at round 100 everyone was at health 100 with satiety 38-86, and Lin had last eaten at 66 and Kai at 41; (f) the larders held 264 nutrition per group and were empty by rounds 24 and 57. Explanations: (1) The groups did not meet by round 100 because nobody had a pressing need to travel far yet, and the dominant tool searched near remembered places. Plans aimed east or west came late: Sol at tick 61 ('a little farther east'), Ivo at 91 ('west of the depleted plants') and Lin at 94 ('farther east'), each a bounded search around a nearby origin. (2) Kai's low satiety follows from (a) its berry-only knowledge, (b) the East's three bushes being emptied by round 57, and (c) its north-going searches finding no berries. (3) The difference between a name in the West and a norm in the East cannot be attributed to the groups or their leanings in one run. (4) The shared 'beyond supplies' theme is convergence within one model, not transmission.

## Limitations

One run, one seed (20261123), one model (gpt-6.1-sol), and a 100-round window that this analysis cannot extend. There is no control run with closer starts, smaller larders or swapped leanings, so no causal estimate is possible. Because the groups never met, R4 items 2-5 are reported as not observed, not as answered. Within 5, closest approaches and step sizes use end-of-round positions. Heard events are not the same as input presence. Text searches for names and phrases are exact word-boundary searches; paraphrases are judged by reading. The labels and their verification are model work (GPT labels, Claude-agent verification), not human annotation. The label files are outside the archive and are not hash-bound by this record. The 'person beside whose start a source stands' wording describes the layout only. Nothing in the world marks ownership, and no input calls those sources anyone's. All quoted archive text is verbatim; this analysis refers to every person as 'it'.
