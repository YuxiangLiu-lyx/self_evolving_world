# ex6: Development Motivation and World Coevolution

## Goal

Observe whether three autonomous actors identify daily problems, request functional capabilities, and obtain sustained or shared benefits through costed world changes over a target of 180 synchronous rounds, retaining unsuccessful outcomes.

## Protocol and outcome

Three unassigned actors share a 24 by 24 primitive world, radius-five vision, starts eight steps apart, health100, satiety80 and prelearned basic eating. Action-first starvation10 and satiety loss2 remain unchanged. Carrying consumes actual energy; base rest recovers4. New abilities, finite ecology, costed objects and delayed processing require reviewed versioned declarations. No supplies or parameters change outside that protocol.

Observed 180 of 180 target rounds. Execution phase: completed. Target of 180 rounds reached. Archive completion means the saved observation and visuals are complete; a budget pause is not completion of 180 rounds. 3/3 actors alive.
