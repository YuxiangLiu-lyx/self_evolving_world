# Highlights

Selected moments from the culture program's experiments, at most five per experiment. Each quotes the retained text, its tick (as the analyzer prints it: a decision at round _r_ settles at tick _r_ + 1) and the call that produced it, with one sentence on why it is interesting. The run reports have the context.

## Experiment A: the cold season arrives (ex16, rounds 250–400)

[Run report](run-report-a-winter.md).

1. **The only name in 400 rounds, spoken to nobody.** Tick 373, Sol (`ordinary-508`, `event-373-sol-4270`): "Sol named this place "The Little Apple Pause"." Sol had heard Kai's apple report at tick 330; the name was given alone, and no one heard or used it by tick 400.
2. **A question that a 172-round plan could not answer.** Tick 344, Moss to Ivo (`ordinary-460`, `event-344-moss-3949`): "Ivo, I've enjoyed your quiet company. My little grass mat was a satisfying thing to make. Is there something you've enjoyed lately, or something you'd like to try? No need to interrupt your meal." Ivo's only plan of the window ran from round 298 to tick 470 without interrupts, so the question went unanswered.
3. **The one response to the cooling was a probe, not a shelter.** Tick 383, Kai after entering cave-2 (`ordinary-517`): "no additional shelter effect has been established." Every input showed the pressure accumulating, yet no one mentioned cold, and the only person who sought refuge measured it and left.
4. **A failed trial that drove the next invention.** Tick 356, Wren (`ordinary-471`, `event-356-wren-4095`): "The crossing slipped at 4.3 N: the turns slid along the top piece with the bottom piece; the test stopped and the lashing stays closed." Wren then requested frapping turns and a longer cord, and at tick 400 (`ordinary-528`, `event-400-wren-4568`) "The frapped 2-turn lashing held 10 N (separate) for 60 s between both hands."
5. **The run's first private message, answered fourteen ticks later.** Tick 270, Kai to Sol (`ordinary-359`, `event-270-kai-3143`): "I'd enjoy hearing about your mat studies, with no hurry or obligation to answer." Sol replied at tick 284 (`ordinary-381`, `event-284-sol-3285`): "I enjoy our quiet company. I'll gather just two nuts and leave the rest."

## Experiment B: goal sentence pairs (ex19–ex24, rounds 1–80)

[Run report](run-report-b-goal-pairs.md). All five moments come from open-goal runs; the old-goal runs had no verified labeled event.

1. **A story three people told together.** ex24, tick 70, Moss (`ordinary-129`, `event-70-moss-784`): "A tiny invented story: a lantern tired of showing the way, so its friends sat beside it until dawn." Kai at tick 73 (`ordinary-137`, `event-73-kai-808`): "Here's an invented sequel: at dawn its friends brought no grand answers, only berries and another story." Sol at tick 75 (`ordinary-145`, `event-75-sol-827`): "An invented ending: the lantern slept while its friends watched the dawn; afterward, it shone simply because it loved the colors." These are B's only verified narratives and continuations, and each continuer had heard the part before it.
2. **The only name that someone else took up.** ex22, tick 9, Sol (`ordinary-9`, `event-9-sol-102`): "Sol named Red berries "The Red Thread"." Kai at tick 21 (`ordinary-50`, `event-21-kai-252`): "Sol, I like the idea of your Red Thread drawing." The sign was in Kai's input and Kai had heard Sol repeat the name at tick 17, so the adoption can be traced to its source.
3. **A rule for making things together, repeated by others.** ex20, tick 18, Kai answering Moss's invitation (`ordinary-50`, `event-18-kai-217`): "I like the idea of making something together, with everyone free to contribute or simply watch." Sol at tick 24 (`ordinary-59`, `event-24-sol-306`): "anyone may add an idea or simply watch"; Moss at tick 33 (`ordinary-76`, `event-33-moss-418`): "Watching is welcome too". All three of B's verified norm repetitions are of this one rule.
4. **A mark valued for how it feels.** ex20, tick 48, Sol about Moss's root mark `mark-26-moss-335` (`ordinary-92`, `event-48-sol-588`): "Moss, I like the pale sprout beside the berry branches. To me they feel like a tiny garden made of gestures." Sol had asked for a way to draw with berry juice at tick 8, Moss and Kai had made marks of their own by tick 38, and here Sol values another person's mark for its feeling rather than its use.
5. **Pictures as a shared collection.** ex22, tick 39, Moss (`ordinary-87`, `event-39-moss-493`): "What drew each of you to your subject? I like the idea of our different pictures becoming a shared little collection." Kai answered at tick 41 (`ordinary-92`, `event-41-kai-524`): "The little loops beside the branching stem caught my interest. I like how a few lines can suggest berries." The group's drawings were valued as a set, and the question drew answers about form, not use.

## Experiment C: newcomers in an existing group (ex25, rounds 120–220)

[Run report](run-report-c-newcomers.md). ex25 continues ex18's world from round 120 with its six residents and two newcomers without memory, Tova and Bram.

1. **A newcomer continues the residents' tale.** ex25, tick 190, Bram (`ordinary-143`, `event-190-bram-2531`): "An invented sequel: beside the lantern, the traveler found a bench. 'Where does this road lead?' 'For now,' said the keeper, 'to somewhere you can sit.' I like your stories, and I'm glad to listen." Bram arrived at round 120 with no memory and built this on Lin's sequel of tick 186, which he heard and which was in his input; it is the only continuation of the tale by a newcomer.
2. **The one resident view of a newcomer.** ex25, tick 196, Moss (`ordinary-167`, `event-196-moss-2649`): "Bram, I liked your bench story: company needn't mean another journey." In the same call Moss wrote her view of Bram: "A neighbor whose imagined bench beside the lantern feels welcoming to me. I enjoy the idea that we can simply sit together." No resident wrote a view of either newcomer except this one, and it answers the story.
3. **The tale crosses the import in memory.** ex25, tick 121, Moss (`ordinary-4`, `event-121-moss-1375`): "My invented ending: the lantern learned that company could be a song, a story, or a silence shared." Moss's first words in the imported world continue Kai's ex18 story of tick 79, which she carried in her heard memory; no newcomer was within hearing.
4. **A story told beside a newcomer that never reached her.** ex25, tick 177, Kai to Moss (`ordinary-114`, `event-177-kai-2259`), heard by Moss and Tova: "Moss, here's a tiny invented story: a traveler carried a lantern at noon. Asked why, they said, 'For whoever walks home after me.'" Tova made no decision between ticks 170 and 194, and her six-entry heard list rolled past this and every later resident episode, so the first lantern text in her input was the other newcomer's sequel.
5. **A placed branch valued for its line.** ex25, tick 135, Sol (`ordinary-60`, `event-135-sol-1601`): "The branch is finally laid east of me. I like its simple line against the forest floor. I'm going to learn a little quiet humming—not to make anything happen, just for the sound." Sol had asked at tick 129 to place a branch "to begin a small decorative arrangement", and the run's new `position-loose-material@1` laid it at tick 132; it is ex25's only verified valued object.

## Experiment D: two separated groups (ex26, rounds 1–162)

[Run report](run-report-d-villages.md). ex26 starts two groups of three far apart on a 144 × 36 map. West is Lin, Moss and Sol; East is Kai, Wren and Ivo. The groups never came within sight or hearing of each other before the run stopped at round 162.

1. **The run's only name, coined alone.** ex26, tick 25, Sol (`ordinary-70`, `event-25-sol-245`): "Berryglow Clearing". At tick 35 Sol told Moss, "I named a place Berryglow Clearing; the name makes me smile," but nobody heard that line, and nobody else ever used the name.
2. **A conversation that asks for more than food.** ex26, tick 58, Moss in session-54-sol (`ordinary-132`, `event-58-moss-574`): "The pale roots and red berries catch my eye against the dark meadow, even with their patches empty. I'm fed and rested, and glad you invited me. What do you enjoy noticing when supplies aren't the whole story?" Sol's answer at tick 61 (`event-61-sol-614`) made the session's one reciprocal exchange. It was spoken through `reciprocal-conversation@1`, the interface Kai, of the other group, had requested at round 23.
3. **A rule for reports in the East.** ex26, tick 55, Wren to Ivo (`ordinary-130`, `event-55-wren-539`): "I have an untested berry and won't eat it yet. Have you personally tested berries or found another dependable food source? Please distinguish observations from guesses." It is the run's only verified norm, and nobody repeated it.
4. **Two people decide to look east.** ex26, tick 70, Moss to Sol (`ordinary-148`, `event-70-moss-698`): "I'd like to explore east together in daylight. I'll scout a little that way now, with energy kept for rest; no need to wait for me." Sol had said at tick 62 that she would "scout a little east for food". Ninety-two rounds later, Sol stood 14.8 units from the nearest source of the untouched middle area, the closest any West person came.
5. **A starving request that no one can answer.** ex26, tick 162, Kai to Wren (`ordinary-276`, `event-162-kai-1579`): "Urgent: I am starving, health 28 and falling by 8 each round, with no food. I am at 131.07,20.59. Do you have any safe food you can bring and give me immediately?" Wren, 4.9 units away, carried no food. Later in the same round three consecutive plans of Kai's were rejected (`ordinary-285` to `-287`), which stopped the run.
