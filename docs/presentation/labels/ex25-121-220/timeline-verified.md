# Verified label timeline: ex25-121-220

Verified on 2026-11-27: verdicts on the model labels (gpt-6.1-sol) for ex25 rounds 121–220 were set by a Claude agent, not by a human annotator, and await a human spot-check. ex25 is experiment C ([PR-45](../../../exec-plans/completed/pr-45-c-newcomers.md)): ex18's world imported at round 120, with the six residents and their memories, plus two newcomers without memory, Tova and Bram.

The window holds 33 labelable items, 15 of them labeled by the model. Every entry of [`verification.json`](verification.json) (15 positives and all 18 negatives, because the window has fewer than 20) was checked against the retained archive in `experiment/ex25`: the call responses and input packets, the replay events with their `heard` listeners, positions within the 5-unit hearing and vision range, and the memory views. Every item text matches the archive verbatim; a private message is shown as its subject and body, as the label items join them.

The labels were made with `--registry ex18-1-120/labels.json`, so the model saw the [ex18 registry](../ex18-1-120/timeline-verified.md) (names N1 and N2, narratives S1–S4) and could link to it; new entries start at S5. A link to an ex18 entry is accepted only along a path of knowledge: a resident's heard memory carried across the import in `experiment/ex25/initial.json` and present in that resident's round input. A newcomer can know an entry only from what they saw or heard in ex25.
