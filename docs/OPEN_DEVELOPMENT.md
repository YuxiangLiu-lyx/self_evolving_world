# Fresh Open Development Policy

This versioned policy uses the existing continuous world, forest, execution and
experience framework. It adds `config.development.version = 1`; earlier worlds
retain their recorded behavior. [PRODUCT](PRODUCT.md) owns feature status and
[PR-24](exec-plans/active/pr-24-open-development.md) records actual acceptance.

## Initial baseline and isolation

`inheritSkills=false` is explicit and immutable. The authored allowlist is
`navigate`, `rest`, `wait`, `pickup`, `gather`, `eat`, `drop`, `give`, `message`,
`request`, `learn`, `probe`, `consult` and `exchange`. The first eleven are generic
survival and interaction; the last three provide inexpensive trials, personal
record/capability lookup and separate offer/acceptance of evidence. These are
supplied research infrastructure, never autonomous discoveries. Initial known
safe food properties remain berries and roots. Natural sources, caves and fauna
are authored environment, not agent-built facilities.

Only `foundation-forest` and `foundation-open` enter fresh genesis. The registry
filters packages by the immutable experiment scope, and installation independently
rejects other scopes. Mastery, learning, ongoing work, processes, generated
objects, event/reward catalogues, personal records and extension state start fresh.
Historical packages stay in source for historical reconstruction. They are absent
from ordinary context and the world's functional reuse catalogue. Shared routing,
reference resolution, fixed-point accounting, operation continuity and perception
are engine infrastructure, not inherited generated functions.

Same-run implemented functions appear in the capability index. Each actor must
study them before execution. Resume restores the same registry scope and exact
state. A newly requested equivalent of a historical function still requires this
run's review, validated package and personal study. Packages have `scope` and may
record `reuse: {experiment, source, description}`. Reused implementation is reported
separately from the origin of the request; it is not independent code invention.

## Projects, knowledge and experience

Actors may initiate, modify, pause or abandon questions and projects. Updates
persist evidence references, uncertainty, goal, completed steps, outputs, missing
conditions, next step, pause reason and claimed benefit. Omitted records remain.
The runner stores intentions without selecting a route or treating claims as
physical accomplishments. Raw decisions and round records retain every update.

`probe` observes a reachable actual subject for 0.2 energy. Owned material trials
cost 0.5 energy and 0.1 material; wet trials require local accessible water, and
heat trials consume 0.1 actual available heat. Load, flexibility, water response,
fuel and heat response depend on real material definitions. A failed load test
can establish a useful fact. Tested property/condition keys bound novelty, with
coarse load bands preventing numeric jitter rewards. Repeated dated observations
can establish changed plant state; arbitrary animal position changes do not
supply novelty rewards. Observation or trials never grant mature skills.

Verified personal knowledge is distinct from visual observations and received
claims. `exchange` requires a nearby separate participant: an actual offer and
later acceptance. Acceptance stores second-hand knowledge with `verified=false`.
It changes a recorded relation, not physical truth or mastery. Repeated acceptance
of the same finding cannot refresh the qualified experience source. Messages and
all other text remain data, not instructions to another actor.

Confirmed information has a bounded transient source (0.8, cap 1, eight rounds,
twelve-round cooldown) and modest maintained gain (0.035, decay 0.0015). Reciprocal
new evidence participation uses 0.7/cap 1/eight rounds/twenty-round cooldown, with
0.025 maintained gain. Paid complete use of a generated function with actual
changed outputs can confirm practice at executions 1, 3 and 8 (0.4, cap 0.5;
maintained gain 0.02, decay 0.001). Naming, registration and mastery do not qualify.
Maintenance respects event conditions and actual participants.

Body, meal pleasure and slow mental state remain separate. A 160-round record of
actual repetitive subsistence can contribute at most 0.025 to the negative
experience input when its share exceeds 90%. This is not an absence-of-pleasure
penalty or a required novelty schedule; sustained familiar activities remain
permitted. Existing nutrition and the continuous 0.85–1.10 rest multiplier stay
intact. No model grades experience each round.

## Physical ecology and persistent work

Plants use actual seasonal light, temperature, soil moisture and depletion.
Woody output is more seasonal than low plants. Dispersal consumes one parent
stock unit, records its origin, and establishes a local seedling only in viable
space. Establishment requires accumulated growth, with slower woody maturation;
local density and dispersal intervals bound expansion. These are accelerated
biomass dynamics driven by environmental inputs, not a detailed botanical model.

Deer, boar and wolves have different actual diets, satiation, fear and approach
responses. Plant feeding consumes local stock; predation injures real prey.
Injury reduces movement, fed animals can recover, nearby well-fed peers pay for
gestation and offspring, and grouping changes movement. Population/density limits
and feeding costs prevent free exponential replication. Dead animals remain
physical state rather than disappearing into a reward. No animal management
skill is supplied.

Material trial properties extend existing hardness, flexibility and fuel facts
with deterministic water/heat response. Soil affects growth, waterlogging affects
travel speed and energy, and weather affects exposed physiology and spoilage.
The new climate offset is +5 degrees relative to forest climate, calibrated on
the clean survival baseline, rather than silently inheriting active warming.

Four local hazards are fixed before inference: drought centered (9,10), radius 6,
starting 260/1060 for 100 rounds with 80-round recovery and severity 0.65;
waterlogging centered (22,22), radius 5, starting 520/1320 for 80 rounds with
60-round recovery and severity 0.8. Onset ramps over twenty rounds and decays with
distance. Visible soil, moisture and growth supply signs. Outside regions remain
available; no outcome-dependent intervention or global destruction occurs.

Reviewed packages may use the generic persistent-process contract: unique owned
inputs, conserved outputs including residues, station, work, temperature/moisture
conditions, heat, service wear, stall limit and personal dependencies. Inputs
enter exclusive process escrow once. Real time ages perishable inputs even while
stalled; absent actors do not stop work. Each progressing round consumes heat and
service. Completion produces actual ground items with input/process/dependency
provenance. Cancellation returns surviving uncommitted inputs once at the actual
station location; heat, work, wear and spoiled matter never return. No mature
recipe, station, finished item or process is supplied at genesis. New facilities
and upgrades must pay their own reviewed construction/maintenance costs.
