# Collective Life Protocol

This optional continuous-world policy compares one bundled architecture change:
shared observed information and unified control (A), versus private knowledge,
mild preferences and persistent personal intentions (B). It does not isolate the
causal effect of any individual component. More actors, actions, skill names or
ambitious plans are not success criteria.

## Physical and capability baseline

Both arms start with the same seeded 96 by 72 continuous forest and eighteen
human members in three groups of six. Positions, bodies, inventories, perception,
skills, learning and finite lives remain individual. There is no replacement
population or remote shared inventory. The existing seasonal ecology, local
hazards, material properties, trials, persistent processes, collisions, operation
escrow and reference resolver remain in use. Population capacity is calibrated
before inference. The preflight controller is an isolated feasibility oracle;
its knowledge, allocation strategy and action choices never enter model inputs.

The explicit authored baseline is `navigate`, `rest`, `wait`, `pickup`, `gather`,
`eat`, `drop`, `give`, `message`, `request`, `learn`, `probe`, `consult`, `exchange`,
`council` and `teach`. Safe berry/root properties are authored starting knowledge.
These interfaces and initial knowledge are not independent discoveries.
`inheritSkills=false` and separate experiment scopes isolate A, B and all older
generated packages, search results, objects, rewards, processes and mastery.
Historical source stays available for historical replay.

## Information and agency

Each group uses the same model and transport for two structurally distinct kinds
of fresh requests. No previous response or shared conversation is sent.

In A, the group controller reads the union of its members' actual observed and
remembered information and assigns plans only to members currently needing a
decision. It cannot inspect another group's private state. In B, an individual
packet is built from that member's observation, records and received information.
The public coordinator reads only group identity metadata, dated reports and its
previous public proposals. It never reads the union of private member records.

The authored meeting interface is a local information channel. Reporting, reading
and responding cost a round and effort and require actual attendance. Reports
contain dated personal facts and carried stock, not a shared inventory. Public
proposals are recorded after the round barrier and do not assign member actions.
Members can accept, offer an alternative or decline. Their actual plans remain
their own choices. The public capability register is an authored lookup interface
at the meeting location; reading it discovers contracts, not mastery. Nearby
teaching requires an actual qualified offer and a separate acceptance, followed
by full individual study. A public implementation is not group-wide mastery.

Personal questions and projects retain evidence, uncertainty, goals, steps,
outputs, missing conditions, next steps, pause reasons and claimed benefits.
Claims are not physical achievements. B starts with reproducible tendencies near
0.5, without occupations. Small preference-dependent multipliers affect actual
event-grounded experience, never basic nutrition. Injury can slowly lower risk
acceptance; independently confirming or contradicting a received finding changes
trust with evidence. Repetition, saturation, useful practice and sustained mental
state retain the existing rules.

## Attention and budgets

Each group has one attention slot per environment round. Existing valid plans,
learning and processes execute without inference. Overdue members receive priority;
otherwise immediate physical danger precedes waiting age and recent service. The
maximum decision wait is eight rounds.
An unfinished atomic operation retains its progress. A content-neutral opportunity
to reconsider a sustained plan occurs after 100 rounds; it chooses no activity.
New reports can receive public coordination after 24 rounds, including after an
eight-round report wait when no member is urgent or overdue. Failed public calls
retain their unresolved reports and still consume the slot and retry interval.
Budget exhaustion or excessive waiting pauses the entire
world, rather than running queued members to death.

| Allocation                                |   A short |   B short | B continuation | Total ceiling |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------: | --------: | -------------: | ------------: |
| Ordinary attempts, including coordination |       120 |       120 |            410 |           650 |
| World review attempts                     |        10 |        10 |             40 |            60 |
| Conversation development handoffs         |         4 |         4 |             12 |            20 |
| Measured/reserved model tokens            | 1,300,000 | 1,300,000 |      3,900,000 |     6,500,000 |

These allocations are frozen in the execution plan after the retained full
offline calibration, before formal inference. Both arms use `gpt-6.1-sol` and
environment seed `20261123`. Short windows target 200 rounds each. B's actual
short checkpoint extends toward 1,600 cumulative rounds after reaching 200 or
exhausting a short call/token allocation. The short comparison is closed and
retained at its actual observed round; an early ending is reported explicitly.
The separately reserved continuation allocation then begins without resetting
world state, pending work or the overall call ledger. Its counter boundary binds
the preserved short checkpoint, including calls made before an unfinished round.
Technical/context failures and uncertain pending calls do not unlock continuation.
Unused allocations do not move between phases or arms.

Every attempted request reserves the UTF-8 byte length of the complete request,
the 8,192 output-token cap and 4,096 framing allowance before transport. Known
provider usage replaces the conservative reservation; unknown usage retains it.
Input, output, latency, context size, reason and failures remain recorded. The
[Responses output limit](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/responses/methods/create)
includes visible and reasoning output. Internal current-conversation development
tokens and preparation/review costs are not directly metered; they are unknown,
excluded from the enforceable provider token ceiling and never reported as zero.
Contexts select whole records with omission counts and on-demand access; source
records remain retained. An oversized essential packet pauses before inference.
World reviews retain every request, use the current functional capability index,
and deduplicate selected observed objects across applicants. Exact execution
schemas and complete state remain available in the source-bound development
handoff; absent context details are unknown rather than proof of absence.

## Recovery and evidence

The existing task intent accepts `comparison: "collective-life-v1"` only for a new
continuous experiment. Its coordinator allocates the next two IDs sequentially,
returns normal development handoffs and preserves B's short archive through the
existing observation-window extension. It does not launch a second task service
or a recursive development model. World time stops during review and development.
Both arms retain independent package scopes and raw journals. Checkpoints occur
every 200 rounds and at pauses. Current-source and archive validation still gate
delivery.

The observatory uses group colors, selectable member state, personal projects,
known facts, received records, responses and ongoing task progress. Global human
inspection is explicitly separate from a model's private perspective. Existing
object and process renderers show actual extension effects.

`results.collective` separates the short window from B's continuation and records
member action distributions, information events, requests, mastery, personally
verified findings, outputs, deaths, attention and measured cost. Grounded progress
events per measured token are descriptive counts, not a claim about all costs or
lasting productivity. Reports must trace actual requests, review, development,
study, use and subsequent consequences, distinguish fixtures from formal choices,
and report absent or repetitive behavior honestly.
