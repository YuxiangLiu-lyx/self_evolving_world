# Forest World Extension

Forest-v1 is an opt-in extension of the version-7 continuous replay envelope. The immutable `config.forest` marker selects its rules; ex7 and all historical protocols retain their existing transitions. [PRODUCT](PRODUCT.md) owns acceptance status. The [current plan](exec-plans/completed/pr-21-forest-lives.md) records this iteration and remaining work.

## Environment and time

Genesis is 48 by 36 continuous units with three distinct starts, two enterable caves, a winding river with two ford regions, woodland, wetland, exposed rocky slopes and clearings. The 26 resource definitions cover distinct food, raw material and plant properties; deer, boar and wolves use three code-controlled animal behaviors. Sources contain finite stock and bounded growth. Known berries provide 8 nutrition instead of ex7's 18; roots provide 14. A body consumes 0.65 satiety per round, while gathering, eating, carrying, movement and learning retain explicit real costs. The isolated preflight verifies suitable-season survival and respawn without supplying its strategy or test capabilities to ordinary agents.

One round advances one simulation time unit after the complete simultaneous barrier. A day spans 80 units and a year 800. These are accelerated ecological cycles, not a claim that real plants mature in seconds. Model, development and rendering time never advance this clock. Smooth seasonal, daily and regional temperature affects body heat and growth. Light and moisture also constrain growth; local heating, light or water changes can alter those conditions through reviewed mechanisms. There is no immutable winter planting prohibition. Food ages under local temperature; longer-lived nuts and roots differ from rapidly spoiling fruit, fungi and fish.

Animals forage, flee, defend or hunt within bounded home ranges. Wolves have intermittent hunting windows, speed below actor travel speed, warning distance and attack cooldown. Boar defend close approaches. Animals move through actual geometry and never invoke a model. These are deterministic experimental ecological templates, not a general biological simulation.

## Lives, memory and plans

Each original identity has three total lives. After action and environmental settlement, health zero records one death, drops existing inventory identities at the death position and cancels study. Pending extension work uses a reviewed `onDeath` hook to release escrow without refunding effort. A living actor's old plan cannot continue after death. Remaining lives restore the body at an unoccupied traversable point within the initial region; unsafe or completely obstructed spawn regions cause a recoverable technical pause instead of deleting structures. The third death ends decisions for that actor. World time, source stocks, objects, other actors, mastery, relationships and important memory persist.

Death evidence includes actual damage events, vital values before/after, location, previous actor-authored intention and remaining lives. Unattributed contributors remain unknown. It supplies no solution and enters the next normal decision, without an extra reflection call. Important events have a separate retained personal stream; empty rest and routine wellbeing changes do not evict it. The full raw event stream remains in replay, including ineffective repetition.

The new event catalogue controls both JSON output enums and parsed subscriptions. Missing aliases such as ex7's `action_failed`, `health_changed` and `learning_completed` are rejected. Failure, death, completed learning, new danger, delivered messages, seasonal transitions and significant injury trigger fresh decisions. Repeated ongoing thermal damage does not create a model call on every round. Action references recheck current owned quantities, actual source reach/stock and observable targets. The executor still chooses no strategy and does not ban rest.

A zero benefit must have a substantive experiential reason; a missing old rule is not a reason to permanently withhold feedback. Installation, names, requests and mastery cannot award happiness. Equivalent implementations remain shared, with separate personal learning time.

New functions, resources and processes continue through reviewed host-code packages and the existing current-conversation development barrier. The new event declaration and optional death hook extend that contract; they do not impose a fixed operation list. Installation grants no stock, structure, score or mastery.

## Request-driven active warming

At formal round 469 Lin independently requested stationary physical exertion in response to observed cooling. `active-warming@1` implements world-221: two rounds of personal study, then operations of one to six consecutive stationary rounds. Each round requires energy at least three, satiety at least twenty and body temperature below the existing sixteen-degree comfort boundary. It spends three energy, uses the authoritative environment and metabolism once, then adds up to 0.35 temperature units toward that boundary. Existing heat exposure is never lowered. Net temperature can still fall; damage already caused by the environment is not healed. Movement, an incompatible action, lost prerequisites or death cancels the operation without refunds. Resolution is idempotent within a round.

Events and the personal adapter report the actual position, remaining rounds, effort, environmental change, exertion heat, net temperature change and same-round no-exertion cold discomfort. Three consecutive completed rounds with measurable relief, no injury or health loss, and satiety still at least twenty can qualify. The existing `thermal-refuge` source accepts this reviewed alternative event, using the same cap, duration and shared twelve-round cooldown as shelter relief. Source histories persist; exercise does not create a second additive reward. Event/interface subscriptions are validated against installed executable declarations. Requesting, studying, starting and merely generating heat do not qualify. The Canvas shows restrained stationary exercise only during active execution and stops it on completion, cancellation or death. Isolated tests establish mechanism availability, not autonomous formal use.
