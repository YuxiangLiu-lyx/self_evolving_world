Its success establishes existence of a tested path, not that an autonomous actor will find it.

## References and operations

All declared reference parameters pass through one resolver before dispatch. `item:ID` selects an actual stack instance, `object:ID` a scene object, and `resource:ID` a category. Bare exact IDs are retained aliases; a bare resource ID is a category alias only when it is not an observed exact identity. Categories with multiple eligible identities are ambiguous, even if only one would satisfy the requested quantity. The error contains personally observable candidates; it never chooses an alternative, aggregates unrelated stacks, reveals another inventory or supplies material. Consumption, reserved quantities, ownership and visibility are checked against current state. Navigation may use explicitly dated personal memory, while interaction still checks actual reach.

`repeat` counts complete executions. It never counts elapsed operation rounds. The kernel binds resolved arguments for ongoing work, records elapsed work and deterministic remaining work (or unknown), and advances the task only after completion. New abilities declare reference and execution contracts; declared fixed durations must agree with completion. Handler contexts distinguish the first execution round from continuation. Costs and escrow remain physical state, not a per-decision charge.

Inference, development, computation and budget pauses preserve the exact operation and do not age material or advance hunger. Resuming that world continues the same work. During simulation, incompatible actions, invalidation and death follow the declared cancellation contract: surviving unconsumed escrow is released once, consumed/spoiled material and spent effort never return. Existing continuous-attention activities do not offer an actor-controlled suspended-work state. A future resumable activity needs explicit reviewed storage, aging and resumption semantics; it cannot assume that waiting freezes physical time.

Outgoing message success advances the sender's existing plan. Ordinary incoming messages update memory. An urgent incoming message can interrupt only its actual recipient and must identify that recipient or the current work target as its subject. Sender text remains untrusted data. Failure, danger, death, important physiological thresholds and actor-selected nonmessage events remain valid interruption causes. Round settlement coalesces triggers into the next normal decision, without a separate message model call.

## Body, pleasure and mental state

Physical comfort still depends on actual health, satiety, fatigue and temperature. Temporary event-grounded satisfaction and meal pleasure remain separately inspectable. A sustained mental value starts at zero and changes slowly from actual experience awards, injury and dietary monotony. Without such evidence it decays toward neutral; an expired reward or absent activity is not a negative experience.

The mental value is bounded to −1…1, its event gain is 0.025 and neutral decay is 0.003 per round. Rest uses a continuous multiplier between 0.85 and 1.10. It never uses total happiness or repeats an extra round because of integer rounding. Eating changes fractional energy effort through the same multiplier; nutrition and toxicity remain physical food properties. Basic ingestion is possible at zero energy. Rest remains effective even at the minimum mental state.

Maintained satisfaction accumulates gradually from registered successful evidence and decays exponentially with its reviewed duration. It has no expiration cliff and remains capped; identical practice can be maintained without forced novelty. Transient rules retain their explicit duration/cooldown semantics. Requests, naming, registration and mastery cannot qualify. Actual useful investigation may qualify after functional, experience and visual review. Social benefits require actual participation and consent, not assertions by one actor.

## Diet and environment

The dietary window retains ten substantial meal episodes. At least eight realized nutrition units are needed to register an episode; nearby intakes are merged and tiny portions accumulate actual intake rather than creating diversity entries. Meal rewards have a twelve-round cooldown. Canonical ingredient composition and actual processing define similarity, not display names or a generic wild-food label. After six meals, a dominant ingredient share above 65% gradually increases monotony; identical processing strengthens similarity. Freshness and reviewed food quality affect bounded pleasure, not nutrition deletion. Entirely wasteful eating adds no meal evidence.

Hunger is 0.9 per round. Seasonal growth retains actual light, moisture and temperature requirements and scales to 0.85, with slow local depletion/recovery. Exposed rain/wind variations use deterministic time/seed functions and visible climate facts. Carried mass increases movement effort by 1.2 times the previous mass surcharge. Actual enclosures can provide reviewed preservation; stock still ages, and reduced spoilage must come from a physical state change. New tools and processing retain explicit material and effort costs.

Initial nearby sources remain unchanged. Before formal inference, the isolated supply inventory found only 1,886 safe nutrition units against 3,240 gross metabolic demand over the requested window. Remote forest food stocks and capacities are therefore initialized at three times the previous local-site quantities, recorded in `remoteStockScale = 3`; acquisition still requires travel, real collection, carrying and eating, and there is no runtime rescue. This supplies a finite exploration path while the higher hunger, winter growth and exposure retain repeated labor. Calibration failures remain in the execution-plan evidence and are not formal agent results.

## Reviewed local fire extension

Lin's actual round-450 request led to `local-fire@1`, after a failed world output and a second accepted review. `tend-fire` requires six personal study rounds. Its five modes prepare a branch friction kit, build a covered stone hearth, feed explicit material instances, ignite, and extinguish. Preparing costs one branch and four energy-rounds; building consumes four stone, two bark and one vine across four rounds at 1.5 energy. Ignition takes eight stationary rounds at 1.5 energy, one of ten kit attempts and 0.1 actual dry-grass tinder. Work binds references and reserves material once; surviving escrow returns on cancellation, while spent effort, tinder and durability never return. Kits retain whole-item identity through handoff and death. Multiple eligible kits are an explicit ambiguity, never a silent choice.

The world review's center-distance requirement conflicts with its radius/clearance/body exclusion. The implementation records one geometric clarification: use the existing 0.65 interaction gap from the hearth surface, including actor radius, while retaining the 0.45 footprint, 0.25 clearance, actual body separation, water/solid exclusion and open sky. This does not change global movement or grant an exception to collisions. The bark cover is not a room. Components and provenance persist in the finished hearth.

Fuel has real input identities, quantity, mass, energy and moisture. Material moisture initializes lazily from current observable rain at this version boundary; no stock or existing physiology is changed on installation. Each round rain adds 0.08 times actual transmitted rain, dry weather removes 0.01 times one plus wind, and lit fuel first dries by 0.08. The 80-condition cover loses 0.25 on rainy rounds, transmits 0.2–1.0 of rain and reduces wind to 0.75 while intact. Ignition requires 0.5 dry grass and 0.5 branch, fuel moisture at most 0.35, transmitted rain at most 0.15 and wind at most 0.65. Paid tinder is accounted for during later progress checks. Feed capacity is eight combustible mass units. Ordered fuel lots release at most one energy per round, scaled by dryness; no-combustion embers last at most three rounds and provide no unearned heat. Heavy transmitted rain extinguishes. Combusted input mass is recorded as 10% persistent ash and 90% released products; failed ignition tinder remains as residue. This bookkeeping approximation does not simulate atmospheric oxygen or indoor smoke.

Actual unobstructed heat reaches every living actor within two units before the existing thermal-damage calculation. Gain is `0.6 * burnFraction * max(0, 1 - distance / 2) * (1 - 0.5 * wind)`. It does not cap at comfort, heal, improve rest directly or survive fuel exhaustion. Three consecutive stationary, fed, injury-free rest/wait rounds with positive heat and reduced cold discomfort qualify for the existing thermal-refuge source. Continuing actual qualification maintains a separate component in that same source at 0.025 per round, capped at one; inactivity decays it by 0.003. The source uses the maximum of transient and maintained components, never their sum. Reentry requires three qualifying rounds. Neither construction, ignition, ownership, naming nor learning itself rewards satisfaction.

Isolated regression and browser fixtures demonstrate this execution path without entering ordinary context. Formal use, persistence, cross-agent benefit and any observed cost savings must still be measured from the single retained run.
