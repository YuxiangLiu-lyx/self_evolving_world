# PR-18: Continuous Space, Open Extensions and Persistent Wellbeing

## Requirement summary

Build an independently versioned seventh world protocol on merged ex6 and latest main. Deliver real continuous 2D simulation, extensible mechanism/spatial/visual packages, outcome-based wellbeing, autonomous persistent tasks, a readable game scene and one resumable real observation targeting 1,000 rounds. Preserve the existing task coordinator, all historical archives and their replay semantics. The user explicitly authorizes development, real inference through the configured plan-funded transport, validation, publication and guarded merge.

## Requirements

- R1: Preserve ex1-ex6 and version-1 rules, inspect and classify ex6 restrictions, allocate the next experiment through the existing coordinator, and implement v7 as a separate protocol without replacing the working task framework.
- R2: Implement deterministic continuous coordinates, arbitrary-direction movement, distance/time/effort consistency, unified collision/vision/interaction geometry, object shapes and entrances, private-knowledge goal navigation and dynamic obstacle updates with simultaneous actor settlement.
- R3: Replace experimental operation, plan, learning, object, niche, yield and source-file ceilings with extensible versioned interfaces and explicit computational safeguards; novel reasonable demands can add state, operations, processes, interfaces, personal harness adaptations and presentation through current-conversation development.
- R4: Register reviewed mechanism, spatial, visual and migration packages at round boundaries with dependencies, source/assets and before/after evidence; retain immutable history and source validation, update model schemas/catalogues together, and require actual materials, learning and execution rather than granting finished outcomes.
- R5: Deliver a coherent illustrated top-down scene with zoom, pan, follow, minimap, event location, timeline/comparison and true actor state/memory/task/wellbeing panels; visuals follow authoritative state, legacy replay stays discrete, and camera/rendering never advances simulation or calls models.
- R6: Implement configurable wellbeing from zero to one hundred with physical comfort at most ten and other satisfaction at most ninety; actual health, hunger, fatigue and thermal states drive comfort while reviewed event-grounded personal sources define persistence, repetition and decay without request/learning/name rewards or unbounded accumulation.
- R7: Limit ordinary goals to health, survival and wellbeing, remove development-route guidance from every ordinary prompt/input/template/feedback, retain optional self-authored plans, actual memory and preferences, and preserve protected goals, scoring, budgets and observation permissions during personal harness extensions.
- R8: Execute registered lifecycle and background processes without per-round inference, support developable repair/removal/recycling/replacement and mechanism-specific renewal, retain consent and actual participant/care costs for any social/reproductive extensions, and pause rather than disguise computation exhaustion as a natural world limit.
- R9: Run one independent real world with three initial actors targeting a first 1,000-round observation window, stage reports/checkpoints every 200 rounds, and further continuation without a terminal world horizon; validate continuous-space/thermal/long-window survival offline without injecting fixtures or rescue plans into actor context.
- R10: Persist attempts before inference and accepted decisions before execution, enforce shared budgets of four hundred ordinary calls, sixty world calls and twenty conversation development handoffs including failures/retries, disclose unknown internal developer costs, preserve consistent barriers and conditional persistent tasks, and globally checkpoint/pause on exhaustion without duplicate calls or effects on resume.
- R11: Prove legacy display compatibility, continuous physics/navigation/costs, synchronous settlement, lifecycle, wellbeing, extension loading, ongoing tasks, budgets and recovery with meaningful isolated fixtures; demonstrate a previously absent mechanism plus visual/spatial component developed and used by another actor, and retain segmented indexed logs/replays/source/assets suitable for long runs.
- R12: Retain honest lifecycle and per-actor behavioral analysis distinguishing requested, reviewed, implemented, mastered, executed, sustained and shared outcomes; report actual rounds, survival, health, wellbeing persistence/sources, trajectories, material reinvestment, failures and available cost, synchronize English documentation and pass full local/visual/PR/current-CI/independent-merge gates.

## Scope and exclusions

In scope: a separate continuous world kernel and controller, versioned extension registry, public-interface synchronization, deterministic spatial and wellbeing mechanisms, game-style viewer, segmented durable archives, current coordinator adapters and one real observation. New activity categories are not prohibited merely by name or era; implementation must supply actual prerequisites and state transitions. The initial baseline contains only necessary survival, navigation, perception, communication and extension-request interfaces. Fixture-only activities never enter real ordinary inputs.

Out of scope: preassigned professions, scripted civilization or autonomous survival strategy executors, unreviewed code execution, automatic private-map disclosure, paid inference fallback, fixture discoveries, model-weight training, historical archive mutation, guaranteed happiness/activity outcomes and implementing 3D before a reviewed demand. Future presentation requests remain eligible for development; unimplemented features stay explicitly unimplemented.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Fractional positions, diagonal/curved navigation and swept collision remain identical across save/replay and renderer frame rates. Distances, time and energy agree; newly placed obstacles affect routes without exposing unknown targets.
2. An isolated previously unregistered capability adds executable state/process/spatial and presentation behavior, registers through the development path with source evidence, and can be individually learned and used by a second actor. Historical prefixes remain byte-equivalent.
3. Physical comfort uses real vitals and temperature; mental satisfaction is event-grounded, personal, bounded and repeatable according to reviewed source rules. Renaming/repeated requests cannot award points. Death and severe health risk remain independently protected decision conditions.
4. Program execution advances continuous tasks and background processes without selecting strategies. An optional empty plan/project is valid. Schemas and observations expose new registered interfaces immediately.
5. Offline long-window survival preflight, deterministic save/resume/budget edge fixtures, historical viewer reconstruction and full tests pass before the real run starts. Initial conditions and budgets are recorded before inference; no stage hints, resource rescue or selective reruns.
6. Every 200-round stage is retained when reached. Target-window completion, budget pause, development pause and engineering block remain distinct; continued runs preserve counts, identities, history and exactly-once effects.
7. All available inputs/outputs/tokens/timing/reasons and development handoffs remain auditable in indexed segments. Reports distinguish production from consumption, physical effects from pleasure and cross-use from consensual interaction.
8. Every changed file and current image is reviewed, full unfiltered validation passes, exact-head PR COMMENT is published, current remote checks pass and independently verified merge completes the task.

## Implementation sequence

4. Add isolated extension and physics/controller/archive/browser fixtures; complete offline preflight and full baseline verification.
5. Run the allocated real observation, resolving necessary development requests in this conversation with version-bound validation and resume. Preserve every attempt and stage without adjusting the scenario.
6. Analyze actual behavior and costs, synchronize documentation, complete full validation and actual review, publish and guarded-merge through the coordinator.

## Risks and follow-up

Continuous collision, long histories and live source/visual changes increase replay and migration risk. Source-bound version records and prefix reconstruction must fail closed. Context reduction must preserve each actor's actual knowledge; happiness proposals require semantic review and event-level audit. The observation is exploratory, with no promised activity or single-mechanism causal attribution. The single proposed follow-up is a pre-registered matched multi-seed comparison of satisfaction-review contracts, retaining zero outcomes and every run while leaving ordinary goals and activity guidance unchanged.
