# PR-23: Consistent execution, diet and sustained experience

## Requirement summary

Build on merged PRs #15 and #16 without repeating repaired navigation, escrow or lifecycle work. Preserve ex1–ex8. Introduce an independently selected forest experience policy, verify it without inference, then observe three real actors under the existing coordinator and development barriers. Behavior emergence is an observation, never an acceptance condition.

## Requirements

- R1: Resolve instance, resource and object references through one observable, ownership-aware contract shared by base and generated interfaces. Reject ambiguity, absence, spent/reserved quantities and invalid ownership without hidden information or substitutions.
- R2: Count repeat as complete executions, expose ongoing work and remaining progress, and conserve costs across progress, interruption, pause/resume, cancellation and death. New abilities inherit the execution contract.
- R3: Keep outgoing and ordinary incoming messages from forcing decisions; qualify relevant urgent incoming events, aggregate round triggers, and retain failure/danger/invalid-condition replanning. Reproduce actual ex8 failures in isolated regressions.
- R4: Separate bodily wellbeing, temporary pleasure and neutral-initialized slow mental state. Bound recovery modifiers to 0.85–1.10, preserve nutrition and basic recovery, and avoid discrete-time amplification.
- R5: Track ten substantial meals using actual ingredient identity, composition and processing; settle quality/spoilage and bounded monotony without renaming or tiny-portion reward exploits.
- R6: Support transient and maintained satisfaction with actual events, participants, conditions, saturation and decay. No application, naming, registration or mastery rewards; genuine investigation may qualify after review.
- R7: Preregister stronger linked seasonality, local depletion, preservation, exposure and carrying/processing costs, starting hunger calibration at 0.9. Preserve geometry, animals, finite lives, drops and memory; verify exploration/learning slack offline.
- R8: Compute concise factual diet/state/time/effort/failure/benefit summaries. Accept problem/effect/known-condition requests with unknown methods and costs. Review function, learning, experience and visuals; support new mechanisms with real materials and real participant actions.
- R9: Run the next allocated experiment once: three actors, gpt-6.1-sol, fixed seed and policy, 1,200 target rounds, 650 ordinary / 60 world attempts / 20 development handoffs. Preserve failed attempts, every 200-round checkpoint, source versions and recoverable budget stops. No retrospective policy tuning.
- R10: Distinguish requests, review, implementation, individual learning, usage, sustained benefit and fixtures. Report execution errors, decision overhead, role costs, diet/mental/recovery trajectories, cross-actor effects, efficiency, interests, social behavior, death and ineffective loops with replay landmarks; ex8 comparison is exploratory.
- R11: Complete scoped acceptance, actual visual/file review, PR, exact-head CI and guarded merge through the existing task state machine. Preserve historical archives and use dependency-bound reuse truthfully.

## Scope and exclusions

Pure core extensions, existing continuous controller/schema, versioned forest scenario, observatory state display, isolated regressions/preflight, retained formal observation and English documentation. No new task framework, recursive developer agent, scripted formal decisions, prescribed activity list, fabricated inference, historical rerun, budget extension or seed shopping. No mandatory death, cooperation or complex invention. Internal developer usage remains unknown rather than zero.
