# PR-25: Collective life with private members

## Requirement summary

Extend the current fresh continuous world to three same-species groups of six
independent members. Compare shared-information coordination (A) with private
knowledge, individual preferences and persistent intentions (B) for 200 rounds,
then continue B's actual checkpoint toward 1,600 cumulative rounds within one
fixed campaign budget. Retain all outcomes, including early budget pauses and
unproductive behavior. Use the existing task coordinator, development handoffs,
archive validation, viewer, review and guarded merge.

The inspected baseline is main `7c56dcf`, with merged PR #18, no open PRs and ten
numbered archives. Ex10 stopped at round 358 after 20 development attempts when
its observation exceeded the fixed input guard. Scope isolation, references,
continuous tasks, projects, ecology and material processes already exist. Reuse
them; add structural information boundaries, fair scheduling and bounded context.

## Scope and exclusions

Reuse continuous-v1 and its scoped generated-package installation, survival,
environment, exact references, operations and source-bound handoffs. Add optional
collective policy/state and a small sequential A/B adapter in the existing task
entry; do not build a second service or recursively invoke coding models.

Exclude inherited generated skills, automatic population replacement, automatic
technology unlocks, assigned occupations, predetermined inventions, free shared
inventory, omniscient B coordination, historical inference, archive rewrites,
new 3D rendering, model-weight training and causal attribution to one component.

## Acceptance criteria

No-model regressions must cover private/public context noninterference, actual
message/knowledge propagation, proposal consent and persistent intention,
preference/trust event provenance, resource contention, task progress/recovery,
A/B/A-resume skill isolation, personal learning, attention fairness, large-context
bounds, durable attempted-call/token limits and the two-arm continuation adapter.
Preflight must use identical fresh resources and all eighteen actual members.
All selected checks must pass. Fixture strategies never enter formal contexts.

## Results and delivery evidence

Implemented optional collective actors, separate private/report-only contexts,
local reporting and teaching, durable intentions, event-grounded tendencies/trust,
bounded attention, token reservations, phase-preserving campaign continuation and
2D member/process inspection. The final development gate passed 749 unfiltered
tests in 100 files, type/dependency/build checks and identical reconstruction of
all 132 prior B rounds before source revision 12. The final local acceptance run passed 751 unfiltered tests in 100 files,
all selected checks and archive preservation. Its 12 archive validations and
74 browser tests (168 images) reused authenticated unchanged receipts with zero
new deep rebuilds. All selected images were personally inspected. Remote delivery
gates remain required separately and are not claimed by local results.

[Calibration](../evidence/pr-25-collective-life/calibration.md) retains every failed
attempt and the successful no-model 1,600-round fresh baseline: eighteen survivors,
no lost lives, zero action failures, 68.2743% optional fixture time. The unchanged
main baseline is `7c56dcf015a7d5905f0ca20392974a0b904caa16`.

The actual runs are ex11 (A) and ex12 (B), with identical physical genesis and
isolated registries. A stopped at round 7 on its four-handoff allocation. B's
short token allocation stopped at 37; the preserved continuation stopped at 136
because the next complete request could not fit its frozen token ceiling. No
allocation was transferred, no policy retuned and no world restarted. All members
survived, but the requested matched 200-round comparison and 1,600-round horizon
were not reached. The final checkpoint includes a half-built tray and preserved
partial extraction work. One actual stone handover and material/knowledge chains
are observed; stable division of labor and sustained savings remain unproven.

[Measured results and limits](../evidence/pr-25-collective-life/final-results.md)
separate all three windows, each stage of capability acquisition and actual use.
The campaign used 415 ordinary attempts, 38 world attempts and 16 counted
handoffs; known provider usage was 5,094,901 tokens, with 443,493 additional
unknown-usage reservations. Internal development/review usage is unobservable,
not zero. Ex1–ex10 archives and original inference remain unchanged. Actual final
review, current CI and guarded merge are recorded by the task completion receipt;
this section does not substitute for those gates.
