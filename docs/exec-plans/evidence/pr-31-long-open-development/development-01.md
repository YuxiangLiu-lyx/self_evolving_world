paid work rounds and two energy. A persistent job and exact escrow survive an
intervening action. Cancellation/death releases surviving unfinished material once;

free source replenishment or assumed germination. The natural dispersal updater
models propagules from living parents; harvested edible stacks do not retain an
established viability contract. This version therefore does not equate food identity
with a viable propagule or invent successful growth. A later functional request can
extend the mechanism after declaring its actual biological and resource model.
