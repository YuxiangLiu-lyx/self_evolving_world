# Round 24: cross-boundary residue movement

After the real round-23 partial clearing result, Lin requested transfer of the
actual 0.04 residue mass to a separately checked adjacent location. `world-64`
approved the bounded precursor. This is an independently requested dependency on
an existing physical result, not a preinstalled activity or proof of better supply.

`bounded-surface-residue-transfer@1` reuses the exact recorded surface and its
creator, one study round at 0.2 energy, and the existing work/geometry infrastructure.
A fresh examination costs one round/0.5 energy; at most two additional rounds cost
one energy and move at most 0.02 each. The receiver is an explicitly selected
radius-0.1 circle outside the radius-0.2 source, with center separation (0.3, 0.5].
Source, receiver and handled portions must be visibly within 0.65 of the actor.
No automatic destination, container, harvestable material or inventory is supplied.

The code checks the entire receiver and each swept-radius short contact route
against real terrain, people, plants and unrelated reservations. Source entries
and deposits use exact hundredth mass arithmetic with source-entry provenance.
Only a fully verified short route may commit atomically; no interpolated gap or
imaginary carrying path is supplied. Invalid identity/range fails before payment;
a performed examination may report blocked without moving matter. New obstructions
pause work before another charge. Both source and receiver locks are rechecked.

Paid rounds, exact receiver, source revision, allowance and material disposition
survive interruption and serialization. Cancel/death release only work locks.
Existing deposits persist; subsequent attempts inspect remaining mass rather than
resetting cover. The event reports planting compatibility as unknown and grants no
fertility, food, happiness or restoration. Actual ground geometry remains subject
to the unchanged planting/examination predicates and all their restrictions.

Replay overlays show actual nonzero source residues and destination deposits;
zero-mass retained provenance entries are not rendered as existing debris.
Targeted fixtures use the retained real source state, with explicitly isolated
positions and obstacles. They test conservation, repeat/allowance, restored work,
invalid range, paid blocked examination, dynamic obstruction, locks, cancellation,
death and fresh-scope isolation. Initial test expectations incorrectly ignored
normal food ageing and inserted an obstacle inside a reserved receiver; both
fixture errors and corrected results are retained. No fixture route is supplied
to the formal actor. Full adapter checks and exact prior-prefix reconstruction
remain mandatory before resumed formal execution.
