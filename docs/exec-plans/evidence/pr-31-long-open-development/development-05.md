Lin independently requested physical preparation after the recorded forest-floor
planting failure and site examination. `world-47` accepted a minimal, conditional
manual attempt. Its review confines all displaced matter to the original radius
0.2 footprint and requires the entire planting footprint to pass the original
bare-ground check. Those constraints do not guarantee successful clearance.

`small-soil-patch-preparation@1` requires one personal learning round, then one
fresh examination round (0.5 energy) and at most two paid work rounds (1 energy
each). Generic placement and persisted work reuse ex12/ex13 helpers; this is
recorded code reuse, not an independent reinvention of those helpers. New local
surface representation explicitly models finite forest litter (0.04 mass units;
hand capacity 0.02 per round), sourced to the exact terrain footprint. This is an
authored world extension, not biomass inferred from artwork or agent discovery.
It creates no food, harvestable stock, personal inventory or reward.

Moved cover remains in source-linked, non-food residue piles inside the footprint.
Consequently a completed two-round attempt can remain partial and unsuitable for
the original whole-footprint planting operation. Examination alone changes no
physical cover. The final outcome uses the same planting predicate; learning and
work completion never imply planting success. Overlapping representations are
rejected, and repeat work reuses remaining cover and residue without resetting
quantities. The local representation does not edit the terrain grid or alter
moisture, fertility, food sources, deeper substrate or environmental policy.

The exact job and transient physical lock survive pauses. Cancellation and death
release only that lock and preserve actual displacement; paid energy is not
refunded. Other individually trained actors can later attempt the same surface;
there is no automatic learning, assistance or transfer of personal records.
Generic replay overlays distinguish examination, real exposed area and residue.
No imaginary crops or successful planting appear.
