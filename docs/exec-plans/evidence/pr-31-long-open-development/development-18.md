# Round 71: joining prerequisite assessment and label readability

Lin independently requested a recoverable vine/two-branch joining trial after a
paid small-sample press. World-201 permits a geometry-assessment precursor when
piece segmentation, dimensions or contact mechanics are missing. The current run
has numeric material quantities and the earlier press finding, but no source-bound
continuous vine segments, branch centerlines or joining force law. Historical ex10
geometry code is retained and was inspected; its scope and material assumptions
are not automatically activated or claimed as measured facts in ex13.

This release implements **only the approved assessment precursor**, named
`recoverable-vine-joining-assessment@1`. Lin must personally study for two rounds,
paying0.2 energy each, then actually acquire two branch units and retain the nominated
vine. Begin validates exact owned identities, quantities, actual ground clearance
and reach. One round/0.5 energy examines the prerequisites. A shared stack must
contain two units when nominated for both branches; this does not establish that
the units already have independently known physical centerlines.

The atomic work ledger records intended allocations, paid effort and unresolved
geometry. Original stack identities are locked only during the examination and
released unchanged; no0.2-unit numerical split fabricates a cut in continuous vine.
No material is consumed, moved, duplicated or assigned to a joint. Full findings
remain personal. A persistent private trial record can be inspected at the actual
location for one round/0.2 energy; its rendering marker is an annotation, **not a
world object** that could accidentally obstruct subsequent work. Invalid references,
access and insufficient energy fail before charge. No active escrow remains at
completion, so there is no invented recovery, resume or maintenance operation.

Wrapping, actual separate branch poses, segment selection, contact/tension and
bounded separation remain explicitly missing development prerequisites. They are
not exposed as working interfaces or counted as completed functionality. There is
no force result, joint-strength inference, useful tool, reward or automatic stock.
This precursor is an interface/material-representation diagnostic, not a demonstrated
new productive capability. Further development must implement those mechanisms
before a future version can claim a real joined specimen or load response.

Isolated fixtures start from the actual round71 world, then use existing finite
gathering and explicit test-only positions to obtain branches. Tests cover personal
study, scope isolation, actual charges, shared-stack quantities, unchanged matter,
no reward, private findings, serialization and rejected fabricated/foreign identities
or unavailable force/recovery modes. The fixture's initial stand position was
inside a real source's collision radius and was corrected; no formal state changed.

An actual read-only browser preview of the hash-verified round56 prefix exposed
overlapping surface/residue labels at round32. The ex13 renderer now packs those
screen labels into separated rows without moving any entity or changing ex9's
layout. A targeted regression retains original x anchors and unrelated labels.
The final captured archive still requires selected browser checks, inspected
screenshots and a dynamic recording. Full adapter checks and identical prior
world/event hashes remain required before resuming formal execution.
