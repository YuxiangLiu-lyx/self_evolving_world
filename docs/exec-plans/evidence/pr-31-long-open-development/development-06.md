material, then distribute its actual tenths. Original material-lot provenance is

regeneration or inventory is inferred. Access/obstruction and exact remaining

paid progress. Cancel/death leave placed moss at its location and release the work

remains inconclusive and coverage unknown. No new reward account or qualified
information/practice event is emitted for these inconclusive findings.

  retrieves surviving reachable tenths for one round and 0.5 energy, after the work

  they do not inherit another person's knowledge or mastery.
