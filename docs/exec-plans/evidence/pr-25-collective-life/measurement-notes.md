# Measurement and interpretation boundaries

## Three retained windows

Report A's actual short window, B's preserved short window, and B's continuation
separately. The folder `windows/through-200` names the requested ceiling, not an
assertion that 200 rounds executed. Use `collectiveContinuation.round`, `.attempts`
and `.developments` for the actual B boundary. Calls issued before an unfinished
round belong to the phase in which they were reserved, not a reconstructed phase
chosen from their tick alone. Do not transfer unused allocations between arms.

Known provider input/output tokens, charged unknown reservations, and unobservable
conversation development/review usage are different quantities. Pending calls are
not failed calls. Development counts are current-conversation handoffs, including
failed validation, not a measurement of every internal coding-model invocation.
Plan-funded provider transport has no measured per-call monetary charge.

## Outcomes and denominators

One environment round advances eighteen independent bodies. Action counts measure
member-rounds, including progress rounds of a single operation. They are not
completed projects. Requests at an unsettled barrier are distinct from settled
`requested` events. An implemented interface, a personal learning completion, a
successful execution and subsequent measured savings are separate stages.

The attention queue produces real waiting while bodies metabolize. Its
`queuedRounds` are scheduling delay, not voluntary leisure. Rest, travel, feeding
and material acquisition may support both survival and projects; their counts
alone do not identify disposable labor time. The no-model calibration's optional
waiting fraction is a feasibility result, not a formal-agent free-time estimate.

A project record describes a member's claim and current plan. Persistent wording
is not completion. Follow its actual actions, inputs, outputs and interruptions.
A message demonstrates transmission when delivered, but a cooperative intention
requires corresponding participant actions before it counts as cooperation.
Shared capability discovery does not establish teaching, and multiple personal
masters do not establish skill diffusion through actual lessons.

Grounded information events count new qualified findings under the authored
feedback rule. Their ratio to known provider tokens is descriptive, excludes
unknown coding costs and is not a general intelligence or productivity score.
Tool or facility benefit requires actual subsequent use with recorded energy,
time, material, preservation or experience changes. Construction alone is not
sustained benefit. Distinct capability names are not distinct functions.

## Confounds and evidence

A and B have identical initial physical-state hashes and frozen ceilings, but
realized observation windows differ. A also encountered generic transport/context
repairs before B started. The bark contract correction activates only at B round
56; prior defective behavior remains in the short window. These technical and
ordering differences preclude a matched-horizon or single-component causal claim.
No seasonal conclusion is supported unless the actual replay crosses a season.

The full raw calls, checkpoints, source histories and replay events remain the
primary evidence. Human global replay views do not demonstrate model access to
private state. Isolated visual/physics fixtures show executable mechanisms and
are never formal discoveries. The unchanged historical archives are checked
without rerunning historical inference.

## Attention arithmetic

B can use up to three personal/public attention slots per environment round, not
eighteen calls. Nevertheless, a continuously busy three-slot queue consumes 120
ordinary attempts in forty rounds. Covering 1,600 rounds with eighteen members
and only 650 ordinary calls across the entire campaign would require at least
44.3 member-rounds per call even if A used none; the reserved 410-call B
continuation is tighter. These are budget arithmetic, not predictions or proof
of a scheduler defect. Compare actual plan duration, failures, queue waiting and
call reasons before concluding that behavior or information architecture caused
the short horizon. Tooling wait contributes no simulated hunger but does have
real unmetered development cost.
