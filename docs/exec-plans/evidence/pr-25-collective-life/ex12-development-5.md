# Ex12 development 5: actual non-ingestive sample opening

Cedar 3 requested a way to investigate an owned hazelnut without ingestion. The
round-80 world review approved a small mechanical opening and inspection precursor,
not a nutritional assay or a safety certificate. This extends the existing probe's
observations and destructive small trials with a retained, partly opened sample.
The original request, constrained approval and two-round personal learning remain
in the barrier and raw call journal.

`non-ingestive-food-inspection@1` is ex12-only. Starting reserves 0.1 actual
hazelnut unit and one owned reusable stone/flint. Its intrinsic contact resistance
must meet the sample's represented resistance, with usable, uncontaminated contact.
Two active rounds each pay 0.5 energy. Every increment verifies actual access and
a clear reachable adjacent work position; departure or insufficient energy pauses
without another reservation. A persistent material exposure fraction retains paid
partial work. Completion returns the same-mass sample and tool. Cancellation or
death releases actual partial contents once at the work position, without refunds.
The existing whole-unit ground pickup limitation also applies to these small
released portions; no fractional recovery capability is silently granted.

Composition, origin, age and chemical quantities stay unchanged. The engine's
composition audit remains outside ordinary input and emitted findings. Inspection
costs a real round and 0.2 energy, retains a dated personal observation and reports
unknown nutrition, toxicity, anatomy and food safety explicitly. No extra anatomical
or spoilage appearance is invented. Baseline aging continues because the physical
applicability of the crushed-root model to opened hazelnut is unvalidated. No
preservation or edible-kernel yield is claimed.

Generic ex11 escrow/work and ex10 contact helpers are code reuse, not inherited
skills or knowledge. A first completed paid opening can establish mechanical
access through the unchanged canonical understanding source. An initial fixture
revealed an additional generic practice award for the same fact; marking this
new finding ineligible for that second award preserves the specific reviewed
experience contract. The explicit regression now checks the actual sole positive
source. Registration, inspection, partial work and repeated equivalent openings
award no new understanding.

Six targeted tests pass for physical conservation and age, paid duration and
complete-execution repeat, movement/energy pause and restoration, ownership and
material/contact failures, overlap rejection, cancellation/death, actual reward
and deduplication, private knowledge and scope/personal mastery. Initial fixture
failures incorrectly treated an identical continuing action as a restart and an
already-dead actor as a new death; the fixtures now test changed-input rejection
and a real starvation transition. They were not accepted as passing results.
Type, lint, architecture and build checks pass at their recorded versions.

Four final browser scenes were personally inspected: unchanged sample inspection,
50% paid opening, completed 100% opening, and cancelled partial material on the

and exact 80-round reconstruction before activation as source revision 5. No
