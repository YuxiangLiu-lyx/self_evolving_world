# Collective life: retained results

The campaign produced limited material accumulation, persistent personal projects,
a refusal followed by useful information exchange, and one real tool handover.
It did not establish stable division of labor, finished communal production,
seasonal adaptation or sustained savings. Neither short arm reached its requested
200 rounds. B stopped at 136 of the cumulative 1,600-round target because the next
complete request reservation would exceed its frozen continuation token budget.
The world is paused with all eighteen people alive and unfinished work preserved.

## What was provided and what was chosen

The authored baseline contains sixteen interfaces: navigate, rest, wait, pickup,
gather, eat, drop, give, message, request, learn, probe, consult, exchange, council
and teach. Safe berry/root knowledge, eighteen human bodies, meetings, mild seeded
B tendencies, feedback rules and attention scheduling were also authored. A and B
share physical genesis but use separate fresh capability scopes with
`inheritSkills=false`; neither inherited generated skills or knowledge.

Agents chose their project goals, requests, messages, paths and actions. The world
review and implementation supplied detailed recipes, dimensional models, learning
durations and execution costs. A functional request is therefore not evidence
that a member independently invented every recipe detail. Tests supply materials,
mastery and trial routes only inside isolated fixtures; their successes are not
formal behavior. Learning changes personal world state, not model weights.

## Request, implementation, learning and use

A settled three requests and retained seven more at its unfinished barrier.
Three actual world decisions approved two developments and one same-run reuse.
Two capabilities were implemented; Cedar 2 alone completed learning and used food
preparation once at round 7, conserving one root into ten portions. None was eaten
before the boundary. No shelter was built.

B settled 37 request events; its action records include 38 request rounds, which
are not identical to review count. Thirty-four world calls returned 38 decisions:
26 reuse and 12 develop decisions, including batched equivalent requests. Twelve
handoffs comprised ten capability deliveries, one failed validation attempt and
one repair of the previously reviewed bark contract. Eleven nonbaseline
interfaces were implemented and individually mastered at least once, totaling
48 personal mastery completions. This is not eleven different inventions:
preparation/preservation variants and their diagnostics refine existing functions.
Five physical function families plus inventory access is a more conservative
classification. Only actual use and its consequences establish benefit.

## Representative evidence chains

- **Accumulated material to unfinished facility:** Ash 1 completed two paid
  separations at rounds 60 and 80. World-294 approved its consolidation request;
  world-324 reused Birch 6's independently requested inventory capability after
  separate personal learning. Round 101 listed all ten carried stacks; round 104
  combined two 0.7 lots. Builds at 113, 118 and 123 failed real overlap. Its new
  world-411 clearance request led to personal study and a passing site at 132.
  At 136, construction reserved two real stones and one bark, paid one energy and
  completed one of two work increments at (7, 9.3). Object
  `object-136-ash-1-3973` and job `manual-136-ash-1-3974` persist with one increment
  remaining. This is a real dependency chain, not a completed or useful tray.
- **Failure to usable explanation:** Birch 3 and Birch 5 encountered the one-unit
  bark rule despite holding 0.3. World-309 batched their independent fractional
  requests. Initial surface checks failed actual placement. Birch 5 requested
  explanation and its own adjacent site passed all thirteen checks at 115;
  Birch 3 later used the same diagnostic after individual study. Neither had
  completed root preparation by the boundary.
- **Refusal and information effect:** Ash 3 declined Ash 4's stone request at 87
  to keep both stones for its own project. Ash 4 accepted the refusal and asked
  for source information. Ash 3 sent dated locations at 91; Ash 4 then selected
  a targeted search and completed paid sample opening at 122. This is grounded
  choice adjustment, not coercive role assignment or a successful loan.
- **Real cooperation with delay:** Cedar 2 offered a spare stone at 104; Cedar 4
  accepted at 106. Repeated approaches hit actual bodies. The one-stone transfer
  finally executed at 130. Cedar 4 acknowledged receipt at 136 and planned the
  paid sample operation, but it had not executed before the world paused. The
  transfer is real; productive use and return remain unobserved.
- **Bounded investigation:** Cedar 3's round-55 vine test found the chosen load
  unsupported; a subsequent wet trial failed for lack of local water. Hazelnut
  openings by Ash 5 at 90, Cedar 3 at 104 and Ash 4 at 122 changed actual samples
  after paid work. They established access only, without food safety, nutrition,
  edible yield or sterilization claims.

## Survival, attention and experience

All eighteen people in each arm finished at health 100 with no lost lives. B's
final satiety range was 37.6–85.6, median 64.6; energy was 22.69–40. Final happiness
was 7.05–10.02/100, median 8.77. Slow mental state remained near neutral at
0.01229–0.02303; diet monotony ranged 0–1 with median 0.04762. B recorded 106
substantial meal-experience events, 80 root and 26 berry; small intakes are not
extra diversity discoveries. No processed meal was consumed. Actual rest
recovered 1,218.71 energy across 308 rounds, including recovery caps; mental
multipliers ranged 1–1.00229, a small effect rather than a major efficiency gain.

B's 2,448 member-round actions included 1,005 wait, 518 navigation, 308 rest,
133 eat, 104 gather, 116 extraction and 57 messages. The attention ledger contains
954 queued member-rounds, 38.97% of all member-rounds, with maximum wait six within
the frozen limit eight. Waiting is not voluntary leisure. The separate no-model
calibration's 68.27% optional time proves baseline feasibility under its fixture
controller, not spare time demonstrated by the formal agents.

There were 115 failed action events: 62 occupied navigation goals, six simultaneous
resource/body conflicts, 27 reference failures, and other missing prerequisites,
wrong process modes or unavailable work conditions. Fourteen ordinary call reasons
explicitly named reference errors; this overlaps rather than adds to event counts.
No duplicate material settlement was found in reconstructed state. One decision
reason mentioned a message alongside rest, animal alert and wellbeing; its cost
cannot be attributed to the message alone. Two public coordination responses,
ordinary-290 and ordinary-367, used an invalid combined member identity. Both were
charged and rejected, with no accepted public assignments. There were two actual
local reports and six shared-record reads, but no accepted lesson or intergroup
interaction. No injury-driven risk update or confirmation-driven trust change
occurred. Shared capability reuse was real; interpersonal teaching was not.

## Limits and next experiment

The frozen resource calibration supported basic survival, but this observation
ended before the first 200-round seasonal transition. Many projects remained
similar and acquisition/diagnostics dominated capability growth. No finished
facility, preservation, long-term labor saving, entertainment, durable division of
labor or population-level institution was demonstrated. Preparation alone was
not designed to grant nutrition or productivity bonuses. The unfinished tray,
material provenance and resumed jobs provide limited evidence of accumulation,
while sustained useful development remains unproven.

The most useful next experiment is a separately authorized, matched 200-round
A/B campaign after an isolated execution-contract preflight based on these exact
failures. Measure whether longer effective plans, precise references, useful
placement feedback and valid public proposals fit the same budget before adding
more ecology. Freeze both arms after preflight; do not feed fixture solutions or
recipes to ordinary agents. This recommendation was not executed here.
