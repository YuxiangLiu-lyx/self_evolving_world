# Retained short observation windows

The equal preregistered allocations were ceilings, not guaranteed 200-round
observations. A (ex11) stopped after seven completed rounds on its four-handoff
limit. B (ex12) stopped after 37 completed rounds because the next complete
request could not fit its 1,300,000-token short allocation. Neither arm reached
200 rounds. B continues from this actual checkpoint using the separately frozen
long allocation; unused short budgets do not transfer.

B's two failed ordinary attempts, 12 and 83, retained provider overload errors.
Both count against calls and token reservations. The second B development
attempt failed architecture validation; the third corrected the dependency and
passed without replaying inference or advancing a world round between attempts.
Internal conversation development and review usage is unknown, not zero, and
outside the measured provider ceiling.

B's boundary records contain actual messages about observed material locations,
requirements, and untested plans. Some members explicitly deferred crushing
because the reviewed contract increases exposed decay unless a preservation
process subsequently works. These are factual communication and expressed
judgment, not demonstrated preservation benefits. Ten members had mastered
at least one generated interface, but no completed root apparatus or material
processing output existed at this boundary. No public report, public coordination
call, teaching acceptance, trust update or death occurred. Most projects concern
food processing and its prerequisites; distinct names are not distinct functions.

The bark interface's first version records newly verified paid separation but
omits its link to the existing understanding source. This was found after its
accepted round-28 handoff. It was corrected at the retained round-56 versioned
development barrier, without retroactive rewards; the original short window
remains unchanged. Its existing tests establish
physical work and deduplication, but did not assert the actual satisfaction
source; that test gap is explicitly retained.

This campaign does not provide a matched 200-round architecture comparison.
A was disproportionately truncated by transport, context and review failures;
B started after generic technical repairs. Short duration, unequal realized
horizons, execution order and implementation overhead confound comparisons.
The no-model 1,600-round calibration establishes a feasible authored survival
path, not evidence that formal agents followed it or achieved long-term survival.
