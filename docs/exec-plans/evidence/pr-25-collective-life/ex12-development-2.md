# Ex12 development 2: a requested material precursor

## Actual dependency and reviewed minimum

At the round-28 barrier, Birch 5 requested usable bark after personally learning
root preparation and failing to identify its required implements. World call 92
approved `bark-extraction@1`, with two rounds of personal study. The retained
`region-1-canopy-18` has exposed branch stock, not represented trunk tissue.
Installation therefore supplies neither trunk bark nor tools, stock or mastery.
The standing tree keeps its geometry, health and existing source state.

The implemented precursor assesses actual material and peels an owned gathered
branch using an actually owned, personally inspected stone or flint contact.
Tool hardness uses the existing intrinsic predicate; paid assessment records
observable contact geometry, not an invented absolute-hardness certificate.
The generic ex10 contact implementation is explicitly attributed code reuse;
its historical abilities, knowledge and extension packages are not activated.
Ex11's generic exact-reference and escrow implementation is also reused.

Otherwise unspecified intact branches receive the review's explicit conservative
layer model on actual processing: at most ten percent of existing mass is outer
material. Existing processing or represented contradictory composition blocks the
default. Peeling never creates new biomass or resets age. Water, solids, fuel,
nutrition, toxicity and source history are retained in per-lot properties. A
peeled residual cannot re-enter an intact-branch recipe or regenerate its layer.
Future consumers must use the shared per-lot material profile for these outputs.
No combustion capability is introduced by this handoff.

Two work-round equivalents and two energy are paid per original branch unit.
Fractional quantities and partially peeled inputs pay proportional remaining
effort, while the executor receives integer remaining simulation rounds. Inputs
and a real reusable implement are reserved once. Replacing references during
unfinished work fails explicitly. Pausing retains progress; cancellation and
death leave actual outputs, remaining substrate and tool at the work location.
The world has soft carrying costs, not a hard capacity limit; completion retains
actual inventory without inventing a capacity rule.

Public quantities retain the engine's exact-tenths accounting. Usable bark rounds
down and sub-unit mass remains a separate real `bark-fines` lot. Four standard
branches of total mass 4.4 produce at most 1.4 usable bark units (mass 0.42),
0.02 fines mass and 3.96 residual wood mass after eight paid rounds. One branch
cannot meet the earlier preparation ability's one-bark-unit requirement. Whole-unit
baseline pickup/transfer remains a limitation for ground fractions after
cancellation; those fractions remain physical rather than disappearing or being
rounded upward. Full completion keeps the actual fractional output owned.
