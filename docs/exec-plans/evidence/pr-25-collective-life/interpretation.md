# What the collective campaign can establish

This record distinguishes executable foundations, observed choices and unresolved
interpretation. Exact final counts belong to the companion analyses and retained
`results.json`; this document explains the evidence standard.

## Authored mechanisms and actual choices

The eighteen bodies, three groups, baseline food knowledge, meeting locations,
attention policy, feedback rules and sixteen initial interfaces are authored.
The small seeded B preferences affect bounded experience and are supplied as facts
to personal decisions; they do not prescribe a role. Injury-based risk change and
confirmation-based trust change require the relevant event. A run with no injury
or verified received finding cannot demonstrate those updates in use.

The model chose food-processing questions, material investigations, temporary
loans, refusals, information exchange and occasional exploration. The detailed
stone/bark recipes, dimensional assumptions and costs were completed by world
review and implementation. A request for practical food preparation is therefore
an independent functional request, not an independently invented recipe. Each
new implementation is scoped to its own arm and still requires personal study.

An implementation shared within B can support another member without another
coding handoff. This occurred with inventory inspection and the fractional
preparation extension. It does not prove person-to-person teaching: capability
lookup is an authored public channel, while an actual lesson requires offer,
acceptance and paid study. No member gains mastery from group membership alone.

## Representative chains

- Ash 1 repeatedly returned to its preservation project after food and recovery.
  Two paid separations produced separate 0.7-unit bark lots. World review 294
  accepted its consolidation request; review 324 reused Birch 6's independently
  requested inventory access. After personal learning, round 101 read all ten
  carried stacks and round 104 combined the actual two lots into 1.4 units,
  retaining provenance. This is accumulated material and a working dependency,
  but not yet preserved food or reduced future subsistence labor.
- Birch 3 and Birch 5 independently encountered the whole-unit preparation
  requirement. Review 309 combined their fractional-surface requests into one
  upgrade. Their first assessments at rounds 103 and 102 were unsuitable. Birch
  5 requested an explanation after repeated failures; review 361 led to separate
  personal diagnostic learning. Its chosen adjacent site passed all thirteen
  checks at round 115. The new explanation resolved a real interface ambiguity;
  the original clearance rule and physical stock were not relaxed.
- Ash 3 declined Ash 4's stone loan at round 87 because both stones were reserved
  in its intended tray project. Ash 4 accepted the refusal and asked for source
  information. Ash 3 supplied dated locations at round 91; Ash 4 selected a
  targeted search, acquired its own stone and completed a paid sample opening at
  round 122. This is a traceable information effect and voluntary disagreement,
  not forced conflict, shared inventory or a successful loan.
- Cedar 2 offered a temporary spare stone at round 104 and Cedar 4 accepted at 106. Repeated delivery plans initially failed real body collision. The one-stone
  transfer finally executed at round 130. That is actual cooperation with a real
  delay and conserved inventory, separate from any later productive use or return.
- Cedar 3's round-55 vine trial supplied useful negative evidence about a tested
  load. Later hazelnut opening by Ash 5 (90), Cedar 3 (104) and Ash 4 (122) changed
  real samples after paid work. The feedback established mechanical interior
  access only. Nutrition, toxicity and safety remained unknown; no edible yield
  or safe-food discovery can be inferred.

## Friction and negative findings

Repeated material requests, exact-reference mistakes, missing compact-context
identities and occupied destinations consumed model attention. Some navigation
plans targeted the meeting center occupied by another member, although attendance
only required being nearby. Several construction plans placed the worksite at the
performer's own location. These are real execution failures, not successful
construction or evidence that collision should be disabled. New diagnostic
mechanisms arose from actual requests; fixture locations were never supplied to
ordinary agents.

Public coordination was constrained by genuinely shared reports. Cedar 5 supplied
the first at round 81. The public responses at calls 290 and 367 put six member
identities in one proposal field and were rejected by the exact identity contract.
Neither response changed personal plans or created accepted public work. Both
calls were charged, and their original contents remain available. Schema-level
prevention of that multi-identity mistake is an unresolved interface limitation.

Model statements such as a willingness to collaborate or a claimed benefit must
be checked against transfers, work, consumption and subsequent state. A retained
project surviving interruption is stronger than a single ambitious response, but
still weaker than a completed productive process. Similar processing projects
across many members do not establish organized division of labor.

## Comparison and cost limits

A completed seven rounds; B's short checkpoint completed 37. Neither realized the
planned matched 200-round short comparison. Generic transport and context repairs
also occurred before B began. These unequal horizons and engineering differences
prevent attributing behavior to privacy, preferences, intention or any individual
component. B continuation is a separate descriptive observation of the same saved
world, not a third independent trial.

Attention-queue waiting is neither voluntary leisure nor available productive
labor. Sustained processes execute without additional inference, but frequent
material and navigation failures still force replanning. Known provider tokens,
unknown charged reservations and unmetered current-conversation development costs
must remain separate. Progress per known token is a narrow event ratio, not an
all-cost efficiency claim. No seasonal or long-term civilization claim is supported
by an observation that ends before its first season transition.

## One next experiment

In a separately authorized campaign, first test whether an improved execution
contract can deliver matched 200-round A/B observations within the same budget.
Use the retained invalid public member field, occupied destination, precise item
reference and whole/partial-material failures as isolated preflight cases. Freeze
both arms only after those fixtures pass, retain fresh capability scopes, and
compare actual useful outputs and information-dependent behavior at matched
horizons. Do not add more ecological mechanisms or inject the successful fixture
recipes into ordinary context. This recommendation is not executed in this task.

## Physical usefulness is a separate question

The implemented preparation functions conserve nutrition; crushing alone is not
a productivity or preservation bonus and can accelerate deterioration. Drying
benefits require an actual loaded, sufficiently dry sample. The world review's
chosen stone/bark recipes and extra diagnostic interfaces added real acquisition,
learning and execution costs. A functional approval therefore does not establish
that the proposed solution improves everyday life. Much of the observed
capability growth addresses prerequisites and interface uncertainty, rather than
reducing subsequent subsistence work. This implementation-level cost is part of
the exploratory result and cannot be attributed solely to personal preferences
or to the members' willingness to cooperate.
