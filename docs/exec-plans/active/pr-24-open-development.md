# PR-24: Fresh worlds and cumulative open development

## Requirement summary

Build on main 9290af4 and merged PR #17. Reuse the validated execution,
experience, replay and task infrastructure, while giving the next real world a
strict fresh skill baseline and foundations for cumulative development. Observe
early behavior under fixed budgets without requiring inventions or an era.

## Requirements

- R1: Default the new policy to inheritSkills=false with an explicit authored baseline allowlist. Isolate registries, search, mastery, learning, objects, processes, rewards, events, pending work and extension state across experiments; retain historical source and identical replay.
- R2: Permit within-run discovery and individual learning of implemented abilities, preserve them on resume, and require new requests, review, validated development and study for equivalent historical functions. Record any implementation reuse without calling it independent reinvention.
- R3: Allow self-directed questions and projects under health, tolerable risk, understanding, reusable capability and personal/shared living goals. Persist actor-selected evidence, uncertainty, goal, progress, outputs, missing conditions, next step, pause reason and measured benefit; never choose projects for actors.
- R4: Provide bounded event-grounded curiosity, useful failure evidence, capability progress, sustained engagement and actual reciprocal social experience. Deduplicate information and rewards; separate beliefs, relations and physical facts; preserve neutral slow mood, nutrition and basic recovery.
- R5: Implement functional plant reproduction/growth/seasonality, differentiated animal diet/fear/grouping/reproduction/injury/approach behavior, and discoverable material strength/flexibility/water/combustion/heat properties. Supply inexpensive generic observation and physical trials without granting mature skills.
- R6: Connect soil, terrain, water and weather to growth, travel and physical effects. Preregister bounded local drought and waterlogging with visible onset, duration and recovery, preserving relocation and accumulated assets without adaptive rescue or difficulty changes.
- R7: Support persistent costed processes with inputs, conditions, progress, outputs and terminal rules after actors leave. Record actual capability/material/tool/knowledge dependencies and provenance, maintenance, measured benefits and newly feasible actions; no era unlock schedule or fixed activity whitelist.
- R8: Review missing mechanisms as the smallest feasible version with prerequisites, costs, study, experience and replayable visual effects. Reuse this world's equivalent rules; actual material, energy, participant actions and consent remain required. Use current-conversation development barriers only.
- R9: Keep repeated actions, ecology, animals, processes and feedback deterministic in code. Bound model context using compact current state, projects, experience and discoverable ability summaries with on-demand detail; retain complete raw history and measure bytes, tokens, latency and failures.
- R10: Verify isolation A/B/A-resume, same-run learning, probes, continuous operations, persistent processes, conservation, multilevel composition, reward deduplication and recoverability without model calls. Keep fixtures, routes and recipes outside formal context and emergent-behavior counts.
- R11: Allocate the next exN once and run three actors toward 1,600 rounds with fixed recorded model/seed/baseline/parameters and 650 ordinary, 60 world and 20 development attempts including failures. Calibrate the real fresh baseline offline before first inference, then freeze policy and save every 200 rounds.
- R12: Retain resumable state at observation/budget boundaries, original failures and exact technical-repair source boundaries. No automatic budget extension, historical inference, policy retuning or rerun selection; the window boundary is not the end of the world.
- R13: Analyze actual questions/tests, functional novelty, project resumption, dependent creations, sustained benefits, non-survival activity, cross-actor use and all measurable costs. Distinguish authored baseline, fixtures, independent requests, code reuse, mastery and real execution; do not infer inevitable civilization or causal effects from one run.
- R14: Synchronize English code, tests, protocol, feature record, architecture, ADR and reports; complete scoped acceptance, actual image/file review, PR, current-head quality/pr-policy, authorized merge and independent integration through the existing harness.

## Scope and exclusions

Versioned fresh continuous forest policy; pure core ecology, knowledge, process
and experience contracts; compact simulator contexts and persistent projects;
shared 2D visuals; isolated validation; one real fixed observation and complete
archives. No new harness framework, recursive developer service, model-weight
training, prescribed development route, automatic mature skills, fabricated
inference, historical archive rewrite, rescue, mandatory invention or era.
Architecture may retain state across tens of thousands of rounds, but this run
does not establish that all performance or behavior scales to that horizon.

selected archive/browser checks, unfiltered code tests and current review must
pass; no passing label substitutes for remote CI or verified integration.
