# Continuous World Protocol

Version 7 uses `continuous-v1`. It is an independent experimental world, not a reinterpretation of ex1-ex6 or a revision of the founding catalogue. The [execution plan](exec-plans/completed/pr-18-continuous-world.md) records requirements and current acceptance; [PRODUCT](PRODUCT.md) owns feature status. Entry remains the [programmatic task coordinator](TASK_HARNESS.md).

## Physics and observation

The initial world is 24 by 24 continuous distance units with three actors at unequal floating-point positions, a five-unit observation radius, established perennial food, loose material and persistent natural geometry. A simulation round advances one fixed time unit after a same-start-state action barrier. Display time, model latency, development and review never advance metabolism. Coordinates and velocities are real numbers. The navigation solver uses a half-unit numerical search mesh and line-of-sight simplification; this discretizes route search only. Actual positions, destinations, travel, costs, swept collision and interaction are continuous. Persisted noninteger state is normalized to eight decimal places so Node and browser replay agree despite floating-point math-library differences.

Actor bodies are discs. Objects expose circles or oriented rectangles, collision, interaction distance and optional local entrance coordinates. A reserved footprint can prevent overlapping construction while remaining walkable. Swept movement uses actual geometry; rectangle collision conservatively expands its bounds by the actor radius. Concurrent shared writes and intersecting actor paths reject together. Private navigation uses observed geometry and dated memory, never hidden object IDs or a complete private map. Unknown obstacles can stop execution; newly observed geometry changes the next route. One waypoint segment advances per round, so sharp corners may consume additional time. This explicit numerical approximation is not frame-rate smoothing.

Movement spends energy by actual distance and carried mass. Baseline rest restores four energy, gathering and consumption spend energy, food restores satiety without healing, and action settlement precedes starvation and the established two-unit hunger loss. Initial health is 100, satiety 80 and energy 40. Thermal state relaxes toward deterministic ambient conditions and affects physical comfort; extreme temperature damages health. Declared insulation actually slows heat exchange. No baseline shelter or automatic climate rescue exists. Surface sunlight and groundwater support established perennial replenishment below standing capacity; they are declared environmental inputs, not runtime gifts. Multiple objects and resource stacks can occupy compatible physical space without a one-resource-per-cell rule.

## Goals, learning and wellbeing

Ordinary goals are only health, survival and happiness. Optional intention, notes and preferences are authored by each actor; no occupation, development project or activity examples are supplied. Initial fundamental interfaces are authored survival knowledge and excluded from discoveries. Learning is world-internal mastery and experience, not model-weight training.

Happiness is physical comfort up to 10 plus other satisfaction up to 90. Actual health, satiety, energy and temperature determine physical comfort. Other sources begin empty and require reviewed successful event triggers, conditions, amplitude, cap, duration or maintenance, repetition mode and cooldown. Sources may refresh, add within a cap or diminish. Repeated enjoyment is allowed. Requests, registration, study, labels and narrative cannot award points. Equivalent sources cannot be registered again under another name. Health and survival remain separate objectives; death sets total wellbeing to zero.

A public implemented interface does not grant personal mastery. Study advances only in simulation rounds. Long-lived plans use explicit repeated actions, per-step conditions and guards, whole-plan conditions and actor-selected interrupt types. Normal hunger decline does not interrupt. Failed execution, newly crossed urgent thresholds, completed study and explicit interrupts require a new decision. The scheduler evaluates the actor's chosen program; it does not select food, projects, destinations or strategies. A private memory adapter can be enabled only by an actor who mastered its interface. It receives a frozen personal observation, not the world, and cannot change protected goals or call accounts.

## Versioned development

The world reviewer batches same-round functional requests and reuses equivalent interfaces. Reasonable gaps enter the existing current-conversation handoff. No activity category or era name is a categorical rejection rule. Difficult demands need actual prerequisites and a minimal executable precursor. Social and reproductive mechanisms additionally require actual participant actions/consent, persistent identities and relationships, costs and care; new identities share budgets. No such mechanism is claimed implemented merely because the review contract allows it. 3D remains a possible future request, not a delivered feature.

Packages declare immutable identity/version, dependencies, public typed interfaces, learning, executable handlers, processes, optional object-state schemas, visual component names and optional migrations. Installations occur at the preserved barrier, and do not grant finished objects, material stock, happiness or personal mastery. Generic object/process identities support paid creation, removal, repair, conversion, recycling and replacement implementations. No six-operation interpreter, twelve-round plan, forty-eight-object cap, three-slot resource niche or universal six-yield lifetime applies in this protocol.

Development declarations list every changed source path, packages, requester/interface assignments and an implementation review. The validator retains source, verifies the original world/call/barrier baseline, runs type, architecture, full unit and build checks, and reconstructs every saved round with identical state and event hashes. The former five-file allowlist is replaced by declared changes across mechanism, geometry, schema, renderer and asset modules. Protected experiment policy, controller, transport, source verifier, historical foundation and dependencies require a separate run version. Existing source versions cannot be deleted. A changed package needs a registered renderer; generic geometry rendering is a diagnostic fallback, not proof of a new visual capability.

Explicit computational protection bounds work per round, input bytes, nesting and serialized state. Exhaustion checkpoints and pauses the whole world. These are engineering thresholds, not natural object, skill or civilization limits. Host-code extensions remain trusted reviewed repository code; this is not an untrusted JavaScript sandbox.

## Calls, journals and windows

The first window targets 1,000 rounds with observational checkpoints at 200, 400, 600, 800 and 1,000. Stage reports never enter actor contexts. No early stop for lack of activity, resource rescue, stage hint or selective rerun is permitted. An isolated 1,000-round feasibility oracle establishes a viable initial survival route, but its policy, routes and extension fixtures are excluded from real inputs and discovery evidence.

Shared limits are 400 ordinary attempts, 60 world attempts and 20 pre-counted current-conversation development handoffs. Failures/retries count. Each network attempt is saved before inference; its immutable raw response/failure record precedes checkpoint advancement. Recovery consumes a saved accepted result once and refuses to repeat an uncertain attempt. Role, reason, timing and available token usage are retained. Developer-internal model calls/tokens and overall preparation/review costs are not directly metered; they remain unknown, never zero.

Each committed round has a durable local write-ahead record. Archive segments contain a starting world and deterministic actions, reviews, package registrations, task/memory changes and events. Checksummed segments, raw calls, scheduled checkpoints and retained source/assets are separately indexed. Source history preserves activation boundaries and validation logs. The observed window can end in `window-complete`, or a global budget/engineering pause; development is a separate nonadvancing phase. `extendContinuousWindow` validates a later target while preserving all counts, identities, tasks and history. Already captured windows cannot be overwritten. A later authorized continuation uses a new task intent with `experiment.mode: "continuous"`, `allocation: "reuse"`, the existing `id` and a larger `rounds` target. Record the actual reuse instruction and the existing plan exception contract, then run `pnpm task -- start next-window-intent.json`. The same coordinator preserves all original capture bytes under `windows/through-N`, records a window-history index, and extends without resetting counters. Missing local round caches are reconstructed from retained segments. Merely reopening playback never continues inference. To resume the current development barrier, submit the validated declaration through `pnpm task -- submit TASK_ID response.json`, then use `pnpm task -- next TASK_ID`; both commands are existing coordinator entries.

## Game observatory and evidence

`/?experiment=exN` (also `/observatory.html?experiment=exN`) opens the illustrated top-down view. Terrain, riverbanks, natural objects, actors, carried/ground resources and persistent facilities derive from actual saved state. Camera zoom, pan, follow, minimap, before/after, event focus, actor vitals, actual happiness sources, task/memory and object provenance are inspection tools. Event emphasis animates recorded interactions; it cannot advance simulation or infer unobserved growth. Model inputs contain structured observations, not images or frames.

The original interactive grid inspector remains at `/classic.html`. The same scene renderer can display old archives, explicitly labeled as historical grid simulation. Their positions, rules and hashes remain unchanged. Happiness and continuous temperature are not fabricated for historical protocols. Version 7 loads checksummed segments on demand with a bounded segment cache; the legacy 500-action and replay-size constraints remain intact for old importers.

The report distinguishes requested, reviewed, implemented, studied, successfully executed and sustained benefits. Production is distinct from consumption and reinvestment; shared-object use is distinct from explicit social interaction. This combined exploratory run does not isolate the causal effect of any one mechanism. Full validation and actual screenshot review remain required before publication and guarded merge.
