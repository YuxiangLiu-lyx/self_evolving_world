# ADR-0019: Versioned Forest Lives and Experience

Date: 2026-11-20

Status: Accepted

## Context

The next authorized experiment requires finite lives, differentiated ecology, seasonal risk, actual experience feedback and readable 2D exploration. Ex7 retained zero mental sources, invalid event subscriptions and stale inventory references. Its successful historical reconstruction must remain unchanged.

## Decision

Add an immutable forest-v1 configuration marker inside the existing continuous replay envelope. Pure forest foundation, environment and lifecycle modules extend the continuous kernel through explicit guarded branches. Genesis owns 26 physical resource definitions and finite ecological sites. Shared enclosure geometry supplies walls, door passage, visibility and interior effects. The renderer projects saved states with Canvas 2D while historical protocols keep their existing view and transitions.

Life accounts, damage-grounded death records and inventory transfer settle at the existing round barrier. Important memory is retained separately from routine events. Event declarations constrain model schemas and subscriptions, and current state invalidates obsolete action references. Every world review explicitly evaluates experience and visuals alongside functional effects. Trusted packages can add events and death cleanup while preserving personal study, protected accounts and source verification.

A computation-only pause can enter a counted technical development barrier through the task coordinator. The original failed capture and unsettled actions are preserved, with no invented actor request or extra inference. Exact protected-source hash pairs permit the narrowly reviewed repair; full prefix reconstruction and unchanged budgets remain mandatory. Perception fixes activate through a separate package marker, preserving preceding evidence.

The real round-331 food-assessment request adds version-gated fixed-point tenths and unique sample escrow. Installation grants no material; subsequent steps normalize quantities losslessly. Existing whole-unit transfers remain compatible, while fractional transfers and death drops conserve provenance and mass. Evidence and rewards settle after actual environmental injury and mortality so a harmful or interrupted exposure cannot be rewarded as successful monitoring. This package boundary also activates current carrier positions in observations without changing earlier replay hashes.

The real round-464 shelter request uses the same escrow and shared enclosure geometry. Cold protection scales the below-comfort cooling component of body-temperature change, preserving ambient conditions and unsheltered results; actual cold injury remains possible. This separates thermal relief from construction and permits the review's no-health-loss experience condition without granting immunity. Protection expires with simulation time, while physical walls persist. Package evidence is projected once into bounded decision summaries; complete dated operation records stay in replay, including adverse outcomes.

The real round-469 active-warming request adds paid stationary exertion after ordinary physiological settlement, never erasing prior damage. Explicitly validated alternative event triggers share the thermal-refuge source, cap and cooldown instead of multiplying rewards. At round 619 a real route-diagnosis request exposed a continuous corner path missed by the broad grid. A bounded local visibility graph now supplements that grid under a new package marker; every segment uses authoritative swept collision and current observed occupancy. Paid inspection distinguishes reachable, proven blocked and unknown, with dated geometry and no reward. This preserves legitimate occupied-door failures and avoids claiming search exhaustion proves physical impossibility.

## Alternatives

Rewriting continuous-v1 globally would invalidate ex7 hashes. A separate replay/storage/controller stack would duplicate working provenance and development recovery. A fixed recipe list or forced exploration strategy would prevent the requested autonomous observation. Model-controlled animals and frame-driven inference would add unnecessary cost and nondeterminism.

## Reasons

The opt-in marker preserves historical semantics and the current task harness. Physical environmental factors can respond to future reviewed changes without special seasonal prohibitions. One geometry definition prevents visual doors and actual collision from disagreeing. Explicit experience review addresses the observed zero-feedback root without awarding proposals or prescribing ordinary activities.

## Costs

Additional ecology and visibility increase replay work. Rectangular enclosure interiors and deterministic animal templates simplify biology and architecture. Trusted extensions still require substantive review, including escrow cleanup; this is not a sandbox. Isolated fixtures establish mechanisms only. Internal development costs remain unmetered.

## Future consequences

Further geometry, materials and capabilities remain demand-driven versioned development. New physics requires preserved source and replay compatibility. Follow-up causal claims require separately designed controls or multiple retained seeds; this single run cannot establish them.
