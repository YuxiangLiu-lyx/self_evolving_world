This independent `coevolution-v1` protocol and version-6 archive preserve versions 1-5. [PRODUCT](PRODUCT.md) owns feature status. The [execution plan](exec-plans/completed/pr-17-development-coevolution.md) records scope and actual acceptance. ## Preregistered configuration

The immutable [configuration](../packages/world-core/src/coevolution-schema.ts) specifies three actors, 24 by 24 cells, vision radius five, seed 20261119, and a target of 180 simultaneous rounds. Starts are eight legal steps apart; all can reach the common area. Nearby forest, bank and rocky environments differ without assigned roles. Initial health100, satiety80 and energy40 leave exploration time. Known safe initial foods and basic eating are authored starting knowledge and excluded from autonomous discoveries.

The action-first ex5 metabolism is preserved: consumption applies its true nutrition/toxicity; zero remaining satiety then costs10 health; finally satiety falls2, floored at zero and capped at100. Food never heals. Learning advances one round at a time and consumes normal hunger. Review, development and testing in real time never advance a round.

The [offline preflight](../scripts/coevolution-preflight.ts) records 72 finite food units, 1296 nutrition, 240 initial reserves and 1080 total metabolic demand. Every first food is one legal step away, with a first meal possible in three rounds. Its explicitly omniscient feasibility policy survives180 rounds with three actors at health100. It establishes a possible path, not model competence or causal benefit, and its routes are never ordinary context. Initial food does not regenerate; only later reviewed finite processes can produce additional stock. No rescue, automatic feeding or runtime parameter tuning is allowed.

## Real constraints and goals

Each carried resource unit or portable object adds one load. Moves cost `2 + ceil(max(0, load - 3 - carryAid) / 2)` energy; pickup costs `1 + ceil(units / 3)`, ability use2, and drop/give1. Learning, waiting and messages cost no energy. Insufficient energy rejects exertion. Explicit rest recovers4 plus the strongest available object's recovery bonus, capped at40. Ordinary waiting does not recover energy. Failed physical attempts still spend attempted effort when affordable; material transactions remain atomic.

Ordinary goals are health, supply reliability, less repetitive maintenance and better living conditions. They receive facts, interfaces, their observations and actual feedback, without named activity examples, occupations, destinations or required projects. No score rewards applications or descriptions. Movement/load/energy, recovery, material ownership, consumed nutrition, repeated actions and facility benefits make consequences observable. Different routes, lighter loads, transfers, tools or shared structures can change those consequences without the controller choosing a solution.

Every accepted decision includes five bounded project fields: problem, reason, progress, missing conditions and observed effects. This record persists until the actor revises it; failed calls retain the prior project. It is self-report, not proof of benefit. Twelve-round finite plans continue through harmless hunger decline. Failures, learning feedback, crossing satiety6/0 or health50/20, and a depleted upcoming target interrupt. Newly seen actors alone do not interrupt. Messages may be considered at the next natural decision; all actors still see the same round-start world.

## Extensible world effects

The new interpreter retains extraction, consumption, conversion and identification, and adds explicit intermediate-output references, material-costed object creation and delayed processing. Generated code is a bounded JSON operation composition, never host JavaScript. New type and blueprint declarations create no inventory by themselves. Use requires both a registered implementation and personal mastery. Shared structural signatures deduplicate renamed equivalent implementations; functional semantic equivalence remains the World Agent's responsibility.

Object blueprints declare portability, recovery/carrying effects and an optional finite input/output recipe. Construction consumes at least two material units; a stationary object persists at the builder's cell, a portable object has one owner. Recovery works for any adjacent actor, carrying assistance for its owner; strongest effects apply without stacking. A stationary processor spends recipe inputs immediately, allows one active job, and places outputs on shared ground after two to twelve rounds. Nutrition cannot exceed input energy, and output units cannot exceed consumed input units. Equal load per resource unit makes this a conservative material-conservation model; finer unit sizes remain outside this version. Others may use an appropriate learned ability or collect the completed output. One stationary object per cell and registry/object bounds keep state finite. Rest needs no extra skill. Drop, adjacent give and visible-recipient messages are minimal base extension interfaces, without trade institutions.

Ecology uses permanent seed/site/niche draws. Surface, canopy and substrate are three independent physical slots, so multiple resources coexist at a site. A slot records its first rule/type/draw even when zero; new names cannot reroll or refill it. Habitat, capacity and per-unit energy bounds apply. A reviewed process may replenish at most one unit per fixed interval up to its initial ceiling and a lifetime budget of at most24 additional units per slot. A zero draw never renews. These bounds prevent unlimited generation, while distribution and habitat plausibility still require actual world review.

The World Agent batches all applications at the barrier, reuses functional equivalents and may propose supporting resources, objects and processes. Reasonable requests outside the declarative envelope go to the existing current-conversation development handoff instead of being rejected solely because the operation is absent. The conversation inspects the frozen snapshot, implements the minimum effect and records its result. No recursive developer model service is launched.

The first real round-2 requests exposed that the initial handoff accepted declarations only. The authorized world-extension path now retains complete before/after engine sources in source-history.json, the unchanged allocation hash, a hash of the complete executed world/program prefix and exact call prefix, the barrier and the implementation review. Only five coevolution interpreter files may change through this path. Configuration, carrying/rest rules, metabolism, initial scenario, prompts, transport and scheduler remain frozen; a base change still requires a new version/run. Typecheck, lint, architecture, the complete unfiltered unit suite and build must pass before the extension is accepted. This establishes prefix compatibility, not a proof of all future behavior; final full browser/PR validation remains required. No live call is repeated and no budget is reset.

The requested photosynthesis-v1 extension adds optional, explicitly versioned stationary planting effects. Planting consumes matching resource input and at least two total material units, checks habitat, and occupies its cell permanently. Growth records its next maturity round, total produced units and cumulative environmental nutrition contribution. A fixed interval, ground-stock ceiling and lifetime production limit prevent unlimited output. Mature resources appear on the planting cell and anyone can collect them with existing pickup; generation alone is not consumption or realized nutritional benefit. New proposals do not reset existing objects. The actual berry/root timings and costs are retained in the development proposal, not seeded as an ordinary task or initial capability.

At round116, a new request for replacement construction material required nonfood biomass output. Source revision3 extends the same growth process to declared nonfood resources; the complete 116-round baseline is verified before resumption. The reviewed woody nursery consumes a viable cutting plus a support branch. Finite forest-tree canopy allocations use the fixed distribution `[0,1,2]`, including zero, without inspecting draws to choose a favorable rule. They coexist with old branch stock and never refill it. Produced units account for environmental biomass; the `environmentalEnergy` field measures edible nutrition only and is zero for branches, not a claim of zero physical growth energy. Cutting collection is implemented separately from each actor's mastery. This remains a coarse environmental model, not a botanical energy simulation.

## Budgets, checkpoints and observation windows

Total attempted-call limits are 120 ordinary and20 world, including rejected outputs, transport failures and retries. World requests share at most two attempts per batch. Ten persisted current-conversation development declaration attempts are allowed, including rejected declarations and retries. Source-validation failures and stale task submissions happen before that ledger write and are recorded separately in source logs/task journals; they are not covered by this cap. Internal model-call and token counts are unavailable and explicitly null, not zero. This intervention cap is only the measurable proxy for the user's development budget, not a claim to meter every model invocation. Ordinary/world attempts retain exact input, output, reason, start time, elapsed time and reported input/output tokens. Missing usage stays null. Output token usage includes nonvisible generated tokens when reported by the [Responses API](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/responses/methods/create); visible text length is not used as an estimate.

Persist an attempted call before network access and an accepted result before advancing time. Unknown interrupted outcomes block resumption without reissuing inference. Exhaustion freezes the entire world and retains pending programs/reviews; it never turns budget exhaustion into starvation waits. A single rejected ordinary response produces the disclosed one-round wait fallback while budget remains. Runtime code and configuration are source-bound, while reviewed versioned declarations are part of the retained simulation.

Automatic checkpoints and stage reports occur at rounds30,60,120,180. They retain the actual snapshot, project records, program/attempt/development prefix indices and measured states. The immutable execution ledger supplies the complete replayable prefixes. Round30 reports link health, projects and execution. At round60 only, stop if there has been no measured benefit and no actor with a project and at least two successful noninitial uses or learning-progress events during rounds31-60. This operational proxy may count ineffective learning as continued progress; the final analysis must distinguish it from benefit. Otherwise continue while budget permits. Stage inspection never sends guidance to actors.

A complete version-6 archive may describe an exhausted-budget pause or the preregistered early stop. Its manifest steps equal the observed window, while configuration and results retain target180. Archive completion establishes saved evidence and visual capture, not180-round success. No selective rerun is permitted.
